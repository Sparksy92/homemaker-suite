# 1.1 The Homemaker Pantry: Your Store

## Concept: The Working Pantry
A "working pantry" is different from a "bunker." In a bunker, you hide food away and hope you never need it. In a working pantry, you shop from your shelves daily and restock from the store weekly.
*   **Goal:** Have 2-4 weeks of food on hand at all times for normal daily eating.
*   **Result:** You are immune to short-term supply chain hiccups, storms, or sick weeks where you can't shop.

## The 3-Deep Rule
For every core item you use (e.g., peanut butter, rice, coffee), aim to have:
1.  **One Now:** The open container you are currently using.
2.  **One Next:** The backup container on the shelf.
3.  **One Reserve:** The deep backup (optional, but good for critical items).

## Categories of the Healthy Pantry

### 1. Dry Grains & Starches (The Base)
*   *Long Life (1-2 years+):* White Rice, Pasta (various shapes), Oats (Rolled/Steel Cut), Popcorn, Dried Corn.
*   *Medium Life (6-12 months):* Brown Rice, Quinoa, Barley, Flour (White/Whole Wheat).
*   *Storage:* Air-tight containers are mandatory to prevent weevils and moisture.

### 2. Canned Goods (The Convenience)
*   *Proteins:* Tuna, Salmon, Sardines, Canned Chicken, Beans (Black, Kidney, Garbanzo, Pinto).
*   *Vegetables:* Tomatoes (Diced/Paste/Sauce), Corn, Green Beans, Peas.
*   *Fruits:* Peaches, Pears, Pineapple (in juice, not syrup).
*   *Other:* Coconut Milk, Stocks/Broths.

### 3. Fats & Oils (The Fuel)
*   *Cooking:* Olive Oil (low heat), Avocado/Vegetable Oil (high heat), Coconut Oil (baking/stable), Ghee, Lard.
*   *Storage:* Cool, dark place. Heat and light make oils rancid.

### 4. Flavor Agents (The Soul)
*   *Vinegars:* Apple Cider, White, Balsamic, Rice.
*   *Sauces:* Soy Sauce/Tamari, Hot Sauce, Worcestershire, Mustard, Ketchup.
*   *Sweeteners:* Honey (never spoils), Maple Syrup, Molasses, Sugar (White/Brown).

### 5. Baking Essentials
*   Baking Soda, Baking Powder (check expiry yearly), Cornstarch, Cocoa Powder, Vanilla Extract, Chocolate Chips.

## The Rotation System (FIFO)
**FIFO = First In, First Out.**
*   When you buy a new can of beans, put it *behind* the old can of beans.
*   Always reach for the front item.
*   *Marking:* If dates are hard to read, write the purchase month/year (e.g., "10/25") on the lid with a sharpie.

## Inventory Management
*   Do not guess.
*   Use the `11 Printables/PantryInventory.md` sheet.
*   Update it simply: When you take the *last* back-up item, circle it on the list or add it to the grocery list immediately.
