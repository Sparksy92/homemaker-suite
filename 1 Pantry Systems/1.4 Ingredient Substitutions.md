# 1.4 Ingredient Substitutions: The Alchemist's Guide

## Philosophy
Cooking is chemistry, but it is forgiving chemistry. If you understand the *function* of an ingredient (acid, fat, binder, leavener), you can usually replace it.

*Note: For a digital tool, see `10 Tools & Wizards/SubstitutionEngine.json`.*

## Dairy Substitutions
*   **Buttermilk (1 cup)**
    *   *Sub:* 1 cup milk + 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice (let sit 5 mins).
    *   *Sub:* 1 cup plain yogurt thinned with water.
*   **Heavy Cream (1 cup)**
    *   *Sub:* 3/4 cup milk + 1/3 cup melted butter (for cooking, not whipping).
    *   *Sub:* 1 cup Coconut cream (for dairy free).
*   **Sour Cream**
    *   *Sub:* Plain Greek yogurt (1:1 ratio).
    *   *Sub:* Crème fraîche.
*   **Butter (Baking)**
    *   *Sub:* Coconut oil (1:1, solid).
    *   *Sub:* Lard (1:1).
    *   *Sub:* Applesauce (half the amount, for healthier muffins/cakes).

## Baking Essentials
*   **Baking Powder (1 tsp)**
    *   *Sub:* 1/4 tsp Baking Soda + 1/2 tsp Cream of Tartar.
*   **Brown Sugar**
    *   *Sub:* 1 cup White Sugar + 1 tbsp Molasses.
*   **Cake Flour (1 cup)**
    *   *Sub:* 1 cup All-Purpose Flour minus 2 tbsp, replace with 2 tbsp Cornstarch.
*   **Egg (Binder)**
    *   *Sub:* 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp warm water (let sit 5 mins).
    *   *Sub:* 1/4 cup applesauce or mashed banana.

## Savory & Pantry
*   **Breadcrumbs**
    *   *Sub:* Crushed crackers, oats (pulsed in blender), crushed cornflakes.
*   **Tomato Sauce**
    *   *Sub:* Tomato paste + water (1:1 ratio), season with salt/sugar.
*   **Wine (Cooking)**
    *   *Red Sub:* Beef broth + splash of red wine vinegar / Grape juice (unsweetened).
    *   *White Sub:* Chicken broth + splash of white vinegar / Apple juice.
*   **Lemon Juice**
    *   *Sub:* White vinegar or Apple Cider Vinegar (1/2 strength).
*   **Garlic Clove**
    *   *Sub:* 1/8 tsp Garlic Powder.

## Herb & Spice Swaps
*   **Fresh vs. Dry Rule:** 1 tbsp Fresh = 1 tsp Dry. (Dry is 3x more potent).
*   **Cumin:** Coriander or Caraway (different flavor but similar earthiness).
*   **Cinnamon:** Nutmeg or Allspice (use 1/2 amount).
*   **Oregano:** Thyme or Basil.

## Thickeners
*   **Cornstarch (1 tbsp)**
    *   *Sub:* 2 tbsp All-Purpose Flour.
    *   *Sub:* 1 tbsp Potato Starch or Arrowroot.
*   **Roux:** Fat + Flour cooked together.
