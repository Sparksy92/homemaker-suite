# 1.5 Shelf Life Guide: When Good Food Goes Bad

## Understanding Dates
*   **"Best By" / "Best Before":** Quality guarantee. The food is safe after this date, but might lose flavor or texture.
*   **"Use By" / "Expiration":** Safety recommendation (especially for baby formula and meats). Adhere to this more strictly.
*   **"Sell By":** For the retailer. You usually have days/weeks after this date.

## The Pantry (Room Temp)
*Assumes cool, dark, dry storage.*

| Item | Unopened | Opened | Signs of Spoilage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **White Rice** | Indefinite | 1-2 Years | Bugs, moisture, smell |
| **Brown Rice** | 6 Months | 3-6 Months | Oily smell (rancid) |
| **Dried Beans** | 2-3 Years | 1 Year | Bugs, mold |
| **Canned Goods** | 2-5 Years | N/A | Bulging, leaking, spurting |
| **Pasta (Dry)** | 2 Years | 1 Year | Pantry moths |
| **Flour (White)** | 1 Year | 6-8 Months | Musty smell, weevils |
| **Flour (Whole)** | 3-6 Months | 1-3 Months | Rancid/metallic smell |
| **Sugar/Honey** | Indefinite | Indefinite | Clumping (still safe) |
| **Oils** | 1 Year | 3-6 Months | Rancid/crayon smell |
| **Spices** | 2-3 Years | 1 Year | Loss of scent/color |
| **Vinegar** | Indefinite | Indefinite | Cloudy "mother" (safe) |

## The Refrigerator (35°F - 40°F)
*   **Eggs:** 3-5 weeks.
*   **Milk:** 1 week past Sell-By. (Smell test is reliable).
*   **Butter:** 1-3 months.
*   **Yogurt:** 1-2 weeks past date.
*   **Cheese (Hard):** 3-6 months (cut mold off).
*   **Cheese (Soft):** 1 week opened.
*   **Leftovers:** 3-4 days MAX.
*   **Raw Chicken/Ground Meat:** 1-2 days.
*   **Raw Steaks/Chops:** 3-5 days.
*   **Condiments:**
    *   *Ketchup/Mustard:* 6-12 months.
    *   *Mayo:* 2 months opened.
    *   *Soy Sauce:* Indefinite.

## The Freezer (0°F)
*Safety is indefinite; dates are for quality (freezer burn).*
*   **Ground Meat:** 3-4 months.
*   **Whole Meats:** 6-12 months.
*   **Bread:** 3-6 months.
*   **Vegetables (Blanched):** 8-12 months.
*   **Ice Cream:** 2-4 months.

## Extending Shelf Life
1.  **Airtight is Right:** Oxygen oxidizes fats and stales grains. Use jars/tupperware.
2.  **Keep it Cool:** Every 10°F drop doubles shelf life for many items.
3.  **Label Everything:** You will not remember when you opened that salsa. Write the date.
