# 9.4 Winter: Rest (December - February)

## The Vibe
Darkness, Silence, Planning, Repair.

## The Pantry
*   **Action:** Eating from the Larder. Using the "Deep" preps.
*   **Cooking:** Heavy calorie meals. Baking bread. Citrus (in season in south).

## The Garden
*   **Task:** DREAMING. Order seed catalogs. Plan the layout. Repair tools.
*   **Indoor:** Sprouts / Microgreens on windowsill.

## The Home
*   **Hygge:** Candlelight, warm drinks, reading.
*   **Maintenance:** Check pipes for freezing. Shovel snow.

## Rituals
*   **Yule/Christmas:** Bringing green inside (Tree). Light in darkness.
*   **Inventory:** Checking what we have, what we used, what we need for next year.
