# 9.2 Summer: Abundance (June - August)

## The Vibe
Heat, Long Days, Overwhelm, Preservation.

## The Pantry
*   **Action:** CANNING. This is the busy season.
*   **Cooking:** Move heat outside (Grill). Salads, cold soups. Avoid oven.

## The Garden
*   **Harvest:** Zucchini, Beans, Tomatoes, Cucumbers.
*   **Task:** Water deeply. Mulch heavily to retain moisture. Weed early.

## The Home
*   **Cooling:** Close blinds during day. Open windows at night.
*   **Pests:** Watch for ants and flies. Manage trash strictly.

## Rituals
*   **Solstice:** Longest day. Bonfires.
*   **The Glut:** Giving away zucchini to neighbors.
