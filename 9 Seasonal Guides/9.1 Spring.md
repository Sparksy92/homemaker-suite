# 9.1 Spring: Awakening (March - May)

## The Vibe
Mud, Hope, Green shoots, Fast growth.

## The Pantry
*   **Action:** Eat down the freezer. Empty the jars. Make room for new harvest.
*   **Forage:** Dandelion greens, Nettles, Morels, Ramps.

## The Garden
*   **Indoor:** Start tomatoes, peppers.
*   **Outdoor:** Plant peas, spinach, lettuce, radishes (cool weather crops).
*   **Prep:** Top dress beds with compost.

## The Home
*   **Deep Clean:** Open windows to air out winter stagnation.
*   **Textiles:** Wash heavy blankets and store. Bring out lighter linens.
*   **Maintenance:** Check gutters (April showers). Check roof for ice damage.

## Rituals
*   **First Salad:** Eating the first fresh greens works as a tonic after a winter of root veg.
*   **Easter/Equinox:** Celebrating the return of light and eggs.
