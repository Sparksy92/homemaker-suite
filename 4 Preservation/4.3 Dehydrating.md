# 4.3 Dehydrating: Removing the Water

## Basics
*   **Mechanism:** Warm, moving air removes moisture, stopping bacteria and mold growth.
*   **Pros:** Reduces weight/volume by 80%. Great for snacking and soups.
*   **Cons:** Texture change.

## Equipment
*   **Electric Dehydrator:** Best consistent results (Excalibur, Nesco).
*   **Oven:** Possible if it goes low enough (<170°F). Prop door open.
*   **Air:** Hang drying (Herbs only).

## Temps
*   **Herbs:** 95°F
*   **Veg:** 125°F
*   **Fruit:** 135°F
*   **Meat (Jerky):** 160°F

## Master Recipe: Beef Jerky
1.  **Meat:** Lean round roast. Freeze partially to slice thin (against grain).
2.  **Marinade:** Soy sauce, worstershire, liquid smoke, garlic powder, black pepper. Soak 12-24 hours.
3.  **Dry:** 160°F for 4-6 hours until it bends and cracks but doesn't snap.
4.  **Store:** Fridge (safest) or vacuum seal.

## Master Recipe: Dried Apples
1.  **Prep:** Peel, core, slice into rings.
2.  **Dip:** Soak in lemon water (prevents browning).
3.  **Dry:** 135°F for 6-10 hours until leathery.

## Conditioning
After drying fruit, put in a jar and shake daily for 1 week. If condensation appears, put back in dehydrator. This balances moisture and prevents mold.
