# 4.4 Fermentation: Controlled Decay

## Basics
*   **Mechanism:** Lactobacillus bacteria eat sugars and produce lactic acid.
*   **Result:** Acid preserves the food and creates probiotics.
*   **Safety:** Salt concentration prevents bad bacteria; acid prevents botulism.

## The Formula: 2-3% Brine
*   **Ratio:** 1 Tablespoon of Kosher Salt per 2 Cups of Chlorine-free Water.
*   (Or weigh it: Weight of Veg + Water * 0.02 = Weight of Salt).

## Equipment
*   Mason Jar.
*   **Weight:** Glass weight, clean rock, or ziploc bag with water. essential to keep veg *under* the brine.
*   **Airlock:** Optional lid vent to let gas escape (burp jar daily if not used).

## Master Recipe: Sauerkraut
1.  **Prep:** Shred cabbage. Weigh it.
2.  **Salt:** Add 2% salt by weight (approx 1.5 tbsp per head).
3.  **Massage:** Squeeze cabbage with hands until it releases liquid (its own brine).
4.  **Pack:** Stuff into jar tightly. Ensure liquid covers cabbage. Weigh down.
5.  **Wait:** Store at room temp (65-70°F) for 1-4 weeks. Taste weekly.
6.  **Done:** When it tastes sour enough, put in fridge.

## Kahm Yeast
White, film-like layer on top is usually harmless Kahm Yeast. Skim it off.
**Mold:** Fuzzy/Green/Black/Pink spots = Toss the batch.

## Garlic Honey
*   **Method:** Fill jar 3/4 with peeled garlic cloves. Cover completely with raw honey.
*   **Result:** Ferments into a runny, potent immune booster.
*   **Wait:** Burp daily. Ready in 1 month.
