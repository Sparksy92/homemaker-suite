# 7.1 The Envelope System: Cash is Truth

## The Concept
Digital money feels unreal. Swiping a card doesn't register as "loss" in the brain. Cash does.
*   **Rule:** For variable expenses (Grocery, Gas, Fun), use cash.
*   **Constraint:** When the envelope is empty, you stop spending.

## Setup
1.  **Budget:** Determine monthly amounts via `BudgetCalculator.json`.
2.  **Withdraw:** Take out cash for the pay period.
3.  **Divide:** Put into labeled envelopes.
    *   *Groceries*
    *   *Eating Out*
    *   *Fuel*
    *   *Personal/Fun*

## The Psychology
*   You will naturally buy cheaper items to "save" the cash.
*   You cannot overdraw.
*   Leftover cash rolls over to next month (Snowball effect).

## The Digital Equivalent
If you must use cards, use a "Zero-Based Budget" app (YNAB) or a spreadsheet where you manually enter every transaction.
*   **The Audit:** Once a month, print your bank statement. Highlight every "stupid" purchase (convenience store, subscription you forgot). Sum it up. Feel the pain. Change behavior.

## The Emergency Fund
*   **Baby Step:** $1,000 cash in the house (small fires).
*   **Goal:** 3-6 months of expenses (job loss).
*   **Location:** High Yield Savings Account (Liquid but separate).
