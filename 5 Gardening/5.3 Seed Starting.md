# 5.3 Seed Starting: The Nursery

## Why Start Seeds?
*   **Cost:** $3 packet = 50 plants. $5 nursery plant = 1 plant.
*   **Variety:** Access to heirlooms not sold in stores.

## Timing
Read the packet. "Start indoors 6-8 weeks before last frost."

## The Needs
1.  **Light:** A windowsill is usually *not* enough (seedlings get "leggy"/tall and weak). Use generic LED shop lights 2 inches above the plants.
2.  **Heat:** Peppers/Tomatoes germinate best at 75-80°F. Use a heat mat or top of fridge.
3.  **Mix:** Sterile seed starting mix. Do not use heavy garden soil (too dense/diseased).

## Hardening Off (CRITICAL STEP)
You cannot take a baby plant from a warm, still house to a windy, cold garden instantly. It will die of shock.
*   **Day 1:** 1 hour outdoors (shade).
*   **Day 2:** 2 hours.
*   **Day 3:** Morning sun.
*   **Day 4-7:** Increase sun/wind exposure.
*   **Day 8:** Plant.

## Direct Sow
Some plants hate transplanting. Plant these directly in the ground:
*   Carrots / Parsnips (Taproots).
*   Beans / Peas.
*   Corn.
*   Squash / Cukes (fast growers).
