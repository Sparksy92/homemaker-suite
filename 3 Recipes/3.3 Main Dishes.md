# 3.3 Main Dishes: Protein-Centric Meals

## The Philosophy
Meat is expensive. We treat it as a highlight, not always the solo act. Use these methods to stretch protein.

## The Roast Chicken (The Weekly Staple)
*Yields: 1 Dinner + 1 Lunch + Stock Bones.*
1.  **Prep:** Dry bird with paper towels. Salting generously inside and out.
2.  **Pan:** Place in cast iron skillet or roasting pan. Bed of carrots/onions optional.
3.  **Cook:** 425°F for 60-80 minutes. Thermometer in thigh reads 165°F.
4.  **Rest:** 15 minutes.

## Braised Pork Shoulder (Carnitas Style)
*The cheapest cut of meat transformed.*
1.  **Prep:** Cut 3-4 lb Pork Butt into large chunks. Season heavily (Salt, Cumin, Oregano).
2.  **Pot:** Dutch oven. Add pork, 1 onion (quartered), 1 orange (halved), 1/2 cup water.
3.  **Cook:** Oven 300°F for 3 hours (covered).
4.  **Crisp:** Uncover. Increase heat to 400°F for 20 mins to crisp the fat. Shred.

## Meatloaf / Meatballs (The Extender)
*How to make 1lb of beef feed 6 people.*
*   **Ratio:** 1 lb Ground Meat + 1 cup Breadcrumbs (or Oats) + 1 Egg + 1/2 cup Milk (or Water/Broth/Oat Milk).
*   **Flavor:** 1 Onion (minced/cooked), Garlic, Worcesterhire, Ketchup.
*   **Cook:**
    *   *Loaf:* 350°F for 60 mins.
    *   *Balls:* 400°F for 20 mins.

## Shepherd's Pie
*The ultimate leftover repurposer.*
*   **Bottom Layer:** Leftover Roast/Stew meat (minced) OR Ground Beef cooked with onions/peas/carrots in gravy.
*   **Top Layer:** Mashed Potatoes (seal to the edges). *Note: Mash with broth and oil/lard instead of milk/butter for lactose-free.*
*   **Bake:** 400°F until gravy bubbles up masterfully.
