# 3.6 Special Considerations: Kid-Friendly & Texture-Adapted Foods

**Context:** Stress kills appetite. Kids revert to "safe foods". Elderly may have swallowing issues (dysphagia) or dental pain.

---

## 1. Kid-Friendly Survival ("The Yuck Factor")
**Goal:** Prevent starvation due to refusal.
**Rule:** Do not introduce "weird" foods during day 1-3 of a crisis.

### 1.1 The "Trojan Horse" Strategy
*   **Powder Power:**
    *   *Action:* Mix powdered milk/eggs/greens into things they *do* like (Pancakes, Mac & Cheese).
    *   *Ratio:* 1 Tbsp hidden powder per cup of "normal" food. Detectability = Zero.
*   **Sweetness:**
    *   *Carbs:* Rice/Oats are bland. Add cinnamon/sugar/raisins heavily.
    *   *Comfort:* Hot Cocoa is a caloric vehicle. Add butter/coconut oil (hidden fats) to it.

### 1.2 Interactive Cooking
*   **Control:** Kids eat what they make.
*   **Job:** "You are the dough smasher." (Bannock bread).
*   **Job:** "You are the bean counter." (Sorting dry beans).

### 1.3 Snack Rationing
*   **The "Currency":** High-value snacks (Gummies, Chocolate) become money.
*   **Use:** Reward for finishing a "boring" meal. DO NOT allow grazing on snacks all day.

---

## 2. Texture Adaptation (Elderly / Dysphagia)
**Challenge:** Dry survival food (Crackers, jerky, hardtack) is un-eatable for those with dentures or swallowing issues.

### 2.1 The "Soak & Slurry" Method
*   **Hardtack/Crackers:** Soak in broth/water for 20 mins.
    *   *Result:* Musth consistency. Savory pudding.
*   **Jerky:** Boil it. Do not chew it dry.
    *   *Soup:* Cut into tiny bits. simmer 1 hour. It rehydrates to soft meat.

### 2.2 Dysphagia (Choking Risk)
*   **Thickening:** Water causes coughing/aspirating.
    *   *Thickener:* Cornstarch, Potato Flakes, or Commercial Thickener (Thick-It).
    *   *Consistency:* Aim for "Nectar" (runs slow) or "Honey" (drips slow).
*   **Posture:** Sit upright 90 degrees. Tuck chin to chest when swallowing.

### 2.3 Flavor Enhancement (The "Taste" Loss)
*   **Problem:** Aging reduces taste buds. "Bland" food = No appetite.
*   **Solution:** Over-season (safely).
    *   *Acids:* Lemon juice, vinegar (Brightens flavor without salt).
    *   *Spices:* Cinnamon, Nutmeg, Garlic. Smells trigger hunger.

---

## 3. Recipes for the Vulnerable

### "The Power Porridge" (All Ages)
*   **Base:** 1 cup Oats + 2 cups Water/Milk.
*   **Hidden:** 1 Tbsp Butter (Fat), 1 Tbsp Peanut Butter (Protein), 1 tsp Sugar.
*   **Texture:** Cook longer for mush (Elderly). Less water for chewy (Kids).
*   **Why:** Comforting, high calorie, easy to swallow.

### "Rice Pudding Rescue"
*   **Base:** Leftover plain rice.
*   **Add:** Rehydrated milk, raisins, cinnamon, sugar.
*   **Why:** Turns a "boring side" into a "treat". Good for picky eaters.
