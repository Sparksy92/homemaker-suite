# 3.5 Sauces & Condiments: The Flavor Upgrade

## The Philosophy
A good sauce saves a bad meal. These staples are cheaper and healthier than store-bought.

## Vinaigrette (The 3:1 Rule)
*   **Ratio:** 3 parts Oil : 1 part Acid.
*   **Basic:** 3 tbsp Olive Oil + 1 tbsp Lemon Juice + Salt + Pepper.
*   **Mustard Vinaigrette:** Add 1 tsp Dijon (emulsifier - keeps it creamy) + Minced Shallot.

## Béchamel (The White Sauce Base)
*   **Roux:** Melt 2 tbsp Butter. Whisk in 2 tbsp Flour. Cook 1 min (don't brown).
*   **Liquid:** Slowly whisk in 1.5 cups Milk. Simmer until thickened.
*   **Transformations:**
    *   *Cheese Sauce (Mornay):* Add 1 cup Cheddar/Gruyere. (Mac & Cheese base).
    *   *Cream Gravy:* Use sausage fat instead of butter. Add lots of pepper.

## Tomato Sauce (Marinara)
*   **Base:** Sauté 1/2 Onion + 2 cloves Garlic in olive oil.
*   **Tomato:** Add 1 can (28oz) Crushed Tomatoes.
*   **Simmer:** 20 mins.
*   **Season:** Salt, pinch of sugar (balances acid), dried Oregano, fresh Basil.

## Pan Sauce (For Seared Meat)
*   *After cooking steak/chicken/pork chops in stainless steel pan:*
    1.  Pour off excess fat. leave brown bits.
    2.  **Deglaze:** Add 1/2 cup wine or broth. Scrape up bits.
    3.  **Reduce:** Boil until liquid is half gone.
    4.  **Mount:** Whisk in 1 tbsp cold butter at the end for gloss.
