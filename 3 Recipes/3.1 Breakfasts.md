# 3.1 Breakfasts: Fuel for the Day

## The Philosophy
Breakfast on the homestead needs to be high-energy and low-effort. We rely on "Master Batches" prepared ahead of time.

## Master Recipe: The Universal Frittata
*Serves 4-6 | Time: 20 mins*
*   **Base:** 8 Eggs + 1/2 cup Milk/Cream (or Water/Oat Milk) + 1/2 tsp Salt.
*   **Fats:** 2 tbsp Butter, Bacon Fat, or Oil.
*   **Fillings (Choose 2 cups total):**
    *   *Veg:* Spinach, Peppers, Onions, Potatoes (pre-cooked), Tomatoes.
    *   *Meat:* Ham, Bacon, Sausage.
    *   *Cheese:* Cheddar, Feta, Goat Cheese.
*   **Method:**
    1.  Preheat broiler.
    2.  Sauté fillings in fat in a 10" cast iron skillet until tender (Medium heat).
    3.  Whisk base ingredients. Pour over fillings.
    4.  Cook on stovetop undisturbed for 5-7 mins until edges set.
    5.  Transfer to broiler for 3-5 mins until top is golden and puffed.

## Master Recipe: Overnight Oats
*Serves 1 | Time: 5 mins prep (Night before)*
*   **Ratio:** 1:1 (Oats to Liquid).
*   **Ingredients:** 1/2 cup Rolled Oats + 1/2 cup Milk/Water/Yogurt/Oat Milk.
*   **Flavor:** Cinnamon, Honey, Chia Seeds, Dried Fruit.
*   **Method:** Combine in a jar. Shake. Refrigerate overnight. Eat cold or warm up.

## Master Recipe: Homestead Pancakes
*Serves 4 | Time: 15 mins*
*   **Dry:** 1.5 cups Flour, 3.5 tsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Salt, 1 tbsp Sugar.
*   **Wet:** 1.25 cups Milk (or Water/Oat Milk), 1 Egg, 3 tbsp Melted Butter/Oil/Lard.
*   **Method:**
    1.  Mix dry. Mix wet.
    2.  Combine (DO NOT OVERMIX; lumps are good).
    3.  Cook on hot greased griddle 2-3 mins per side.

## Master Recipe: Scrambled Eggs (The Right Way)
*   **Secret:** Low heat and patience.
*   **Method:** Whisk eggs with salt (add splash of water instead of milk). Melt butter/oil in pan on LOW heat. Pour eggs. Stir constantly with spatula. Pull off heat when they look *slightly* wet (they finish cooking on the plate).
