# 3.4 Breads & Grains: The Staff of Life

## The Philosophy
Bread requires time, not effort. "No Knead" methods let yeast do the work for you.

## Master Recipe: No-Knead Dutch Oven Bread
*   **Ingreds:** 3 cups Flour, 1.5 tsp Salt, 0.5 tsp Yeast, 1.5 cups Water (room temp).
*   **Step 1:** Mix in bowl until shaggy. Cover. Let sit on counter 12-18 hours.
*   **Step 2:** Oven 450°F. Put empty Dutch Oven inside to heat up.
*   **Step 3:** Scrape dough onto parchment. Drop into hot pot. Cover.
*   **Step 4:** Bake 30 mins covered. Remove lid. Bake 15 mins uncovered.

## Master Recipe: Sandwich Loaf (Soft White)
*   **Ingreds:** 3.5 cups Flour, 1 cup Milk/Water/Oat Milk (warm), 2 tbsp Butter/Oil/Lard (melted), 2 tbsp Sugar, 2 tsp Yeast, 1 tsp Salt.
*   **Method:**
    1.  Knead 10 mins (hand or mixer).
    2.  Rise 1 hour (doubled).
    3.  Shape into cylinder. Place in greased loaf pan.
    4.  Rise 45 mins.
    5.  Bake 350°F for 30-35 mins.

## Perfectly Cooked Rice
*   **Absorption Method (The standard):**
    *   Rinse rice until water runs clear (removes starch/gumminess).
    *   Ratio: 1 cup Rice : 1.5 cups Water.
    *   Bring to boil. Cover. Reduce to LOWEST simmer.
    *   *White:* 18 mins. *Brown:* 45 mins.
    *   **Crucial:** Remove from heat. Keep covered 10 mins. Fluff with fork.

## Biscuits (The Fast Bread)
*   **Cold is King:** Keep butter and milk cold.
*   **Ingreds:** 2 cups Flour, 1 tbsp Baking Powder, 1 tsp Salt, 1 stick Butter/Lard (frozen/grated), 3/4 cup Milk/Water.
*   **Method:** Cut butter into flour. Stir in milk. Pat out dough. Fold over 3 times (layers). Cut circles.
*   **Bake:** 425°F for 12-15 mins.
