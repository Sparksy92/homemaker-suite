# Distillation: Concentrating Spirits

*WARNING: Distilling alcohol is illegal in most countries without a license. Methanol (bad alcohol) can blind you.*

## The Physics
*   **Water Boils**: 212°F (100°C)
*   **Ethanol (Good Alcohol) Boils**: 173°F (78°C)
*   **Methanol (Blindness) Boils**: 148°F (64°C)

## The Process
If you heat a "Mash" (fermented beer/wine) to **175°F**:
1.  The Alcohol boils into steam.
2.  The Water stays liquid.
3.  The steam travels up a tube, cools down (condenser), and drips out as strong liquor.

## The Cuts (Safety)
1.  **Foreshots (POISON)**: The first part that comes out (low boiling point Methanol). **THROW AWAY THE FIRST 100ml PER 5 GALLONS**. Use for solvent/fire starter.
2.  **Heads**: Solventy smell. Save to redistill.
3.  **Hearts**: Sweet, clean smell. This is the drinking/medicinal alcohol.
4.  **Tails**: Wet dog smell. Low alcohol. Stop collecting.
