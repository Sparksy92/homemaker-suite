# Brewing: Liquid Bread

Beer provided safe hydration and calories for civilization.

## Malting (The Magic Step)
Grain (Barley/Wheat) is starch. Yeast cannot eat starch; it needs sugar.
1.  **Steep**: Soak grain in water until it sprouts (1/4 inch root).
2.  **Enzymes**: Sprouting unlocks enzymes (Amylase) that convert starch -> sugar.
3.  **Halt**: Dry/Roast the sprouted grain ("Malt") to stop it growing.

## Mashing
1.  **Mash**: Soak ground Malt in hot water (150°F) for 1 hour. The enzymes turn the water sweet ("Wort").
2.  **Boil**: Boil the Wort with Hops (preservative/flavor) for 1 hour.
3.  **Cool**: Cool rapidly.

## Fermenting
1.  **Pitch**: Add Yeast.
2.  **Wait**: 2 weeks. Yeast eats sugar, pees alcohol and farts CO2.
3.  **Bottle**: Add a tiny bit of sugar to bottle for carbonation.
