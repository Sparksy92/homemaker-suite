# 34.3 Distillation: Fuel and Medicine

**Warning**: Distilling alcohol is illegal in many jurisdictions without a permit. *However*, in a grid-down scenario, high-proof alcohol (Ethanol) is the only renewable fuel for sterilization, tinctures, and engines.

## 1. The Theory
Alcohol boils at **173°F (78°C)**. Water boils at **212°F (100°C)**.
If you hold a mash at 175°F, the alcohol turns to steam, but the water stays liquid. You capture the steam, cool it back into liquid, and you have concentrated alcohol.

## 2. The Pot Still Hardware
*   **Boiler**: A sealed pressure cooker or stainless keg.
*   **Column/Riser**: Copper pipe rising from the boiler (clean steam).
*   **Condenser (Worm)**: A coil of copper pipe submerged in a bucket of cold water.
*   **Collection Jar**: Where the drip comes out.

## 3. The Cuts (Critical Safety)
Methanol (Wood Alcohol) causes blindness and death. It boils FIRST. You typically discard the first output.
1.  **Foreshots (Methanol)**: The first 50-100ml per 5 gallons. **THROW THIS AWAY OR USE AS SOLVENT ONLY. POISON.**
2.  **Heads**: High proof, solvent smell. Good for fuel/cleaning.
3.  **Hearts (Ethanol)**: The sweet spot. Clean smell. This is the medicine/drinking grade (140-160 proof).
4.  **Tails**: Low alcohol, smells like wet cardboard. Stop collecting here.

## 4. Uses
*   **70% (140 Proof)**: Perfect disinfectant.
*   **95% (190 Proof)**: Fuel for alcohol stoves or converted engines.
*   **Tinctures**: Soaking medicinal herbs (Willow Bark, Valerian) in alcohol extracts the active compounds far better than water tea.
