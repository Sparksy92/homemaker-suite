# Fermentation: Controlled Decay

Fermentation is the oldest food preservation method. It encourages "good" bacteria (Lactobacillus) to create acid, which kills "bad" bacteria (Botulism/E. Coli).

## 1. Safety Rules
*   **Submerge**: Everything must be under the brine. "Under the brine, everything's fine."
*   **Salt**: Salt regulates the fermentation speed.
*   **Temperature**: 65-75°F is ideal.

## 2. Sauerkraut (The First Ferment)
*   **Ingredients**: 1 head Cabbage, 1.5 tbsp Salt.
*   **Process**:
    1.  Shred cabbage finely.
    2.  Sprinkle salt. Massage with hands for 10 minutes until it releases water (its own brine).
    3.  Pack into a jar tightly.
    4.  Weight it down (glass weight or ziploc bag of water).
    5.  Wait 3-6 weeks.

## 3. Kombucha (The Fermented Tea)
*   **SCOBY**: Symbiotic Culture Of Bacteria and Yeast. (The rubbery puck).
*   **Recipe**: Sweet Black Tea + SCOBY.
*   **Process**:
    1.  Brew sweet tea. Cool to room temp (Hot tea kills SCOBY).
    2.  Add SCOBY and some starter liquid.
    3.  Cover with cloth (it needs to breathe).
    4.  Wait 7-14 days. Tastes like cider vinegar? It's done.

## 4. Ginger Bug (Wild Soda)
*   **Start**: Fresh grated ginger + sugar + water in a jar.
*   **Feed**: Add 1 tsp sugar + 1 tsp ginger daily.
*   **Result**: In 5 days it will bubble. Use this liquid to carbonate homemade fruit juice sodas.
