# 3.2 Soups & Stews: The Economy of Flavor

## The Philosophy
Soup is the ultimate "zero waste" vehicle. Any vegetable, any meat scrap, and any grain can become soup. It creates volume from varied ingredients.

## Master Stock (The Golden Liquid)
*Never buy boxed broth again.*
*   **Ingredients:** Chicken carcasses (bones/skin), Onion skins, Carrot ends, Celery tops, Peppercorns, Bay Leaf.
*   **Method:**
    1.  Collect scraps in a freezer bag. When full, dump in pot.
    2.  Cover with water.
    3.  Simmer 4-12 hours (Stovetop) or 24 hours (Slow Cooker).
    4.  Strain and freeze.

## Master Recipe: "Clean the Fridge" Vegetable Soup
*   **The Trinity:** Onion, Carrot, Celery (diced). Sauté in oil.
*   **The Liquid:** 6 cups Master Stock + 1 can Diced Tomatoes.
*   **The Bulk:** Potatoes, Green Beans, Corn, Zucchini (add in order of hardness).
*   **The Finish:** Salt, Pepper, 1 tbsp Vinegar (vital!), Fresh Herbs.

## Master Recipe: Beef & Barley Stew
*Hearty, cheap, and freezes perfectly.*
*   **Meat:** 1 lb Chuck Roast (cubed). Coat in flour. Sear hard in pot. Remove.
*   **Veg:** Onions, Carrots, Potatoes. Sauté in beef fat.
*   **Liquid:** 4 cups Beef Stock + 1 cup Red Wine (or Stout Beer).
*   **Grain:** 1/2 cup Pearled Barley.
*   **Cook:** Return meat. Simmer covered low for 2 hours until beef falls apart.

## Master Recipe: Potato Leek Soup (The Creamy Base)
*   **Base:** 4 Leeks (cleaned/sliced) + 2 tbsp Butter. Sweat until soft.
*   **Body:** 2 lbs Potatoes (peeled/cubed).
*   **Liquid:** 6 cups Chicken broth.
*   **Cook:** Simmer 20 mins.
*   **Blend:** Use immersion blender until smooth. stir in 1/2 cup cream (optional).
