# Cast Iron Cooking

Cast iron is the workhorse of the homestead kitchen. Indestructible, non-stick (if treated right), and perfect for open fire or stove.

## Why Cast Iron?
*   **Heat Retention**: stays hot, sears meat perfectly.
*   **Iron Enrichment**: adds small amounts of dietary iron to food.
*   **Versatility**: stovetop to oven to campfire.

## Seasoning
Seasoning is polymerized oil. It's not dirt; it's a protective layer.
1.  **Clean**: Scrub rust with steel wool (only time you use steel wool!).
2.  **Oil**: Apply a *very* thin layer of flaxseed, grapeseed, or Crisco.
3.  **Bake**: Upside down in oven at 450°F-500°F for 1 hour.
4.  **Repeat**: 3-4 times for a bulletproof finish.

## Daily Care
*   **Cleaning**: Hot water and a stiff brush. **Soap is okay!** (Modern soap won't strip polymer).
*   **Drying**: Dry immediately and thoroughly. Put it on the stove for a minute to evaporate moisture.
*   **Oiling**: Wipe with a tiny drop of oil before storing.

## Cooking Tips
*   **Preheat**: Iron heats slowly but evenly. Give it 5-10 mins on low heat.
*   **Acid**: Avoid simmering tomato sauce for hours in a new pan (can taste metallic). Well-seasoned pans can handle it.

## Best Uses
*   **Cornbread**: Preheat the skillet with butter for a crispy crust.
*   **Steak**: Sear hard, finish in the oven.
*   **Fried Eggs**: With enough butter and heat control, it's better than Teflon.
