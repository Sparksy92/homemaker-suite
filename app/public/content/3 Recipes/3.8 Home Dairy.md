# Home Dairy: Butter, Yogurt, & Soft Cheese

You don't need a cow to practice home dairy skills. Store-bought milk (preferably non-homogenized/pasture-raised) works for many things.

## 1. Making Butter
Turning cream into fat.
*   **Ingredients**: Heavy Cream (room temp works fastest).
*   **Process**:
    1.  Agitate heavy cream (Stand mixer, fast blender, or shake in a jar).
    2.  *Stage 1*: Whipped Cream. Keep going.
    3.  *Stage 2*: Separation. Yellow globs (butter) separate from thin liquid (buttermilk).
    4.  **Wash**: Pour off buttermilk. Squeeze butter in ice water to remove all milk solids. This prevents spoilage.
    5.  **Salt**: Add salt to taste.

## 2. Yogurt
Culturing milk with bacteria.
*   **Heat**: Scald milk to 180°F (denatures proteins for thickness).
*   **Cool**: Cool to 110°F (warm bath temp).
*   **Inoculate**: Whisk in 2 tbsp of plain yogurt (live cultures).
*   **Incubate**: Keep at 110°F for 8-12 hours (Instant Pot, cooler with warm water, or oven with light on).
*   **Strain**: Strain through cheesecloth for Greek Yogurt.

## 3. Farmer's Cheese (Paneer)
Acid-set cheese. No rennet required.
*   **Heat**: Bring 1 gallon milk to a slow boil (stirring constantly).
*   **Acidify**: Turn off heat. Stir in 1/2 cup vinegar or lemon juice.
*   **Curdle**: Milk will instantly separate into Curds (white solids) and Whey (greenish liquid).
*   **Strain**: Pour through colander/cheesecloth.
*   **Press**: Squeeze out liquid. Salt and press under a heavy pot for a block of cheese.

## 4. Kefir
A drinkable yogurt powerhouse.
*   Requires **Kefir Grains** (symbiotic colony of bacteria/yeast).
*   **Process**: Put grains in milk on the counter for 24 hours. Strain grains out to use again. Drink the milk.
