# Curing & Smoking: Ancient Preservation

## Curing (Salting)
Salt draws moisture out of bacterial cells, killing them.
*   **Nitrates (Pink Salt)**: Specifically kills Botulism bacteria. Mandatory for sausages and smoked meats that are cooked at low temps (danger zone).
    *   *Prague Powder #1*: For cooked meats (Bacon, Ham).
    *   *Prague Powder #2*: For dry cured meats (Salami, Prosciutto) that hang for months.

## Bacon Recipe (The Gateway Cure)
*   **Meat**: Pork Belly (5 lbs).
*   **The Mix**:
    *   1/4 cup Kosher Salt (basic cure)
    *   1 tsp Pink Salt #1 (safety)
    *   1/3 cup Brown Sugar (flavor)
    *   Pepper/Maple syrup to taste
*   **Process**: Rub mix on belly. Ziploc bag in fridge for 7 days. Flip daily.
*   **Finish**: Rinse off cure. Smoke at 225°F until internal temp hits 150°F.

## Cold Smoking vs Hot Smoking
*   **Cold Smoking (< 100°F)**: Does not cook the meat. Adds flavor and preservation (Cheese, Lox). Needs nitrates.
*   **Hot Smoking (200°F - 275°F)**: Cooks the meat (BBQ).

## Wood Pairing
*   **Hickory/Oak**: Strong. Beef and Pork.
*   **Fruit woods (Apple/Cherry)**: Mild. Poultry and Fish.
