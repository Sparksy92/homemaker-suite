# Pie Crust: Cold Hands, Flaky Crust

The goal: Layers of butter trapped in flour.

## The Enemy: Heat
If the butter melts before the oven, you get a greasy cookie. If the butter melts *in* the oven, the water evaporates, creating steam pockets = Flakes.

## Ingredients (Single Crust)
*   1 1/4 cups Flour.
*   1/2 tsp Salt.
*   1 stick (1/2 cup) Butter, FROZEN.
*   Ice Water.

## Technique
1.  **Cube**: Cut frozen butter into tiny cubes.
2.  **Cut In**: Use a pastry cutter or forks to cut butter into flour until it looks like rough peas. You want chunks!
3.  **Water**: Sprinkle ice water 1 tbsp at a time. Mix gently.
4.  **Shaggy**: Stop when it *just* holds together. It should look messy.
5.  **Chill**: Wrap in plastic. Fridge for 1 hour.
6.  **Roll**: Roll quickly. If it gets sticky, put it back in the fridge.

> [!TIP]
> Vodka Trick: Replace half the water with Vodka. Vodka doesn't form gluten (toughness) like water does, so you can add more liquid to make it workable without making it tough. The alcohol cooks off.
