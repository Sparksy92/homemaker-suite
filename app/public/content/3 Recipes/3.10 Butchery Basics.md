# Butchery 101: Respecting the Animal

Butchery is the final act of husbandry. It requires a sharp knife and a steady hand.

## Essential Tools
*   **Boning Knife**: Stiff, narrow blade (6 inch).
*   **Breaking Knife**: Curved blade (8-10 inch) for cutting through cartilage.
*   **Bone Saw**: For cutting through vertebrae.

## Concepts
*   **Follow the Seams**: Muscles are separated by silver skin. If you cut along the seams, you don't need a saw.
*   **Against the Grain**: When cutting steaks/roasts for the table, cut perpendicular to the muscle fibers for tenderness.

## Breaking Down a Chicken
1.  **Legs**: Pull leg away from body. Slice skin. Pop the hip joint. Cut through.
2.  **Wings**: Find the shoulder joint. Cut through.
3.  **Breasts**: Run knife down the keel bone (center). Slice gently against the rib cage to remove meat.
4.  **Carcass**: Save for stock. Never throw away bones.

## Breaking Down a Deer/Large Game
1.  **Quartering**: Remove 4 legs in the field to transport.
2.  **Backstraps**: The prized loin running along the spine. Remove carefully.
3.  **Tenderloins**: Inside the cavity, under the spine. The most tender meat.
4.  **Hams (Rear Legs)**: Separate into "Top Round," "Bottom Round," and "Eye of Round" by following muscle seams.
