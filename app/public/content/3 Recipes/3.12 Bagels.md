# Bagels: The Boil is Key

True bagels are boiled, then baked. This gelatinizes the starch on the outside, creating that chewy skin.

## Ingredients
*   500g Bread Flour (High protein is mandatory for chew).
*   290g Water.
*   10g Salt.
*   6g Instant Yeast.
*   20g Sugar/Malt Syrup.

## Process
1.  **Knead**: This is a stiff dough. Knead for 10 minutes (good workout).
2.  **Rise**: 1 hour.
3.  **Shape**: Divide into 8 balls. Poke a hole in the middle and stretch.
4.  **Proof**: Let sit 20 minutes.
5.  **Boil**: Pot of boiling water with 1 tbsp Malt Syrup or Molasses. Boil 1 min per side.
6.  **Top**: Brush with egg wash. Dip in seeds (Poppy, Sesame).
7.  **Bake**: 425°F for 20 minutes.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use All-Purpose flour. You will just make round bread, not bagels.
