# Sourdough Mastery

Sourdough is not just bread; it's a living ecosystem of wild yeast and bacteria that you care for. It provides flavor, nutrition, and preservation.

## 1. Creating a Starter
You don't need to buy yeast. It's in the air and on the flour.
*   **Day 1**: Mix equal parts whole rye flour and water. Cover loosely.
*   **Day 2**: Bubbles appearing? Discard half, feed with equal parts flour and water.
*   **Day 3-7**: Repeat daily. When it doubles in size within 4-6 hours of feeding, it's ready.

## 2. Maintenance
*   **Counter**: Feed daily if baking often.
*   **Fridge**: Feed once a week for occasional baking.
*   **Hooch**: The liquid on top means it's hungry. Stir it in or pour it off.

## 3. Basic Country Loaf Ratio
*   1000g Flour (Bread flour + 10% Whole Wheat)
*   750g Water (75% hydration)
*   200g Leaven (Active starter)
*   20g Salt

## 4. The Process
1.  **Autolyse**: Mix flour and water. Let sit 30 mins.
2.  **Mix**: Add starter and salt. Pinch to combine.
3.  **Stretch & Fold**: Every 30 mins for 2 hours. Strengthens gluten.
4.  **Bulk Ferment**: Let rise until puffy (30-50% increase).
5.  **Shape**: Create tension on the surface.
6.  **Proof**: Overnight in the fridge develops flavor.
7.  **Bake**: Dutch oven at 475°F (245°C). Lid on 20 mins, Lid off 25 mins.

> [!TIP]
> A "float test" tells you if your leaven is ready. Drop a spoonful in water. If it floats, it's ready to bake.
