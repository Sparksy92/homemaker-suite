# Fresh Pasta: Flour and Eggs

Once you make this, you won't buy dried noodles for lasagna again.

## The Ratio
**100g of Flour per 1 Egg.**
(serves 1 person).

## Process
1.  **Volcano**: Mound flour on counter. Make a well (crater) in the middle.
2.  **Crack**: Crack eggs into the crater. Fork them to scramble.
3.  **Incorporate**: Slowly pull flour from the walls into the egg pool.
4.  **Knead**: Once it's a ball, knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic. If it doesn't bounce back when poked, knead more.
5.  **Rest**: Wrap in plastic. Rest 30 mins (Gluten must relax or it will fight the rolling pin).
6.  **Roll**: Roll thin enough to read a newspaper through it.
7.  **Cut**: Roll up like a rug and slice (Tagliatelle).
8.  **Cook**: Boil salted water. It takes only 90 seconds to cook!
