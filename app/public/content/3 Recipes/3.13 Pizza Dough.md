# Pizza Dough: The 72-Hour Cold Ferment

Why does pizzeria pizza taste better? Time.

## The Science
Yeast creates flavor as a byproduct of eating sugar. But yeast works fast at room temp. In the fridge (cold ferment), yeast works slows, creating complex organic acids (flavor) without over-proofing the dough.

## The Recipe (Makes 2 Pies)
*   500g 00 Flour (or Bread Flour).
*   325g Water (65% Hydration).
*   15g Salt (High salt for flavor).
*   2g Yeast (Tiny amount!).

## The Schedule
*   **Day 1**: Mix ingredients. Knead smooth. Put in oiled bowl. **Put in Fridge**.
*   **Day 2**: Leave in fridge.
*   **Day 3**: Remove from fridge 2 hours before bake to warm up. Stretch and bake at max oven temp (550°F+).

## Shaping
Do not use a rolling pin. It pushes out the air bubbles. Use your fingertips to push gas to the rim (creating the "Cornicione" or crust).
