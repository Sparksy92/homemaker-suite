# Tortillas: The Daily Bread of the Americas

## Flour Tortillas (Sonoran Style)
*   **Key Ingredient**: Lard or Tallow. This makes them soft and foldable.
*   **No Yeast**: Just Flour, Salt, Fat, Hot Water, Baking Powder.
*   **Rest**: After forming balls, let them rest 10 mins so they don't snap back when rolling.

## Corn Tortillas (Nixtamal)
*   **Masa Harina**: Corn treated with lime (calcium hydroxide). This unlocks the nutrients (Niacin). You cannot use Cornmeal.
*   **Hydration**: Mix Masa and Warm water until it feels like Play-Doh.
*   **Press**: You need a tortilla press ($15). Use a cut-up ziploc bag as a liner so it doesn't stick.
*   **Cook**: Cast Iron skillet. High heat. 30 seconds per side. It should puff up like a balloon.

> [!TIP]
> A "Taco Warmer" is mandatory. Steaming the tortillas together in a towel after cooking is what makes them soft.
