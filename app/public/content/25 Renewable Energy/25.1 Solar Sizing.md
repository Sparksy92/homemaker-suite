# Solar Power: The Math

You cannot guess with solar. You must do the math.

## The Components
1.  **Panels**: Collect sun (Watts).
2.  **Charge Controller**: Protects battery from overcharging. MPPT (Efficient) vs PWM (Cheap).
3.  **Battery**: The tank. Stores energy.
4.  **Inverter**: Turns DC (Battery) into AC (Wall outlet).

## Sizing Your System
1.  **Calculate Load**: List every device. (e.g., LED Light = 10W x 5 hours = 50Wh). Sum total Wh per day.
2.  **Battery Sizing**:
    *   *Lead Acid*: You can only use 50% capacity. If you need 1000Wh, buy a 2000Wh battery.
    *   *LiFePO4*: You can use 90%. much lighter, longer life, expensive.
3.  **Panel Sizing**: Assume 5 "Peak Sun Hours" per day (Average).
    *   Load (1000Wh) / 5 hours = 200 Watts of panels needed (Minimum). *Double this for cloudy days.*

## Wiring
*   **Series**: Voltage adds up. Amps stay same. (Good for long wire runs).
*   **Parallel**: Amps add up. Voltage stays same. (Good for shade tolerance).
