# Batteries: The Heart of the System

The sun doesn't shine at night.

## Lead Acid (Flooded/AGM)
*   **Pros**: Cheap. Recyclable. Indestructible-ish.
*   **Cons**: HEAVY. Can only discharge 50%. Needs ventilation (Hydrogen gas). Sulfation kills them if left dead.
*   **Maintenance**: Start equalization charge once a month to de-sulfate. Check water levels (Flooded only).

## Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4)
*   **Pros**: Light. 90-100% discharge depth. last 10 years (vs 3-5 for lead).
*   **Cons**: Expensive upfront. **CANNOT CHARGE BELOW FREEZING** (0°C). Doing so destroys the battery instantly. You need battery warmers in winter.

## Nickel Iron (Edison Battery)
*   **Pros**: Lasts 30+ years. Extremely robust.
*   **Cons**: Expensive. Low efficiency (you lose 30% power just as heat).
