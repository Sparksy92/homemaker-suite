# The Home Pharmacy

When the ambulance isn't coming, you are the doctor.

## Trauma (Stop the Bleed)
*   **Tourniquet**: CAT Gen 7. Learn to use it. For massive limb spurts.
*   **Israeli Bandage**: Pressure dressing.
*   **Hemostatic Gauze**: (QuikClot). Promotes clotting.

## Sick Call (OTC Meds)
Stock generic versions in bulk.
*   **Pain/Fever**: Ibuprofen (Advil) & Acetaminophen (Tylenol).
*   **Allergy**: Diphenhydramine (Benadryl). Critical for anaphylaxis.
*   **Stomach**: Loperamide (Imodium). Dehydration from diarrhea kills.
*   **Infection**: Neosporin.

## Wound Care
*   Betadine (Povidone-Iodine): Disinfection.
*   Steri-strips: "Butterfly stitches" for closing clean cuts.
*   Irrigation Syringe: For flushing dirt out of deep wounds.

## Antibiotics (The Controversial Prep)
*   In some places, you can buy "Fish Antibiotics" (Moxifloxacin, Amoxicillin). They are often the same USP grade as human meds. **Reference a guide like "The Survival Medicine Handbook" before EVER considering use.** Misuse creates resistant bacteria.
