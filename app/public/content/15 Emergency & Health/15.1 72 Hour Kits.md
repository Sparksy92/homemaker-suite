# 72-Hour Kits: Bug Out Bags

A kit designed to keep one person alive for 3 days while moving.

## The Rule of 3s
You can survive:
*   3 Minutes without Air
*   3 Hours without Shelter (in bad weather)
*   3 Days without Water
*   3 Weeks without Food

## The Checklist

### 1. Water (Priority #1)
*   **Quantity**: 3 Liters minimum.
*   **Method**: Stainless steel single-wall water bottle (can be boiled in).
*   **Filter**: Sawyer Squeeze or LifeStraw.
*   **Tablets**: Iodine or Chlorine Dioxide tabs as backup.

### 2. Shelter / Warmth
*   **Tarp**: 8x10 Silnylon tarp (lighter than tent).
*   **Cordage**: 50ft Paracord.
*   **Fire**: Bic Lighter x2 (wrapped in duct tape), Ferro Rod (backup), Vaseline cotton balls (tinder).
*   **Clothing**: Wool socks (x2), Rain poncho, Wool hat.

### 3. Food
*   **Calorie Dense**: No cooking required.
*   Peanut butter packet.
*   Beef Jerky.
*   Protein bars.
*   Calories: Aim for 2000/day.

### 4. First Aid
*   Tourniquet.
*   Gauze + Tape.
*   Moleskin (for blisters - critical if walking).
*   Personal meds.

### 5. Tools
*   Multi-tool (Leatherman).
*   Headlamp (+ spare batteries).
*   Cash ($50 in small bills).
*   N95 Mask.
