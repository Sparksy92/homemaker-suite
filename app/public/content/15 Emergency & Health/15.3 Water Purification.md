# Water Purification: Safe to Drink

Clear water is not safe water. It can contain Protozoa (Giardia), Bacteria (E. Coli), and Viruses.

## Method 1: Boiling (The Gold Standard)
*   **Efficacy**: Kills everything. 100%.
*   **Process**: Bring to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes at high altitude).
*   **Cons**: Uses fuel/time. Water must cool.

## Method 2: Filters (Mechanical)
*   **Types**: Hollow fiber membranes (Sawyer, Katahdin).
*   **Efficacy**: Removes Protozoa and Bacteria. **Does NOT remove viruses** (viruses are too small).
*   **Pros**: Instant drinking. Lightweight.
*   **Maintenance**: Must be "back-flushed" to clean. **Do not let freeze** (ice crystals break the fibers).

## Method 3: Chemicals
*   **Chlorine (Bleach)**: 8 drops of regular unscented bleach per gallon. Let sit 30 mins.
*   **Iodine**: Tastes bad, but works.
*   **Chlorine Dioxide**: The best chemical tab. Kills Crypto and Giardia.

## Method 4: SODIS (Solar Disinfection)
*   **Process**: Fill clear PET plastic bottle (recycle code 1). Lay in direct sun for 6 hours.
*   **Mechanism**: UV-A radiation kills pathogens.
*   **Use**: Good for 3rd world / extreme survival when no other options exist.

> [!TIP]
> **Pre-filter**: If water is muddy, filter it through a bandana/shirt first. Dirt clogs filters and creates hiding spots for bacteria against chemicals.
