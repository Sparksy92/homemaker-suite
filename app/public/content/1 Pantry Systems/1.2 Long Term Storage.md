# 1.2 Long Term Storage: The Deep Larder

## Concept: Sustenance vs. Variety
The Homemaker Pantry (1.1) is for variety and daily cooking. Long Term Storage (LTS) is for calorie security and survival. These are items intended to sit for 5–25 years.
*   **Focus:** Calories, Protein, Water.
*   **Enemies:** Oxygen, Light, Heat, Moisture, Pests.

## The LTS "Big Five"
These five items are cheap, store essentially forever, and keep you alive.
1.  **Wheat Berries (Hard Red/White):** Grind into flour. Stores 25+ years.
2.  **White Rice:** Stores 25+ years. (Brown rice goes rancid in 6 months).
3.  **Dried Beans:** Protein source. Stores 10-20 years (needs longer cooking as they age).
4.  **Salt:** Use for cooking and preservation. Never spoils. Store 20lbs+.
5.  **Honey/Sugar:** Pure energy. Honey never spoils; sugar lasts indefinitely if dry.

## Packaging Methods

### 1. Mylar Bags + Oxygen Absorbers (The Gold Standard)
This creates a vacuum-packed, oxygen-free, light-proof environment.
*   **The Container:** Food-grade 5-gallon bucket (protection from mice/physical damage).
*   **The Liner:** 5-mil or 7-mil Mylar bag.
*   **The Agent:** Oxygen Absorbers (O2 absorbers). *Note: These are different from silica gel (moisture).*
*   **Process:** Fill bag $\rightarrow$ Add O2 absorber $\rightarrow$ Heat seal immediately.
*   **Result:** Shelf life extension of 5x-10x.

### 2. Glass Jars + Vacuum Seal
Best for items you rotate every 1-3 years.
*   Use a vacuum sealer attachment for mason jars.
*   Good for: Dehydrated veggies, pasta, snacks.
*   *Warning:* Glass breaks and lets in light. Store in boxes.

### 3. Freezer Storage
Best for fats, nuts, and meats.
*   **Flour/Nuts:** Rancidity is the enemy. Freezing stops the oil breakdown.
*   **Meat:** Vacuum seal to prevent freezer burn. Lasts 1-2 years reliable.
*   **Power Failure:** A full freezer stays frozen for 48 hours if unopened.

## Water Storage
You can live 3 weeks without food, but only 3 days without water.
*   **Rule:** 1 gallon per person per day (drinking + hygiene).
*   **Storage:** Food-grade jerry cans (blue) or thoroughly cleaned soda bottles. (Avoid milk jugs; they degrade and leak).
*   **Treatment:** Rotate every 12 months OR treat with bleach (8 drops per gallon, let sit 30 mins) before consuming.

## Storage Conditions
*   **Ideal:** 40°F - 60°F (Cellar/Basement). Darkness.
*   **Acceptable:** 70°F (Climate controlled closet). Under beds.
*   **Avoid:** Garages (heat swings), Sheds (freezing/thawing), Attics (heat kills nutrients).

## The "Eat What You Store" Rule
Do not store 500lbs of wheat if you don't know how to bake bread.
Do not store 100lbs of pinto beans if your family hates beans.
Store what you *actually eat*, just in a different format.
