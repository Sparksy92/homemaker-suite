# Sanitation: The Silent Killer

In 1900, Diarrhea/Dysentery was the #3 killer. In a collapse, it returns.

## The F-Diagram
Feces -> Fluids/Fields/Flies/Fingers -> Food -> Face.
You must break the chain.

## Waste Management
*   **Latrines**: 100 feet *downhill* from water source.
*   **Bucket Toilet**: 5 gallon bucket. Cover each use with sawdust/ash (Carbon covers Nitrogen, stops smell).
*   **Composting**: Humanure must cook at >122°F for weeks to kill pathogens (Thermophilic). Do not use on garden veggies unless aged 2 years.

## Hygiene
*   **Ash Soap**: (See guide 20.1).
*   **Dishes**: Wash -> Rinse -> **Sanitize** (Boiling water or Bleach dip).
*   **Quarantine**: Isolate sick individuals instantly. Dedicated caregiver.
