# Quick Pickles: The Refrigerator Method

You don't need a canning pot to make pickles. You just need a fridge.

## The Master Brine
*   1 cup Water
*   1 cup Vinegar (White or Cider)
*   1 tbsp Salt (Kosher)
*   1 tbsp Sugar (Optional)

## Flavor Ideas
*   **Classic Dill**: Garlic cloves + Dill weed.
*   **Spicy**: Jalapeno slices + Peppercorns.
*   **Bread & Butter**: More sugar + Turmeric + Mustard Seed.

## Process
1.  **Chop**: Slicing cucumbers (Use Kirby or Persian, not waxy salad cukes).
2.  **Pack**: Stuff jar tight with veggies and spices.
3.  **Boil**: Boil the brine to dissolve salt.
4.  **Pour**: Pour hot brine over veggies.
5.  **Cool**: Let cool on counter. Lid. Fridge.
6.  **Eat**: Ready in 24 hours. Good for 1 month.
