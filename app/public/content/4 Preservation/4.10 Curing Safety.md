# 4.10 The Cure: Salt, Smoke, and Safety

Preserving meat without refrigeration requires precision. Getting it wrong allows *Clostridium botulinum* (Botulism) to grow, which is 100% fatal without an ventilator.

## 1. The Danger Zone
Botulism loves:
1.  **No Oxygen** (Inside a sausage or sealed can).
2.  **Low Acid** (Meat, Vegetables).
3.  **Room Temp**.

To kill it, we use **Curing Salts (Nitrates)**.

## 2. Pink Salt (Prague Powder)
Sodium Nitrite inhibits botulism.
*   **Prague Powder #1 (Insta-Cure #1)**: For meat cooked before eating (Bacon, Ham, Sausage). Contains 6.25% Sodium Nitrite.
*   **Prague Powder #2**: For dry-cured meat eaten RAW (Salami, Prosciutto). Contains Nitrite + Nitrate (Time release).

**Ratio**: 1 teaspoon per 5 lbs of meat. Never use plain salt for long-term anaerobic storage of meat.

## 3. Smokehouses
Smoke coats meat in antimicrobial compounds (phenols) and dries it.
*   **Cold Smoke (<100°F)**: Flavor only. Does not cook. Requires Curing Salt first!
*   **Hot Smoke (160°F-225°F)**: Cooks the meat. Good for immediate eating or jerky.

## 4. Pemmican: The Ultimate Survival Food
Invented by Native Americans. Lasts 50 years.
1.  **Dry Meat**: Lean meat, dried until it snaps (cracker dry). Powder it.
2.  **Render Fat**: Melt suet (kidney fat) until liquid gold. Strain.
3.  **Mix**: 50% Meat Powder : 50% Liquid Fat.
4.  **Store**: Pack into jars. Excludes all water and air.
