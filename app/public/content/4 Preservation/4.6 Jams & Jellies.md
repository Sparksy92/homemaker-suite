# Jams & Jellies: Bottle the Summer

## Jam vs. Jelly
*   **Jam**: Made from crushed fruit (has seeds/bits).
*   **Jelly**: Made from fruit juice (clear).

## The Variable: Pectin
Pectin is the glue that makes it gel.
*   **High Pectin Fruit**: Apples, Cranberries, Currants, Citrus skins.
*   **Low Pectin Fruit**: Strawberries, Peaches, Blueberries. (These need added pectin or lemon juice).

## The Ratio
**1 Part Fruit : 1 Part Sugar.**
Yes, it's a lot of sugar. Sugar is the preservative. Low-sugar jams mold faster.

## The Spoon Test
How do you know it's done?
1.  Put a metal spoon in the freezer.
2.  Dip cold spoon into boiling jam.
3.  Wait 30 seconds.
4.  Run your finger through it. If it wrinkles and stays separated, it will set. If it runs back together, keep boiling.
