# Freeze Drying: The Space Age Preservation

## Freeze Dryer vs. Dehydrator
*   **Dehydrator**: Uses heat. Changes texture (chewy/leathery). Loses some nutrients.
*   **Freeze Dryer**: Uses vacuum and cold (-40°F). Texture becomes airy/crunchy. Retains 97% nutrients.

## How it works (Sublimation)
Ice turns directly into vapor, skipping the water phase. This means the food structure doesn't collapse. A freeze-dried strawberry looks exactly like a fresh one, but weighs nothing.

## What to Freeze Dry
*   **Leftovers**: Spaghetti, Stew, Chili. Rehydrates perfectly.
*   **Candy**: Skittles explode into crunchy puffs.
*   **Milk/Eggs**: Powder raw eggs for 25-year shelf life.

## The Cost
The machine is expensive ($2k+), requires oil changes, and makes noise. But for a serious prepper/homesteader, it is the ultimate tool.
