# 4.5 Root Cellaring: Nature's Refrigerator

## Basics
Before electricity, we stored veggies alive.
*   **Goal:** High humidity (90%), Cool temps (32-40°F), Ventilation.
*   **Location:** Unheated basement corner, garage (insulated), or clamped (buried in ground).

## Storage Crops

### Potatoes
*   **Cure:** Dry in dark, warm place 2 weeks to toughen skin.
*   **Store:** Absolute dark (light turns them green/toxic). Burlap sacks or crates. 40°F.
*   **Keep Away From:** Onions and Apples (Gas makes potatoes sprout).

### Carrots / Beets
*   **Prep:** Cut green tops off (leaving 1/2 inch). Do NOT wash.
*   **Store:** Packing in damp sand or sawdust keeps them crisp. If left in open air, they go rubbery (limp carrots).

### Onions / Garlic
*   **Need:** Dry, cool air.
*   **Process:** Cure in sun/warmth until necks are tight and dry.
*   **Store:** Mesh bags or braided. Good airflow required.

### Squash (Winter)
*   **Need:** Warmer (50-55°F) and drier.
*   **Store:** Upper shelves. Don't let them touch.

## In-Ground storage
In mild climates, you can mulch carrots/parsnips heavily (12 inches hay) and harvest straight from the frozen ground all winter.
