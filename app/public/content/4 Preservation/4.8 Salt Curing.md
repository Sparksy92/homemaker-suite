# Salt Curing: Preserved Lemons & Herbs

Salt creates an environment where bacteria cannot live.

## Preserved Lemons (Moroccan Gold)
*   **Ingredients**: Lemons + Kosher Salt.
*   **Process**:
    1.  Slice lemons almost in half (leave bottom attached).
    2.  Stuff inside with salt.
    3.  Squish into a jar.
    4.  Top with lemon juice if not submerged.
    5.  Wait 1 month.
*   **Use**: The *rind* becomes soft and edible. Dice it into stews or salads.

## Herb Salt (Green Salt)
Don't let basil rot.
*   **Ratio**: 1 cup Fresh Herbs : 1/4 cup Salt.
*   **Process**: Blitz in food processor. Spread on tray to dry for a day. Store in jar.
*   **Result**: Shelf-stable basil flavor all winter.
