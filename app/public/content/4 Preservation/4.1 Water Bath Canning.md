# 4.1 Water Bath Canning: High Acid Preservation

## Basics
*   **Target:** High acid foods (pH < 4.6). Fruits, Jams, Jellies, Pickles, Tomatoes (with added acid).
*   **Temp:** 212°F (Boiling).
*   **Risk:** Safe from Botulism ONLY because of acidity.

## Equipment
*   Large Stock Pot with Rack (jars must not touch bottom).
*   Mason Jars, Lids (New), Rings (Reusable).
*   Jar Lifter.

## The Process
1.  **Sanitize:** Boil jars or run through dishwasher sanitize cycle.
2.  **Prep Food:** Follow an *approved* recipe (USDA/Ball Blue Book). *Do not wing canning recipes.*
3.  **Fill:** Ladle hot food into hot jars.
4.  **Headspace:** Leave appropriate gap at top (usually 1/4 inch for jams, 1/2 inch for fruits/pickles).
5.  **Debubble:** Run a plastic knife around inside edge to release trapped air.
6.  **Seal:** Wipe rim CLEAN with damp cloth. Apply lid. Screw band to "fingertip tight" (don't crank it).
7.  **Process:** Submerge in boiling water (cover by 1 inch). Boil for recipe time (adjust for altitude!).
8.  **Cool:** Remove. Let sit 24 hours undisturbed. Check seal (lid should not pop up).

## Master Recipe: Simple Strawberry Jam
*   **Ingreds:** 4 cups crushed strawberries, 4 cups sugar, 1 box Pectin, 1/4 cup lemon juice.
*   **Cook:** Mix berries and pectin. Boil. Add sugar. Hard boil 1 min.
*   **Process:** 10 mins.

## Altitude Adjustments
*Water boils at lower temps at optimization. You must boil LONGER.*
*   1,001 - 3,000 ft: +5 mins
*   3,001 - 6,000 ft: +10 mins
*   6,000+ ft: +15 mins
