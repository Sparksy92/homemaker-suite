# 4.2 Pressure Canning: Low Acid Safety

## Basics
*   **Target:** Low acid foods (pH > 4.6). Vegetables, Meats, Stocks, Soups, Bean.
*   **Temp:** 240°F (Requires pressure).

> [!DANGER]
> **BOTULISM RISK:** *Clostridium botulinum* spores survive boiling water (212°F). They thrive in anaerobic (oxygen-free) environments like sealed jars. Only 240°F kills them. If you fail to reach or maintain this temperature, your food could become lethal.

## CRITICAL SAFETY RULES
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **NEVER** use a water bath canner for beans, corn, meat, or any low-acid food.
> **NEVER** use a standard electric multi-cooker (Instant Pot) unless it is a specific model explicitly rated and tested for canning. Most electric valves are not precise enough for safe processing.

## Equipment
*   **Pressure Canner:** Heavy gauge pot with locking lid, vent pipe, and gauge (dial or weighted).

## The Process
1.  **Fill:** Jars with hot food (Headspace usually 1 inch).
2.  **Load:** Put jars in canner with ~3 inches of water (check manual).
3.  **Vent:** Heat until steam flows aggressively from vent pipe. Let steam for **10 minutes** to exhaust all air.
4.  **Pressurize:** Put weight on / close valve. Wait for pressure to build to target (usually 10lbs or 11lbs).
5.  **Process:** Start timer. Maintain pressure. If it drops below target, you must restart timer from zero.
6.  **Depressurize:** Turn off heat. Walk away. Let drop to zero naturally (takes 30-60 mins). DO NOT FORCE COOL.

## Master Recipe: Green Beans
1.  **Prep:** Snap ends. Cut to 1 inch.
2.  **Pack:** Pack tightly into jars. Add 1 tsp salt. Cover with boiling water (1 inch headspace).
3.  **Process:** Pints for 20 mins @ 10lbs (weighted gauge).
