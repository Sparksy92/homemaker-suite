# Emergency Dental: No Dentist Available

Tooth pain can incapacitate a strong person. Infection can kill them (sepsis).

## Red Flags (Infection)
If there is swelling in the cheek or neck, or fever, **antibiotics (Amoxicillin/Clindamycin) are required**.
*   **Natural Alternative**: Raw garlic (Allicin) held against the gum (Warning: Burns). Frequent saltwater rinses.

## Lost Filling / Cavity Pain
Air reaching the nerve causes agony.
1.  **Clove Oil (Eugenol)**: The gold standard. Dab on a cotton ball and place in the hole. Numbs the nerve.
2.  **Temporary Filling**: Mix Zinc Oxide powder with Clove Oil to make a paste. Push into the hole. It hardens into a temporary cement. (Keep a "Cavity Repair Kit").

## Extraction (Last Resort)
Only if the tooth is loose or infection is spreading and no antibiotics are available.
1.  **Numb**: Ice + Clove Oil.
2.  **Loosen**: Rock back and forth against the bone (don't pull *out* yet, push *in* and rock).
3.  **Twist**: For single root teeth (front).
4.  **The Pull**: Firm grip with pliers (sterilized). Pull straight.
5.  **Clot**: Bite on gauze for 45 mins. **DO NOT SPITTING** (Causes Dry Socket).
