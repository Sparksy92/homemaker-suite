# 15.2 Waste Management: Closing the Loop

**The Problem:** In a grid-down scenario, a standard toilet is a biohazard. 
**The Solution:** Turn waste into wealth (Kompost).

## 1. The Humanure System (Composting Toilet)
A safe, odorless, low-tech way to manage human waste.

### The Bucket System
**Components:**
1.  **5-Gallon Bucket:** The receptacle.
2.  **Toilet Seat:** Mounted on a box (plywood) that fits over the bucket.
3.  **Cover Material:** Sawdust (best), peat moss, or rice hulls. This is the "flush."

![Humanure Bucket System](/images/diagrams/humanure_bucket.png)

### The "Flush" Protocol
1.  **Deposit:** Do your business in the bucket.
2.  **Cover:** IMMEDIATELY cover with a layer of sawdust until *no* waste is visible.
    *   *Science:* Sawdust acts as a bio-filter. It traps odors and balances nitrogen with carbon.
3.  **Fill:** When full, snap a lid on it and swap for a fresh bucket.

### The Compost Piles
You cannot just dump this in the garden. It must **Cook**.
1.  **The Active Pile:** Where you dump the buckets. Cover fresh dumps with straw/hay.
2.  **The Thermophilic Stage:** The pile must get **HOT** (120°F+). This heat kills pathogens (E. coli, etc.).
3.  **The Aging Period:** Once full, let the pile sit for **1 YEAR** before using on fruit trees (avoid root crops like carrots just to be safe).

## 2. Greywater Systems
Greywater is used water from sinks, showers, and laundry. It is *not* toilet water (Blackwater).

### Gravity-Fed Laundry-to-Landscape
Use gravity to water your orchard every time you do laundry.

**Key Rules:**
1.  **No Storage:** Greywater goes septic (smells bad) if stored for >24 hours. Use it immediately.
2.  **Mulch Basins:** Do not spray greywater in the air. Pipe it into a deep hole filled with woodchips around a tree.
3.  **Bio-Compatible Soap:** You *must* use salt-free, boron-free soap (e.g., Oasis, Ecos).

**Simple Setup:**
*   **Diverter Valve:** A switch behind your washer (Sewer vs. Garden).
*   **1" Poly Tubing:** Runs from the machine to the garden.
*   **Outlets:** Drill holes in the tubing inside the mulch basins.
