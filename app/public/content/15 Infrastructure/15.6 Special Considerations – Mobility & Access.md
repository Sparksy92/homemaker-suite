# 15.6 Special Considerations: Mobility & Access

**Context:** "Bugging Out" is impossible for many. "Bugging In" requires a home that doesn't become a prison.

---

## 1. Mobility Modifications
**Rule:** If electricity fails, stairlifts stop.

### 1.1 The "Ground Floor Rule"
*   **Action:** Immediate relocation of bedroom/sanitation to the ground floor.
    *   *Why:* Stairs in pitch black are deadly. Carrying an adult upstairs is exhausting/impossible.
*   **Setup:** Temporary bed in living room. Clear path to bathroom.

### 1.2 Ramps & Access
*   **Material:** Keep two 2x10 planks (8ft long) in garage.
    *   *Use:* Instant ramp for 2-3 steps.
*   **Wheelchair:** Keep tires inflated. Have a patch kit (bicycle kit works).
*   **Walkers:** Add tennis balls/glides for rough terrain/carpet if power vacuuming is impossible.

---

## 2. Sanitation & Hygiene (The Dignity Challenge)
**Reality:** Toilets might not flush. Getting to the bathroom might be hard.

### 2.1 The Bedside Commode
*   **Essentials:**
    *   [ ] 5-Gallon Bucket with Pool Noodle rim (comfort).
    *   [ ] Sawdust/Kitty Litter (cover smell immediately).
    *   [ ] Privacy Screen (Sheet/Curtain). Dignity matters.
*   **Disposal:** Caregiver must empty daily. Dig a hole > 200ft from house.

### 2.2 Bathing
*   **No Shower:** Use "Bed Bath" technique.
    *   *Tools:* 2 basins (One soapy, one rinse). 4 Washcloths. warmed water on stove.
    *   *Order:* Face -> Arms -> Chest -> Legs -> Back -> Perineal area (Last).

---

## 3. Emergency Evacuation (Assisted)
**Rule:** You cannot carry everyone.

### 3.1 Transport
*   **Vehicle:** Keep gas tank > 3/4 full. It's life support.
*   **Manual:** Garden Cart / Wheelbarrow / Sled.
    *   *Action:* Test moving a person in these *before* crisis. Pad with blankets.
*   **Go-Bag:** Meds are priority #1. Spare glasses. Hearing aid batteries.

### 3.2 Communication
*   **Non-Verbal:** If hearing aids die, use a whiteboard/notepad.
*   **Whistle:** Every mobility-limited person gets a whistle around their neck.
    *   *Code:* 3 Blasts = "I need help NOW."
    *   *Why:* Saves voice. Cuts through noise.

---

## 4. Sensory Issues (Autism/Dementia)
**Challenge:** Change in routine/environment triggers meltdown/wandering.

### 4.1 The "Safe Zone"
*   **Action:** Designate one corner as the "Normal Spot". Same chair, same blanket, same books.
*   **Noise:** Ear defenders (Construction headphones) block scary storm/generator noises.
*   **Wandering:** Bells on doors. Deadbolt high up (out of reach) or low down (unexpected).
