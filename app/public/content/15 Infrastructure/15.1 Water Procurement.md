# 15.1 Water Procurement: The First Priority

**Survival Rule of 3s:** You can survive 3 weeks without food, but only 3 days without water.

## 1. Sourcing Water

### Rainwater Catchment
Your roof is your biggest asset. 1 inch of rain on a 1,000 sq ft roof yields over 500 gallons of water.

**The Formula:**
> `Roof Sq Ft x Rain (Inches) x 0.623 = Gallons Collected`

**Example:**
*   **Roof:** 20ft x 50ft = 1000 sq ft
*   **Rain Event:** 1 inch
*   **Yield:** `1000 x 1 x 0.623` = **623 Gallons** (Theoretical).
*   *Note:* Account for 10-20% loss (splash/absorption), so safely ~500 Gallons.

**Equipment:**
*   Gutters with leaf guards.
*   **The First Flush Diverter:** A vertical pipe that captures the first few gallons of wash-off (bird poop, dust) before the water enters your tank. 
> [!IMPORTANT]
> A First Flush Diverter is NOT optional. Without it, your cistern becomes a bacterial soup of roof debris and animal waste. This is the single most common cause of illness in catchment systems.

### Natural Sources
*   **Springs:** Ideal source. Look for lush green vegetation on hillsides during dry spells.
*   **Dew:** In emergencies, drag a clean t-shirt through tall grass at dawn and wring it out.

## 2. Filtration: The Bio-Sand Filter
A sustainable, lifelong filter you can build from scavenged materials. It uses biology (a living layer of microbes) to eat bad pathogens.

![Bio Sand Filter Diagram](/images/diagrams/bio_sand_filter.png)

### Construction Guide
**Goal:** Create a 20-inch column of sand that water flows through slowly.

**Layers (Bottom to Top):**
1.  **Drainage Layer (3 inches):** Large clean gravel (hazelnut size). Casing for the outlet pipe.
2.  **Separation Layer (2 inches):** Pea gravel. Prevents sand from washing into the drainage layer.
3.  **Filtration Sand (20 inches):** Fine, sharp masonry sand. MUST BE SIFTED. This is where the work happens.
4.  **Biolayer (Schmutzdecke):** The top 2 inches of sand where good bacteria live.
5.  **Diffuser Plate:** A plastic lid/plate with holes, hovering 2 inches above the sand. Prevents pouring water from churning up the sand.

**The "Pause" Period:**
You must let the filter *rest* for 6-12 hours between fillings. The oxygen depletion during the pause is what kills viruses.

## 3. Purification (The Final Step)
> [!DANGER]
> FILTRATION IS NOT PURIFICATION. Filters (like sand or cloth) remove dirt and large parasites, but VIRUSES and BACTERIA remain. You MUST apply a purification step (Heat or Chemical) before drinking.

### Boiling
*   **The Gold Standard.**
*   Bring to a rolling boil for **1 minute** (3 minutes if above 6,000ft elevation).

### Chemical
*   **Bleach (Sodium Hypochlorite):** Must be plain, unscented.
    *   **Dose:** 2 drops per quart (liter). Let sit 30 minutes. Water should smell faintly of chlorine.
*   **Calcium Hypochlorite (Pool Shock):** 
    > [!CAUTION]
    > Pool shock is highly concentrated. Never add granules directly to drinking water. You must create a "Stock Solution" first. Research exact ratios—overdosing is toxic.

### Solar (SODIS)
*   Fill clear PET bottles (soda bottles).
*   Lay in direct full sun on a reflective surface (aluminum foil) for **6 hours**.
*   UV-A radiation kills pathogens.
