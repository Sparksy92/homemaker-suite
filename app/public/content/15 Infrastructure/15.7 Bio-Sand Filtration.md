# 15.7 Bio-Sand Filtration for Off-Grid Water

In a long-term off-grid scenario, mechanical filters (like Sawyer or Berkey) will eventually fail or clog. A Bio-Sand Filter (BSF) is a low-tech, permanent solution that uses biological processes and layers of sand/gravel to purify water.

## How it Works
The BSF works through four processes:
1.  **Mechanical Filtration**: Sediment is trapped between sand grains.
2.  **Adsorption**: Pathogens stick to the sand grains.
3.  **Natural Death**: Lack of light and food causes pathogens to die.
4.  **Biological Action**: The *Schmutzdecke* (biological layer) at the top of the sand eats harmful bacteria.

## Construction Materials
*   **Container**: Food-grade plastic barrel or concrete container (approx. 3 feet tall).
*   **Outlet Pipe**: 1/2" food-grade PVC or flexible tubing.
*   **Media**:
    *   **Bottom Layer**: 2 inches of large gravel (1/2" diameter).
    *   **Middle Layer**: 2 inches of small gravel (1/4" diameter).
    *   **Top Layer**: 15-20 inches of clean, fine silica sand.
*   **Diffuser Plate**: A plate with small holes to prevent the incoming water from disturbing the biological layer.

## Setup Instructions
1.  **Wash the Media**: Wash the gravel and sand until the water runs clear. This is the most critical step.
2.  **Install the Pipe**: The outlet pipe must come from the bottom but rise up the outside to a height *above* the sand layer (about 2 inches above). This ensures the sand stays wet, keeping the biological layer alive.
3.  **Layer the Media**: Add the large gravel, then small gravel, then the fine sand.
4.  **Add Water**: Fill with water.
5.  **Install Diffuser**: Place the diffuser plate on top.

## Operation & Maintenance
*   **Ripening Period**: It takes 2-3 weeks for the biological layer (*Schmutzdecke*) to mature. Water should be boiled or treated during this time.
*   **Wet Sand**: Never let the sand dry out. The water level should always be 2 inches above the sand.
*   **Flow Rate**: The water should drip at a rate of approx. 1 liter per minute.
*   **Cleaning**: If the flow rate slows too much, use the "swirl and dump" method: gently agitate the top 1/2 inch of sand and scoop out the muddy water.

## Limitations
*   Does not remove 100% of viruses (though very effective).
*   Does not remove dissolved chemicals or heavy metals (use activated charcoal for this).
