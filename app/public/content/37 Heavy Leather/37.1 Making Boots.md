# Heavy Leather: Cobbling

Civilization is built on work boots.

## The Last
You must have a "Last" (a wooden/plastic foot shape) to build a boot. You cannot build it flat.

## Construction Types
1.  **Cemented**: Glued sole. Fast, cheap, falls apart in 2 years.
2.  **Stitch-down**: The upper leather is flared out and stitched directly to the midsole. (Veldtschoen).
3.  **Goodyear Welt**: A strip of leather (Welt) is stitched to the Upper and the Insole. Then the Outsole is stitched to the Welt.
    *   *Advantage*: You can replace the sole infinitely without damaging the upper.

## Soles
*   **Leather**: Slippery, wears out fast, breathable.
*   **Rubber (Tire)**: Cut a sole from an old car tire. Indestructible.
