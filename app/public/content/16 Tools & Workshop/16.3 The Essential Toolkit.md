# 16.3 The Essential Toolkit

**Concept:** You can't rebuild civilization with your bare hands. A well-stocked tool shed is the defining difference between a survivor and a scavenger.

## 1. The Buying Philosophy
*   **Buy Quality:** "Buy once, cry once." A cheap saw that bends is worse than no saw.
*   **Manual First:** Power tools are great until the battery dies or the grid fails. Own the manual version *first*.
*   **redundancy:** "Two is one, one is none." Have backups for critical items (blades, drill bits).

---

## 2. The Core Carpentry Kit (Woodworking)
If you can build with wood, you can build shelter, furniture, and tools.

### Measuring & Marking
*   **Tape Measure (25'):** Standard locking tape.
*   **Speed Square:** Critical for cutting 90° and 45° angles.
*   **Chalk Line:** For marking long straight lines (ripping boards).
*   **Carpenter's Pencils:** They don't roll away.

### Cutting
*   **Hand Saw (Panel Saw):** General purpose cutting.
*   **Hacksaw:** For cutting metal. Stock many extra blades.
*   **Bow Saw + Blades:** For processing firewood and logs.

### Shaping & Fastening
*   **Claw Hammer (16-20oz):** The universal tool.
*   **Chisel Set (1/4", 1/2", 3/4", 1"):** For joinery and hardware installation.
*   **Bit Brace (Manual Drill):** The off-grid drill. It has infinite torque and never needs charging.
*   **Auger Bits:** For the brace. 1/4" to 1".

---

## 3. The Mechanics Kit (Fixing Things)
Machines will break. You need to fix them.

*   **Socket Set (Metric & SAE):** 3/8" drive is the sweet spot.
*   **Wrench Set (Combination):** Metric and SAE.
*   **Vise Grips:** The "third hand." Indispensable.
*   **Adjustable Wrench (Crescent):** For odd sizes.
*   **Screwdrivers:** Heavy duty set (Flat and Phillips). Pro tip: Get ones with hex bolsters so you can use a wrench for extra torque.

---

## 4. The Homestead Kit (Fencing & Earthworks)
*   **Shovel (Long handle):** Round point for digging, square point for moving loose material.
*   **Pickaxe / Mattock:** For breaking hard ground / roots.
*   **Post Hole Digger:** Essential for fencing.
*   **Sledgehammer (8-10lb):** For driving posts and demolition.
*   **Fencing Pliers:** Special tool for cutting/twisting wire.

---

## 5. Consumables (The "Shadow" Kit)
Tools are useless without fasteners. Stockpile these deep.

*   **Nails:** 16d (framing), 8d (sheathing), 4d (finish). 50lbs of each isn't crazy.
*   **Screws:** 1", 2", 3" construction screws (Torx or Robertson drive—Phillips strips too easily).
*   **Wire:** Baling wire or electric fence wire. Universal fix-it material.
*   **Glue:** Titebond III (waterproof wood glue).
*   **Sandpaper:** Variety pack (60 to 220 grit).

---

## 6. Workspace Setup
*   **The Workbench:** Build this first. Heavy, solid, waist height. Bolt a vise to it.
*   **Lighting:** Workbench should be near a window for natural light.
*   **Shadow Board:** Trace tools on the wall. If a tool isn't in its spot, you know immediately.
