# Spinning: Fleece to Yarn

You cannot knit a sweater from a sheep without spinning.

## The Process
1.  **Shearing**: Remove the fleece (preferably in one piece).
2.  **Skirting**: Throw away the dirty edges (poop/vegetable matter).
3.  **Scouring**: Wash the wool in hot water (140°F) with soap to remove Lanolin (grease). *Don't agitate or it will felt.*
4.  **Carding**: Brush the fibers so they all align in one direction using hand cards. This makes "Rolags".
5.  **Spinning**: Twisting the fibers together to create strength.

## The Drop Spindle
The primitive tool.
*   A stick with a weight (whorl) at the bottom.
*   **Park and Draft**: Spin the spindle, park it between your knees. Draft (pull) the fiber out. Let the twist run up the fiber. Wind it on.

## The Wheel
Faster, but mechanically complex. Uses a foot treadle to spin the bobbin.
