# Weaving: Yarn to Cloth

Knitting makes loops (stretchy). Weaving makes grids (strong).

## The Loom
You need a frame to hold the "Warp" (vertical threads) under high tension.

## The Mechanics
1.  **Warp**: The vertical threads stretched tight.
2.  **Weft**: The horizontal thread you weave through.
3.  **Shed**: The gap between the Up and Down warp threads.
4.  **Shuttle**: The boat that carries the weft through the shed.
5.  **Beater**: The comb that smashes the new thread down tight against the cloth.

## Plain Weave
Over-Under-Over-Under. The strongest, simplest fabric (Canvas, linen).

## Twill
Over-Over-Under. Creates diagonal lines (Denim/Jeans). Drapes better and feels softer.
