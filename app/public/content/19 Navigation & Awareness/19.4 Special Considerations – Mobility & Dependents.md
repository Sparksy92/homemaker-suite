# 19.4 Special Considerations: Mobility & Dependents (Navigation)

**Context:** A "Bug Out" plan assumes healthy adults walking 3mph. With kids/elderly, assume 0.5mph. Or Zero.

---

## 1. Pace & Logistics (The Reality Check)
**Math:** 1 Adult = 3mph. 1 Adult + Toddler = 1mph. 1 Adult + Wheelchair = 0.5mph (uphill/rough terrain).

### 1.1 The "Convoy" Rule
*   **Speed:** The group moves at the speed of the slowest member.
    *   *Lead:* Strongest navigator.
    *   *Rear Guard:* Carrying the heaviest load.
    *   *Middle:* Children/Elderly (Protected).
*   **Breaks:** Kids need breaks every 45 mins. Elderly need hydration breaks every 30 mins.

### 1.2 Carrying Capacity
*   **Strollers:** Survival Strollers (Jogging type with big wheels) carry 100lbs of gear + Kid.
*   **Wagons:** Garden carts are better than backpacks for elderly evac. You pull them. Or they pull gear while walking.

---

## 2. Tracking & Lost Prevention
**Fear:** A child wandering off in chaos is a nightmare.

### 2.1 The "Buddy System"
*   **Rule:** No one moves alone. Bathroom? Buddy. Getting water? Buddy.
    *   *Kids:* Tethers (Leashes) are acceptable safety tools in crisis. Rope or Paracord.
*   **ID:** Write name/medical info/parents' names on child’s arm in Sharpie.
    *   *Tag:* Laminated card on lanyard inside shirt.

### 2.2 Re-Grouping
*   **Lost Protocol:** "Huge the Tree." Teach kids: If you can't see Mom/Dad, stop. Sit down. Blow your whistle 3 times. Wait.
*   **Signaling:** Chem-lights (Glow sticks) on every family member at night. Different color for kids (Green) vs Adults (Red).

---

## 3. Sensory Overload & Psychology
**Challenge:** Evacuation is loud, scary, and chaotic.

### 3.1 The "Block Out"
*   **Headphones:** Noise-canceling or just earmuffs for autistic/sensitive kids.
*   **Blindfolds:** If passing through traumatic scenes (wreckage), cover eyes. "We are playing the blindfold game."

### 3.2 The Narrative
*   **Truth:** Don't lie ("We are going to Disney"). Do frame realistically ("We are going to a new safe camping spot").
*   **Comfort:** Let them carry *one* toy. It is their anchor.

---

## 4. Vehicle Evacuation
**Rule:** Car seats take up space but save lives.

### 4.1 Loading Priority
1.  **People & Pets.**
2.  **Critical Meds/Documents.**
3.  **Water/Food.**
4.  **Comfort Items.**

### 4.2 The "Stuck" Scenario
*   **Traffic:** If gridlocked 10+ hours.
    *   *Entertainment:* Tablet with downloaded movies (Headphones).
    *   *Potty:* Portable travel potty or bucket in trunk. Do not leave vehicle in traffic.
