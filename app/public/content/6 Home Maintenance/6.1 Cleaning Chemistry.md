# 6.1 Cleaning Chemistry: The Stain Bible

## The 4 Cleaners
You don't need 50 bottles. You need chemistry.
1.  **Ph Neutral:** Dish Soap. (Grease cutter).
2.  **Acid:** Vinegar / Lemon Juice. (Mineral deposits, rust, alkaline stains).
3.  **Alkaline:** Baking Soda. (Abrasive, odor eater, acidic stains).
4.  **Solvent:** Rubbing Alcohol. (Ink, marker, adhesives).

## Master Stain Guide
*   **Blood:** COLD water + Hydrogen Peroxide. (Hot water sets the protein).
*   **Grease (Clothing):** Dish soap directly on spot. Rub. Let sit. Wash hot.
*   **Coffee/Tea:** Vinegar soak.
*   **Red Wine:** Salt immediately (absorb). Then boiling water stretch (pour from height).
*   **Pet Stains:** Enzymatic cleaner is mandatory (removes the pheromones). Vinegar masks it but they can still smell it.
*   **Ink:** Rubbing alcohol. Blot, don't rub.

## Homemade Cleaners
### All-Purpose Spray
*   1 part White Vinegar.
*   1 part Water.
*   Lemon rind (for scent).
*   *Warning:* Do not use on granite/marble (acid eats stone). Use Dish Soap + Water instead.

### Soft Scrub (Tub/Tile)
*   1 cup Baking Soda.
*   1 tbsp Dish Soap.
*   Add water until it forms a paste.
*   Spread on grime. Wait 10 mins. Scrub.

### Glass Cleaner
*   50% Water / 50% Rubbing Alcohol.
*   (Vinegar streaks less, but Alcohol evaporates faster).

## The Tools
*   **Microfiber:** Traps dust, doesn't spread it.
*   **Old Toothbrush:** Essential for faucets and grout.
*   **Razor Blade:** Scrapes burnt food off glass stovetops and paint off windows.
