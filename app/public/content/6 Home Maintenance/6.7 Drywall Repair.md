# Shelter Maintenance: Canvas & Wood

Living outdoors means fighting the elements.

## Canvas Tent Repair
*   **Tears**: Sew them shut with a "Herringbone" stitch (cross stitch) using heavy waxed thread.
*   **Waterproofing**: Periodically re-treat canvas.
    *   *Recipe*: Dissolve beeswax in turpentine (danger: flammable) or rub solid beeswax bar over canvas and melt in with a hot rock/iron.
*   **Mold**: The enemy. Never pack a tent wet. If mold appears, scrub with vinegar and sun dry immediately.

## Wood Care (Cabins/Shelters)
*   **Chinking**: The gaps between logs.
    *   *Traditional Mix*: Clay + Sand + Straw/Ash. Push hard into gaps.
*   **Rot**: Check bottom logs (sills). Keep dirt/leaves piled away from the wood.
