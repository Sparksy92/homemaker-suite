# Off-Grid Laundry: The Bucket Method

No washing machine? No problem.

## The Setup
*   **Two Buckets**: One for Wash, One for Rinse.
*   **Agitator**: A (clean) plunger works amazingly well. Push and pull to force water through fabric.

## The Process
1.  **Soak**: Put clothes in soapy water. Let sit 30 mins. (Let chemistry do the work).
2.  **Agitate**: Plunge for 5 minutes.
3.  **Wring**: Essential. Twist as hard as you can. The more dirty water you get out here, the cleaner the rinse.
4.  **Rinse**: Fresh water. Plunge again.
5.  **Sun Bleach**: Sunlight kills bacteria and whitens whites. Line dry everything.

## Winter Laundry
Freezing temperatures?
*   "Freeze Drying" actually works (sublimation). Clothes will freeze stiff, then slowly dry.
*   Beat the ice out of them before bringing inside to finish drying near the stove.
