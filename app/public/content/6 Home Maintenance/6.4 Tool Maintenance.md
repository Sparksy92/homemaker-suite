# Tool Maintenance: Take care of your tools

"A poor workman blames his tools." A good workman oils them.

## Wooden Handles
Wood dries out and cracks.
*   **Linseed Oil**: Once a year (at least), rub Boiled Linseed Oil into all wooden tool handles (axes, shovels, hoes). Let sit 15 mins. Wipe off.

## Removing Rust
*   **Vinegar Soak**: Soak heads in vinegar for 24h.
*   **Wire Brush**: Scrub off the sludge.
*   **Oil**: Coat in 3-in-1 oil or mineral oil immediately to prevent flash rust.

## Sharpening
*   **Shovels/Hoes**: Use a mill file. Clamp the tool. Push the file away from you along the bevel angle. A sharp shovel cuts through roots; a dull one bounces off.
