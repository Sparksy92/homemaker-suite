# Painting: It's 90% Prep

If you just start painting, it will look terrible.

## The Steps
1.  **Clean**: Wash walls with TSP or water/vinegar. Grease prevents paint sticking.
2.  **Tape**: Blue tape on trim. Press edges down firmly with a credit card (prevents bleed).
3.  **Cut In**: Paint a 3-inch border around all edges with a brush.
4.  **Roll**: Use a roller for the center. Make "W" shapes. Do not press dry roller hard; reload often.
5.  **Remove Tape**: Pull tape while paint is still *slightly* wet (prevents peeling).

## Priming
*   **New Drywall**: MUST use primer (drywall soaks up paint like a sponge).
*   **Dark to Light**: MUST use primer.
