# Lighting the Night: Life Without Grids

When the sun goes down, the work doesn't have to stop.

## Oil Lamps
The reliable classic.
*   **Fuel**: Clear K-1 Kerosene or Lamp Oil. (Never use gasoline!).
*   **Wick Maintenance**: Trim the charred top of the wick ("snuffing") daily. A flat cut = broad flame. A pointed cut = tall peak flame.
*   **Glass Chimney**: Clean soot with a crumpled newspaper.

## Candles
*   **Beeswax**: Burns cleaner and brighter than paraffin. Smells like honey.
*   **Safety**: Never leave unattended in a tent.

## Solar Tasks
If you have small solar lights (like garden stakes):
*   **Charging**: They need direct sun. Point panels South.
*   **Cleaning**: Wipe pollen/dust off panels weekly for maximum charge.
