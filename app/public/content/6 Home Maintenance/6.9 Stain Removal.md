# Stain Removal: Chemistry Class

Different stains require different solvents.

## The Big Three
1.  **Protein (Blood, Egg, Dairy)**: **COLD WATER ONLY**. Hot water cooks the protein into fibers. Soak in cold water + enzyme detergent.
2.  **Grease (Oil, Butter)**: **Dish Soap**. Rub blue Dawn into the spot. Let sit. It breaks down oil.
3.  **Tannin (Wine, Coffee, Tea)**: **Vinegar/Acid**. Flush with vinegar.

## Ink
**Rubbing Alcohol**. Blot (don't rub) with alcohol on a cotton ball.

## Red Sauce (Tomato)
Sunlight. Wash it, then hang it wet in direct sun. Ultraviolet breaks down tomato pigment.
