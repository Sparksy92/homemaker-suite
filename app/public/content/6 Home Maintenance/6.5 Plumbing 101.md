# Off-Grid Hygiene: Staying Clean Without Taps

When you don't have a faucet, water is precious.

## The Bush Shower
You don't need 20 gallons to get clean. You need 1 gallon.
1.  **The Vessel**: A garden sprayer (pressurized) is the gold standard. Paint it black and leave it in the sun for a warm shower.
2.  **The Method**: Wet down (30 sec). Turn off. Soap up. Rinse off (30 sec).
3.  **Greywater**: Ensure your runoff goes into a garden bed or gravel pit, not directly into a stream (keeps soap out of fish habitats).

## The Latrine
If you don't have a composting toilet setup yet.
*   **Location**: 200 feet (70 big steps) away from water sources.
*   **The Cathole**: Dig 6-8 inches deep. Cover completely.
*   **Pack it out**: Ideally, pack out toilet paper in a ziploc.

## Oral Care
*   **Water Saving**: Don't run water while brushing. extensive rinsing wastes water.
*   **Miswak**: Ancient toothbrush stick (Arak tree root). Antimicrobial and needs no water.
