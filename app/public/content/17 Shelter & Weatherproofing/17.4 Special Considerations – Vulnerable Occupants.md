# 17.4 Special Considerations: Vulnerable Occupants (Shelter)

**Context:** A "Survivor" can sleep in a dirt hole. A grandmother or infant cannot. Shelter must be upgraded for fragility.

---

## 1. Temperature Control (The Narrow Window)
**Reality:** Healthy adults tolerate 50°F-85°F easily. Infants/Elderly cannot. Their window is 65°F-75°F.

### 1.1 The "Micro-Climate" Tent
*   **Action:** If house heat fails, pitch a tent indoors.
*   **Why:** Smaller volume = easier to heat with body warmth.
*   **Modification:**
    *   *Floors:* Use foam pads or 3 layers of blankets under the tent. Cold seeps from below.
    *   *Ventilation:* Leave top vent OPEN. CO2 buildup is dangerous for infants.

### 1.2 Safe Heat Sources
*   **Open Flame:** NEVER use open flame (candles/propane) unsupervised with kids/pets/dementia.
    *   *Risk:* Knock-over fire / CO poisoning.
*   **Alternative:**
    *   *Hot Water Bottles:* Fill with heated water. Wrap in towel. Place at feet (Elderly). Do NOT place directly on infant skin (burn risk).
    *   *Body Heat: "The Huddle".* Shared sleeping bag is the safest heater.

---

## 2. Air Quality (Asthma/COPD)
**Challenge:** Dusty houses, smoke from wood stoves, or mold triggers breathing crises.

### 2.1 Dust Control
*   **Action:** If "hunkering down", damp mop floors daily.
*   **Why:** Dust carries allergens. Sweeping kicks it up.

### 2.2 Smoke & Fumes
*   **Rule:** No indoor combustion without a chimney. Period.
*   **Masks:** Stock N95 masks for "Ash Days" (Wildfire smoke) or dusty cleanup.

---

## 3. Safety & Fall Prevention
**Challenge:** Clutter + Darkness = Broken Bones.

### 3.1 The "Clear Path"
*   **Action:** Create a 3ft wide "Highway" from Bed -> Toilet -> Kitchen.
*   **Rule:** NOTHING goes on the floor in the Highway. No shoes, no bags, no toys.
*   **Light:** Tape glow sticks to door frames and bathroom handle.

### 3.2 Childproofing (Crisis Mode)
*   **The "Corral":** Use furniture to block off dangerous areas (Stairs, Kitchen with hot stove).
*   **Chemicals:** Move cleaning supplies/meds HIGH up immediately. Kids explore when bored.

---

## 4. Pets in Shelter
**Philosophy:** A stressed dog bites. A scared cat bolts.

### 4.1 The "Den"
*   **Action:** Crate training is survival training.
*   **Why:** A crate is a "Safe Cave" during storms/loud noises. Cover with a heavy blanket (sound dampening).
*   **Escaping:** Pets will bolt through open doors.
    *   *Rule:* "Air Lock" system. One door closes before next opens.

### 4.2 Sanitation
*   **Indoor:** If outside is dangerous (Chemicals/Fallout), set up "Indoor Potty" zone in Garage/Bathroom using sod or newspaper.
*   **Cleaning:** Enzymatic cleaner is mandatory. Ammonia (urine smell) stresses humans and pets.
