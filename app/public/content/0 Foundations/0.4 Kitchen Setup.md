# 0.4 Kitchen Setup: optimizing Your Workspace

## The Work Triangle
The classic kitchen design principle connects three points:
1.  **Storage (Refrigerator/Pantry)**
2.  **Prep/Cleaning (Sink)**
3.  **Cooking (Stove)**

Minimizing the steps between these three points reduces fatigue. Ensure the path is clear. If you cannot move the appliances, organize your movable tools to bridge the gaps.

## Zoning Your Kitchen
Organize items by *activity*, not just by category.

### Zone A: Prepping (Near Sink/Trash)
*   **Action:** Washing, peeling, chopping.
*   **Store Here:** Knives, cutting boards, colanders, peeler, trash bags, compost bin.

### Zone B: Cooking (Near Stove)
*   **Action:** Sautéing, boiling, frying.
*   **Store Here:** Pots, pans, wooden spoons, spatulas, cooking oils, salt "cellar" (quick access salt), pepper, spices.

### Zone C: Baking (Counter/Cabinet)
*   **Action:** Mixing, rolling, measuring.
*   **Store Here:** Flour, sugar, mixing bowls, measuring cups, baking sheets, rolling pin, mixer.

### Zone D: Storage (Pantry/Fridge)
*   **Action:** Retrieval and putting away.
*   **Store Here:** Dry goods, tupperware, foil/wrap.

## The "Mise en Place" Station
Every kitchen needs a primary "cockpit" where 90% of prep happens. This should be a clear counter space (ideally between sink and stove).
*   **Right Hand:** Knife.
*   **Left Hand:** Ingredient being cut.
*   **Center:** Cutting board.
*   **Top Right:** "Garbage bowl" for scraps (saves trips to the trash can).
*   **Top Left:** Mise en place bowls (prepped ingredients waiting to cook).

## Organization Rules
1.  **Frequency Rule:** Daily items at eye/waist level. Weekly items down low. Monthly/Yearly items up high or in the back.
2.  **Decanting:** Remove dry goods from bulky, air-filled cardboard boxes. Store in square, stackable, airtight containers. This saves 30-50% of pantry space.
3.  **Verticality:** Use shelf risers and tension rods to use the vertical air space in cabinets. Never stack unstable items.
4.  **Visibility:** If you can't see it, you won't use it (and it will expire). Label everything with content and date.

## Small Kitchen Hacks
*   **Magnetic Knife Strip:** Clears drawer/counter space.
*   **Over-the-Sink Cutting Board:** Creates counter space where there is none.
*   **Hanging storage:** Rails for pots, pans, and utensils utilize empty wall space.
*   **Oven Storage:** Store cast iron or baking sheets in the oven (MUST REMEMBER TO CHECK BEFORE PREHEATING).
