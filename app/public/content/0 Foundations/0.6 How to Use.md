# 0.6 How to Use This Suite

The *Homemaker Suite* is designed to be your offline-first digital field guide and homestead manager. While it works on any device, it is optimized for use as a **Mobile App** to accompany you in the garden, kitchen, or deep woods.

## Install on Your Phone (PWA)
To get the best experience, you should "Install" this suite to your home screen:
1.  **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button (box with arrow) and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
2.  **Android (Chrome):** Tap the **Three Dots** in the corner and select **"Install App"**.

Running it as an app allows it to work without a steady internet connection and gives you a clean, full-screen interface.

## The App Workflow
1.  **Dashboard (Home):** Your "Pulse" check. See your most recent guides and featured tools.
2.  **Library (Guides):** Your textbook. Browse by category (Gardening, Foraging, etc.). Tap a guide to read.
3.  **Tools (Wizards/Calculators):** Interactive assistants. Use these to calculate water storage, harvest times, or unit conversions.
4.  **Wildlife & Botany:** Use this for field ID. 
    *   **Magic Discovery:** If you add your own photos to the system, they will automatically appear in the gallery.
    *   **Capture:** Log your own sightings using your device's camera. These are stored locally on your device for privacy.

## Offline Usage
This suite is designed for "The Grid-Down scenario."
*   **Search and Read:** Once the app is loaded, most content is cached for offline use.
*   **Printables:** For true zero-power backup, visit the `11 Printables` section and print key reference sheets and checklists.

## Managing Your Data
*   **Private Observations:** Your camera photos and notes are stored only on your local device. 
*   **Customizing Content:** If you are accessing these files directly on a PC, you can edit the Markdown files or JSON data to personalize your recipes and guides.

## Emergency Mode
In an active crisis, tap the **Emergency FAB (Red Icon)** on the dashboard. This jumps you straight to the highest-priority protocols for food safety, water purification, and trauma first aid.
