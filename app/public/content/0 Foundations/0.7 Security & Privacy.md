# Security & Privacy Policy

Homemaker Suite is designed for **maximum privacy and resilience**. Unlike traditional apps that sync your data to a cloud, this suite operates on a **Local-First** model. This guide explains exactly how your data is handled.

---

## 🛡️ The Local-First Model
Your data belongs to you. We do not store, see, or have access to any of your personal observations, field notes, or photos.

*   **Private Data Stays Private**: Everything you log (Wildlife sightings, garden plans, inventory) is stored in **IndexedDB** — a database that exists only inside your browser's local memory.
*   **No Central Database**: There is no server-side database to target. This eliminates the risk of "mass data breaches" common in traditional web apps.
*   **Offline Functionality**: Because data is local, the app works perfectly when the grid is down. Your data is available even without an internet connection.

---

## 🔒 Security Layers

### 1. Cloudflare Infrastructure
The application is served as static files through Cloudflare Pages. This provides:
*   **Automatic HTTPS**: Your connection to the app is always encrypted.
*   **DDoS Protection**: Resilience against network attacks aiming to take the site offline.

### 2. Zero Personal Information (PII)
*   **No Accounts**: You don't need to log in or provide an email address.
*   **No Tracking**: We do not use advertising trackers, cookies for identity, or telemetry.
*   **Functional Cookies Only**: We only use `localStorage` to remember if you've seen technical notices (like the Beta popup).

### 3. Media Handling
*   **Photos**: When you take a photo to log a sighting, that image is converted to a local data string and saved in your device's browser memory. It is never uploaded to any server.

---

## ⚠️ User Responsibilities

Because we do not store your data, **we cannot recover it for you**. 

1.  **Browser Selection**: Use a consistent browser. If you log a sighting in Safari on your iPhone, it will not appear in Chrome on your laptop unless you use a backup/export feature (coming in Phase 2).
2.  **Clearing Cache**: Be cautious when "Clearing Browser Data." If you delete "All Data" or "IndexedDB," your sightings will be permanently erased.
3.  **Local Backups**: We recommend taking screenshots of critical plans or sightings you wish to keep permanently.
