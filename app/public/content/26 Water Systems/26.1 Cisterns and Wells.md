# Water Storage: Cisterns & Gravity

If electricity fails, gravity still works.

## Cistern vs. Rain Barrel
A 50 gallon barrel lasts 2 days. A 2,000 gallon cistern lasts all winter.
*   **Ferrocement**: Build a wire cage. Plaster with cement. Cheap and huge.
*   **Burial**: Bury cistern below frost line to prevent freezing and keep water cool/dark (prevents algae).

## Gravity Pressure
Water pressure is created by height ("Head").
*   **The Math**: Every **2.31 feet** of elevation = **1 PSI**.
*   Standard house pressure is ~40-60 PSI.
*   To run a modern shower, your tank must be 92 feet above your house.
*   **Solution**: Low-flow fixtures or 12V RV pumps.

## The Sand Point Well
If the water table is shallow (<25 ft) and sandy.
1.  Buy a "Drive Point" (Pipe with a mesh cone tip).
2.  Sledgehammer it directly into the ground. Add pipe sections as you go.
3.  Attach a pitcher pump. Pump until clean.
