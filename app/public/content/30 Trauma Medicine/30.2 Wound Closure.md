# Wound Closure: Needle vs Glue

Closing a wound traps bacteria inside. **If the wound is dirty or >6 hours old, do not close it.** Pack it open and let it heal from inside out.

## 1. Steri-Strips (Tape)
The safest method.
*   Clean wound with boiled water (High pressure syringe flush).
*   Push skin edges together.
*   Tape across the cut to hold it closed.

## 2. Super Glue (Cyanoacrylate)
Good for small, clean slices.
*   Hold cut closed.
*   Apply glue *on top* of the calm (bridging). Do not get glue *inside* the wound (prevents healing).

## 3. Suturing (Last Resort)
Only for huge gaping wounds that tape can't hold.
*   **needle**: Curved needle.
*   **Knot**: Square Knot.
*   **Technique**: Enter at 90 degrees. Rotate wrist. Match layers (skin to skin, fat to fat).
*   **Removal**: Face - 5 days. Scalp - 7 days. Body - 10 days. Joints - 14 days.
