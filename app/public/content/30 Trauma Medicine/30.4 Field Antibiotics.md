# 30.4 Field Antibiotics (Veterinary Source)

In a collapse, human pharmacies close. Veterinary antibiotics often come from the *exact same USP factories* as human drugs but are labeled for fish or birds.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Check ID**: Tablets are often marked with codes (e.g., "WC 20"). Google these codes NOW to verify identity before the internet goes down.

## Common "Fish" Meds
1.  **Fish Mox** (Amoxicillin) - 250mg/500mg.
    *   *Use*: General infection, Strep throat, Dental abscess, Sinus.
    *   *Dose*: 500mg every 8 hours (Adult).
2.  **Fish Flex** (Cephalexin) - 250mg/500mg.
    *   *Use*: Skin infections (Staph), Wounds.
    *   *Dose*: 500mg every 6 hours.
3.  **Fish Zole** (Metronidazole) - 250mg.
    *   *Use*: Gut infections (Giardia), Anaerobic dental infections.
    *   *Warning*: NO ALCOHOL. Severe vomiting if mixed.
4.  **Bird Biotic** (Doxycycline) - 100mg.
    *   *Use*: Lyme disease, Pneumonia, Anthrax, Plague.
    *   *Dose*: 100mg every 12 hours.
    *   *Warning*: Expired Doxycycline can be toxic to kidneys (unlike most meds that just get weaker).

## Calculation Safety
Use the **Medical Dosage Calculator (Tool 50.5)** for children.
*   Pediatric doses are strictly weight-based.
*   *Amoxicillin Standard*: 20mg to 40mg per kg of body weight, divided into 3 doses per day.

## Allergic Reactions (Anaphylaxis)
*   If lips swell or breathing gets tight: **STOP IMMEDIATELY**.
*   Administer **Diphenhydramine (Benadryl)** 50mg immediately.
*   If available, Epinephrine.
