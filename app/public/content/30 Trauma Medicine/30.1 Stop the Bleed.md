# Trauma: Stop The Bleed

You can bleed to death in 3 minutes. Ambulance response is 10+ minutes.

## The Hierarchy
1.  **Direct Pressure**: Push HARD on the wound. Use your knee if needed.
2.  **Tourniquet (Limbs)**: If bright red blood is spurting.
    *   **High and Tight**: Place as high on the limb as possible (armpit/groin).
    *   **Windlass**: You MUST have a stick/rod to twist. Tighten until bleeding stops AND pulse is gone.
    *   **Pain**: It will hurt more than the wound. Do not loosen it.
3.  **Wound Packing (Joints)**: If you can't TQ (neck, groin, armpit).
    *   Stuff gauze/shirt *inside* the hole. Push it right against the bleeding artery.
    *   Hold pressure for 3+ minutes (hemostatic gauze) or 10+ minutes (plain gauze).

## Chest Seals (Sucking Chest Wound)
If a hole is in the torso (navel to neck), air can get sucked in and collapse the lung.
*   **Seal**: Tape plastic over the hole on 3 sides (lets air out, stops air coming in).
