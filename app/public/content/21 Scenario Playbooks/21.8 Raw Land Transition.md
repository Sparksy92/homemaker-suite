# 21.8 Raw Land Transition: The First 30 Days

**The Scenario:** You have arrived at a piece of raw land. No power, no well, no septic, no structure. Just trees and you. This guide is your roadmap to establishing a foothold without dying of exposure, dehydration, or despair.

---

## Phase 1: The First 48 Hours (Survival)

**Priority 1: Shelter (The Micro-Climate)**
*   **Don't start building a cabin yet.** Use a heavy-duty tarp or a 4-season tent.
*   **Location:** High ground. Avoid valley bottoms (cold air pools) and dead trees ("widow-makers").
*   **Insulation:** Get off the ground immediately. Use a camping pad or 12 inches of dry leaves/evergreen boughs.

**Priority 2: Water (The Lifeblood)**
*   Locate your source. If you don't have a spring or creek, your first 48 hours will be spent hauling water or setting up rain catchment.
*   **Filtration:** Set up your filtration system (Sawyer, Berkey, or Bio-Sand) immediately. Assume all wild water is contaminated with Giardia.

**Priority 3: Hygiene (The Morale Booster)**
*   **Dig a Cathole:** 200 feet from water. 6-8 inches deep.
*   Establish a designated "Kitchen" area away from your "Sleeping" and "Waste" areas.

---

## Phase 2: Days 3–7 (Establishment)

**1. The Humanure System**
*   Stop using holes in the ground. Build a **Bucket Toilet** (See 15.2 Waste Management). It’s cleaner, odorless, and preserves your land’s health.

**2. The Fire & Cooking Center**
*   Clear a 10-foot radius of all flammable material.
*   Build a **Rocket Stove** or a stone hearth. An open campfire is inefficient for cooking and wastes fuel.
*   Build a "wood shed" (even just a tarp over logs) to keep fuel dry.

**3. Food Storage**
*   **Bear Safety:** If in bear country, hang your food 10 feet up and 4 feet out from a branch, at least 100 feet from your sleeping area.
*   Protect against rodents. Hard plastic bins or metal cans are essential.

---

## Phase 3: Days 8–14 (Infrastructure)

**1. Permanent Shelter Prep**
*   Identify the site for your cabin or A-frame.
*   **Solar Gain:** Orient the long side of the future building to face South (Northern Hemisphere).
*   Begin clearing trees. **Save the logs.**

**2. Water Storage**
*   Set up 55-gallon drums for rain catchment or hauling.
*   Gravity is your friend. If possible, store water *higher* than your kitchen area.

**3. The Tool Workshop**
*   Organize your tools. Rust is your enemy. Build a simple "Tool Bench" (two logs and a plank) and keep tools oiled and covered.

---

## Phase 4: Days 15–30 (The Long Haul)

**1. Perimeter & Security**
*   Walk your boundaries. Mark them.
*   Establish "Lines of Sight." Clear brush that would allow someone (or a large predator) to approach your camp unseen.

**2. Foraging for Subsistence**
*   By now, your "store-bought" food may be running low.
*   Identify "The Big Three" on your property (See 12.1).
*   Set a trapline (See 13.3) for supplemental protein.

**3. The Mental Game**
*   **The "Wall":** Around day 20, the romanticism often fades and is replaced by exhaustion.
*   **The Cure:** Routine. Wake up at the same time. Have a "Sunday" where you don't work on infrastructure. Write in a journal.

---

## Essential Gear for Day 1
*   **Cutting:** Felling Axe, Bow Saw, Multitool.
*   **Binding:** 500ft Paracord, 100ft 3/8" Manila Rope.
*   **Shelter:** 2x Heavy Duty Tarps (10x12), 4-Season Tent.
*   **Fire:** 5x Lighters, Ferro Rod, 1 gal Denatured Alcohol (backup fuel).
*   **Water:** Sawyer Squeeze Filter, 2x 5-gallon collapsible carriers.
*   **Lighting:** Headlamp + Spare Batteries.

---

## The Rule of 3 for Raw Land
1.  **3 Hours** without shelter in extreme weather will kill you.
2.  **3 Days** without a reliable water source will break you.
3.  **3 Weeks** without a routine will drive you insane.

**You are not "camping." You are building a kingdom. Start with the foundation.**
