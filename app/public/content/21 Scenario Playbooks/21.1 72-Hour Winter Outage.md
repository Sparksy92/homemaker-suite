# 21.1 Scenario: 72-Hour Winter Outage (Winter)

**Scenario:** The grid is down due to a winter storm. Temperatures are below freezing (20°F / -6°C). Roads are impassable.
**Duration:** 72 Hours (3 Days).
**Goal:** Maintain core body temperature, protect home infrastructure (pipes), and stay fed/hydrated without grid inputs.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Hour 0-1)

### 1.1 Secure Heat (The "Warm Room")
*   **Action:** Do NOT try to heat the whole house. Pick ONE small room (South facing is best).
*   **Isolate:** Close all doors. Hang blankets over the doorway of your Warm Room.
*   **Setup:** Pitch a tent INSIDE this room. Sleeping inside a tent inside a room adds 10-15°F of warmth.
*   **Reference:** See `Module 6: Shelter & Home Systems - Winterization`.

### 1.2 Protect Infrastructure (Pipes)
*   **Why:** Frozen pipes burst. Thawing them later causes massive flooding.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Locate main water shutoff (Street or Basement). **Turn it OFF.**
    2.  Open the lowest faucet in the house (Basement/Utility sink) to drain the system.
    3.  Open all other faucets to break the vacuum.
    4.  Flush toilets to empty tanks. (Pour 1 bucket of water into bowl to seal sewer gas if needed).

### 1.3 Preserving Food (The Fridge)
*   **Action:** Do NOT open the fridge/freezer yet.
*   **Logic:**
    *   *Fridge:* Stays cold 4 hours if unopened.
    *   *Freezer:* Stays frozen 48 hours if full.
*   **Plan:** If outage > 4 hours, move perishables (Milk/Meat) to a cooler and place OUTSIDE (secure from animals) or in unheated garage. Nature is your freezer.

---

## 2. Daily Protocol

### Day 1: Acclimation & Setup
*   **08:00:** Eat perishable breakfast (Eggs/Bacon) before they spoil. Use camping stove OUTSIDE or in well-ventilated garage.
*   **10:00:** Set up "Warm Room". Bring in sleeping bags, pillows, books, flashlights.
*   **12:00:** Lunch. No-cook (Sandwiches).
*   **14:00:** Water check. If you didn't fill bathtubs, locate bottled water. **Drafting:** 1 Gallon/Person/Day.
*   **16:00:** Light prep. Sunset is early (4:30 PM). Get headlamps/lanterns ready.
*   **18:00:** Dinner. Hot meal is critical for morale and body heat. Soup/Stew (Canned).
*   **20:00:** Sleep early. It gets cold fast. Sleep in layers (Hat is mandatory).

### Day 2: The Slog
*   **08:00:** Check family health. Any signs of hypothermia (shivering, confusion)?
*   **09:00:** Hygiene. Sponge bath with warmed water (wet wipes if water scarce).
*   **12:00:** Lunch. Canned Tuna/Crackers.
*   **14:00:** Boredom kicks in. Board games, cards. Avoid draining phone battery on games.
*   **18:00:** Dinner. Pasta (if water is plentiful) or Beans.
*   **Note:** Keep the "Warm Room" sealed. Body heat is the heater.

### Day 3: Anticipating Return
*   **08:00:** Monitor radio/phone for grid status.
*   **10:00:** Food inventory check. Eat the "best" leftovers.
*   **14:00:** Pack up non-essentials.
*   **Restore:** When power returns:
    1.  Turn OFF all heavy appliances (prevent surge damage).
    2.  Turn Main Breaker ON.
    3.  Wait 10 mins.
    4.  Turn appliances on one by one.
    5.  **Water:** Close faucets. Open Main Water Valve slowly. Listen for leaks.

## 3. Systems Checklist

### 💧 Water
*   **Source:** Bottled storage or Melted Snow.
*   **Melting Snow:** DO NOT eat yellow snow (obviously). DO NOT eat ice (lowers body temp). Melt in pot first. Warning: Snow has low water content (10:1 ratio). Fuel intensive.
*   **Filtration:** Melted snow is generally safe but boiling 1 min is best practice.

### 🔥 Heat & Energy
*   **Primary:** Sleeping Bags / Wool Blankets.
*   **Secondary:** Propane Heater (Mr. Buddy). **WARNING:** Carbon Monoxide risk. Crack a window 1 inch. Never leave unattended.
*   **Lighting:** Headlamps > Lanterns > Candles (Fire risk).

### 🩺 Health & Safety
*   **Carbon Monoxide:** The #1 killer in outages. Generators run OUTSIDE, 20ft from house.
*   **Hypothermia:** "Paramount Rule": Use the buddy system. Share body heat if necessary.

### 🧠 Morale
*   **Information:** Hand-crank radio for news.
*   **Routine:** Keep to meal times. It creates order in chaos.

## 4. Lessons Learned (Post-Event Upgrade)
*   *Did you freeze?* -> **Buy a -20°F Mummy Bag.**
*   *Did pipes burst?* -> **Learn to shut off water faster.**
*   *Bored kids?* -> **Stash new Lego sets/books for emergencies.**
