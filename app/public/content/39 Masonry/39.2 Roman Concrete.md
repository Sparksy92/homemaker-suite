# 39.2 Roman Concrete: Building for the Millennia

**Concept:** Modern Portland cement lasts 50-100 years before cracking. The Pantheon has stood for 1,900 years. Why? The Romans used **Seawater** and **Volcanic Ash** to create a self-healing crystal structure.

## 1. Cultural History: Opus Caementicium
*   **The Secret:** The Romans didn't have "cement factories." They had kilns.
*   **The Loss:** When Rome fell, the recipe was lost for 1000 years. Medieval people scavenged Roman ruins for bricks because they couldn't make their own.
*   **Mayan Stucco:** Across the ocean, the Mayans were burning limestone to create plaster so hard it survives in the jungle today.

## 2. The Ingredients

### A. Quicklime (Calcium Oxide)
You cannot dig this up. You have to make it.
1.  **Source:** Limestone, Chalk, Marble, or **Seashells** (Calcium Carbonate).
2.  **The Kiln:** Heat the stone to **900°C (1650°F)**. A bright cherry red heat.
3.  **The Reaction:** The heat drives off CO2. The stone turns crumbly and white. This is QUICKLIME.
    *   *DANGER:* Quicklime is caustic. blindlingly so. Do not breathe the dust.

### B. Slaked Lime (The Binder)
1.  Add water to Quicklime.
2.  **Reaction:** It boils violently and releases steam (exothermic).
3.  **Result:** "Lime Putty" (Calcium Hydroxide). Let this age for months if possible.

### C. The Pozzolan (The Magic)
Lime putty alone is just weak plaster. To make it water-proof **concrete**, you need a Pozzolan (reactive silica).
*   **Roman Source:** Volcanic Ash (from Pozzuoli).
*   **Survival Source:**
    *   **Fly Ash:** From coal power plants (rare in apocalypse).
    *   **Brick Dust:** Crush old red bricks into fine powder.
    *   **Pottery Shards:** Crushed terracotta.

## 3. The Recipe
*   **1 Part Lime Putty**
*   **3 Parts Pozzolan** (Volcanic Ash or Brick Dust)
*   **Aggregate:** Evaluation of gravel/stones (fist-sized chunks for foundations).

## 4. The "Hot Mixing" Technique
Recent research (2023) suggests Romans mixed the Quicklime *directly* with the aggregate while it was still hot/reactive.
*   **Self-Healing:** This leaves tiny "lime clasts" in the concrete. If the concrete cracks later, rain enters, hits the lime clast, and recrystallizes to seal the crack.

## 5. Usage
*   **Cisterns:** Waterproof lining for water tanks.
*   **Foundations:** Provide a base for log cabins.
*   **Flooring:** Durable, cleanable floors for homes/barns.
