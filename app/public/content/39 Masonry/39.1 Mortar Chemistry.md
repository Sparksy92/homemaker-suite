# Masonry: Mortar Chemistry

Stone is forever. The glue is the weak point.

## Portland Cement (Modern)
*   Gray powder. Hardens chemically (hydraulic).
*   **Pros**: Super hard. Sets underwater.
*   **Cons**: Too hard for old brick (spalls the face). Traps moisture. Hard to make from scratch (requires 2,700°F kiln).

## Lime Mortar (Traditional)
*   **Limestone (Calcium Carbonate)**: Burn it at 1,600°F (wood fire temp) -> **Quicklime**.
*   **Slake**: Add water (dangerous reaction) -> **Lime Putty**.
*   **Mix**: 1 part Lime Putty : 3 parts Sharp Sand.
*   **Cure**: Hardens by absorbing CO2 from the air (Carbonation). It turns back into Limestone.
*   **Pros**: "Self Healing" (cracks fill in with calcite). Breathable. Easy to make off-grid.
