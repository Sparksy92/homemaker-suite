# The Humanure System (Composting Toilet)

Sewage is a waste of nutrients and a pollution hazard. Composting turns it into safe soil.

## The "Sawdust Bucket" Method
The simplest, most effective system (Joseph Jenkins method).

### Equipment
1.  **Reception**: A 5-gallon bucket inside a wooden box (the "Throne").
2.  **Cover Material**: Sawdust (best), peat moss, or rice hulls. *Must be carbon-rich.*
3.  **Compost Pile**: A designated bin outside, surrounded by straw bales.

### The Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1.  Use the toilet (Solids and Liquids together is fine for this system).
2.  **Cover**: Cover deposits *completely* with clean sawdust.
3.  **Smell?**: If it smells, you didn't add enough sawdust. It should smell like woodshop.
4.  **Full?**: Swap bucket with a fresh one. Lid the full one.

### The Compost Pile
1.  Empty buckets into the center of the pile.
2.  Rinse bucket with hose/scrub brush. Pour rinse water onto pile.
3.  Cover fresh material with straw.
4.  **Thermophilic Phase**: The pile MUST get hot (120°F+) to kill pathogens.
5.  **Time**: Let the pile rest for **1 Year** after filling.
6.  **Use**: Use finished compost on fruit trees/bushes (Zone 2), typically not directly on salad greens (Zone 1) just to be safe.
