# Rainwater Catchment: Harvesting the Sky

1 inch of rain on a 1,000 sq ft roof = **600 gallons** of water. That is free resources falling from the sky.

## The System Components

### 1. Catchment Surface (Roof)
*   **Metal**: Best. Cleanest water.
*   **Asphalt Shingle**: Okay for irrigation. Not recommended for drinking (potable) without heavy filtration due to petroleum grit.

### 2. Gutters & Downspouts
*   Must be screened to keep leaves out.

### 3. Pre-Filtration (First Flush)
*   **Concept**: The first 5 gallons of rain wash the bird poop and pollen off the roof. You don't want that in your tank.
*   **Diverter**: A simple vertical pipe that fills up *first*. A floating ball seals it off, and the remaining clean water flows to the tank.

### 4. Storage (Cistern)
*   **IBC Totes (275 gal)**: Cheap, ubiquitous. Must be painted or covered (sunlight = algae).
*   **Food Grade Barrels (55 gal)**: Good for small gardens. Link them in series at the bottom.
*   **Concrete/Ferrocement**: Best for large (5000+ gal) underground storage.

### 5. Overflow
*   When the tank is full, where does the water go? Ensure it piped *away* from your house foundation.

## Uses
*   **Garden**: Gravity feed (low pressure) or pump. Plants love rainwater (no chlorine).
*   **Livestock**: Perfect for watering animals.
*   **Potable**: Requires particulate filter (sediment) + UV Light or Chlorination to kill bacteria.
