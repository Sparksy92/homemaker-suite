# Solar Food Dehydrator Build

Preserve the harvest using free energy. A solar dehydrator moves hot air over food using convection.

## The Design
Two parts: The **Collector** (heats air) and the **Box** (dries food).

### 1. The Collector
*   A flat box (2ft x 4ft) angled toward the sun (45 degrees).
*   **Bottom**: Painted black sheet metal (absorbs heat).
*   **Top**: Glass or Plexiglass (traps heat).
*   **Bottom Vent**: Allows cool air in.

### 2. The Drying Box
*   Attached to the top of the collector.
*   Contains screen trays (food grade mesh) for food slices.
*   **Top Vent**: Chimney for moist air to escape.

## How it Works
1.  Sun heats the black metal plate in the collector (temps reach 140°F+).
2.  Hot air rises into the drying box.
3.  Hot air passes over food, picking up moisture.
4.  Moist air exits the chimney.
5.  This creates a "draft" that pulls fresh dry air into the bottom.

## Usage
*   **Apples**: Slice thin. Dry until leather-like.
*   **Herbs**: Dry until crispy.
*   **Tomatoes**: Dry until leathery ("Sun dried tomatoes"). Use paste tomatoes (Roma) for best results.
