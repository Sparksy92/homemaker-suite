# Ram Pumps: Water Moving Water

The closest thing to perpetual motion. A Ram Pump uses the energy of falling water to lift water uphill, with **zero electricity**.

## Physics
1.  Water flows down a "Drive Pipe" into the pump.
2.  A "Waste Valve" slams shut.
3.  The momentum (Hammer effect) forces a squirt of water past a check valve and UP the hill.
4.  The pressure resets. The waste valve opens. Repeat.
*   *Click-Clack-Click-Clack*. It runs 24/7/365.

## Requirements
*   **Head**: You need a drop (waterfall) of at least 2 feet.
*   **Flow**: You need a flowing stream.
*   **Ratio**: Uses 10 gallons to lift 1 gallon. It wastes a lot of water, but it lifts that 1 gallon hundreds of feet high.
