# Micro Hydro: River Power

Solar stops at night. Wind stops in calm. Rivers never stop.

## The Concept
Spinning magnets inside coils of wire = Electricity.

## High Head vs High Flow
*   **Peltier Wheel**: Little cups. Needs high pressure (water falling from high up). Efficient.
*   **Propeller**: Needs high flow (like a big river). Harder to anchor.

## DIY Generator (Old Washing Machine)
The "Smart Drive" motor from Fisher & Paykel washing machines is the holy grail of DIY hydro. It is a permanent magnet motor that works as a generator.
1.  Mount motor to a pelton wheel.
2.  Pipe water down a hill (Penstock pipe).
3.  Squirt water at wheel nozzle.
4.  Rectify AC to DC. Charge Batteries.
