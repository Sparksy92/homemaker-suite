# 19.3 Solar Battery Bank Sizing

To survive off-grid, you must calculate your energy storage needs precisely. Guessing leads to dark nights or destroyed batteries.

## 1. Calculate Daily Consumption (Watt-Hours)
First, audit every device.
*   **Formula**: Watts x Hours Used = Watt-Hours (Wh)
*   *Example*: 10W LED Light x 5 hours = 50 Wh.

## 2. Days of Autonomy
How many cloudy days must you survive?
*   **Standard**: 3 Days.
*   **Critical Systems (Freezer/Medical)**: 5-7 Days.

## 3. Battery Depth of Discharge (DoD)
Using 100% of a battery kills it.
*   **Lead Acid (Flooded/AGM)**: Max 50% DoD. (You need 2x Capacity).
*   **LiFePO4 (Lithium Iron Phosphate)**: Max 80-90% DoD. (You need 1.2x Capacity).

## 4. The Sizing Formula
**Total Wh x Days Autonomy x Temperature Factor / DoD = Required Bank Capacity**

### Example Scenario
*   **Usage**: 2000 Wh/day (Fridge, Lights, Laptop).
*   **Autonomy**: 3 Days.
*   **Battery**: LiFePO4 (0.8 DoD).

`2000 * 3 / 0.8 = 7,500 Wh Required`

### Convert to Amp-Hours (Ah)
*   **12V System**: 7500 Wh / 12V = **625 Ah**.
*   **24V System**: 7500 Wh / 24V = **312 Ah**.
*   **48V System**: 7500 Wh / 48V = **156 Ah**.

> [!TIP]
> **Go Higher Voltage**: For systems over 2000W, use 24V or 48V. It reduces wire thickness (and cost) significantly. Copper is expensive; voltage is free.

## 5. Maintenance
*   **Equalization**: Controlled overcharge to balance cells (Lead Acid only).
*   **Temperature**: Batteries lose 50% capacity at freezing temps. Insulate your battery box!
