# 9.3 Autumn: Harvest (September - November)

## The Vibe
Golden light, Hunkering down, Storage, Feasting.

## The Pantry
*   **Action:** Root cellaring. Fermenting cabbage. Stocking up staples for winter.
*   **Cooking:** Return to slow roasts, soups, baking (warming the house).

## The Garden
*   **Harvest:** Pumpkins, Winter Squash, Potatoes, Carrots.
*   **Task:** Plant garlic (for next year). Cover beds with leaves.

## The Home
*   **Maintenance:** Seal drafts. Service furnace. Clean chimney.
*   **Textiles:** Bring out wool blankets and rugs.

## Rituals
*   **Thanksgiving:** The culmination of the producer's year.
*   **Putting to Bed:** The satisfaction of a finalized garden.
