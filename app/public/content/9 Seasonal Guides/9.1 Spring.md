# 9.1 Spring: Awakening (March - May)

Spring is the season of renewal, transitioning from cold winter storage to active growth and soil preparation. Success in spring dictates your yields for the rest of the year.

## The Vibe
Mud, awakening soil, thawing streams, green shoots, and intense daylight acceleration.

---

## 🌾 The Pantry & Foraging
As winter reserves dwindle, shift your eating habits and look to the land.

*   **Pantry Stock Rotation**: Audit what is left in your winter jars. Bring older items to the front and begin planning to consume them before the summer harvest rush starts. Refer to the [Shelf Life Guide](#/library?folder=1%20Pantry%20Systems&file=1.5%20Shelf%20Life%20Guide.md) and [Long Term Storage Guide](#/library?folder=1%20Pantry%20Systems&file=1.2%20Long%20Term%20Storage.md).
*   **Wild Harvest Spring Tonics**: Fresh wild greens are mineral-rich tonics that help cleanse the body after a winter of root vegetables.
    *   **Nettles & Dandelions**: Harvest young stinging nettle shoots (wear gloves!) and dandelion greens before flowers bloom. Cook to remove nettle stings.
    *   **Wild Garlic & Ramps**: Look in damp forests for wild ramps. Take only 10% of a patch to ensure sustainable regeneration.
    *   For identification rules, consult [The Big Three Foraging Guide](#/library?folder=12%20Foraging%20%26%20Wildcrafting&file=12.1%20The%20Big%20Three.md) and [Wild Harvest & Processing](#/library?folder=12%20Foraging%20%26%20Wildcrafting&file=12.4%20Wild%20Harvest%20%26%20Processing.md).

## 🌱 The Garden
Spring is the busiest period for garden preparation and propagation.

*   **Indoor Seed Starting**: Sow tomatoes, peppers, eggplants, and summer herbs indoors under lights or in south-facing windows. Use the [Seed Starting Guide](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.3%20Seed%20Starting.md) for detailed soil mix and depth rules.
*   **Soil Amendment**: Once the soil thaws and is dry enough that it doesn't form a sticky ball when squeezed, top-dress beds with 2 inches of compost. Read the [Soil Health Manual](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.2%20Soil%20Health.md) and set up your composting bins using the [Composting Guide](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.4%20Composting.md).
*   **Outdoor Direct Sowing**: Direct-sow hardy cool-season crops (peas, radishes, spinach, kale, lettuce, and carrots). Utilize companion mapping via the [Companion Planting Master List](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.8%20Companion%20Planting%20Master%20List.md) and protect young plants from late frost using passive setups detailed in [Season Extension & High Tunnels](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.9%20Season%20Extension%20%26%20High%20Tunnels.md).

## 🏡 Home & Infrastructure
Winterize your home out of heating mode and prepare for rainwater capture.

*   **Catchment Maintenance**: Clear winter leaves and sludge from gutters and first-flush diverters. Ready your storage tanks for spring rains using [Rainwater Harvesting & Irrigation](#/library?folder=5%20Gardening&file=5.10%20Rainwater%20Harvesting%20%26%20Irrigation.md) and evaluate water sourcing setups in [Water Procurement](#/library?folder=15%20Infrastructure&file=15.1%20Water%20Procurement.md).
*   **Spring Deep Cleaning**: Open windows on dry, windy days to purge stale winter air. Shake out heavy rugs and wash winter bedding. Transition heavy wool linens into storage using the insulation tips in [Insulation & Layering](#/library?folder=20%20Textiles%20%26%20Clothing&file=20.2%20Insulation%20%26%20Layering.md).
*   **Woodstove Shutdown**: Clean ash from the fireplace and perform a draft inspection. Inspect chimneys for creosote build-up to prevent flue fires next autumn.

## 🪵 Rituals
*   **The Equinox Fire**: Celebrate the moment day and night are equal. Burn winter wood scraps and outline your homestead goals for the coming summer.
*   **First Salad Feast**: Make a ritual meal using exclusively wild gathered greens and early spring garden chives.
