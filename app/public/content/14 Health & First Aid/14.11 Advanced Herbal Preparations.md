# 14.11 Advanced Herbal Preparations

Moving beyond simple teas and salves, these preparations allow for better extraction of specific compounds and longer shelf stability.

---

## 1. Oxymels (Acid + Honey)
An oxymel is a combination of ACV (Apple Cider Vinegar) and RAW HONEY. This is the best method for extracting minerals and making bitter herbs palatable.

### The Standard Ratio:
- 1 part dried herb
- 1 part raw honey
- 1 part apple cider vinegar

### Why it works:
- Vinegar extracts minerals and alkaloids.
- Honey is antimicrobial and provides energy.
- Excellent for respiratory issues and digestive support.

### Process:
1. Fill a glass jar 1/3 full with dried herb (e.g., Mullein, Elderberry, or Sage).
2. Pour over honey and vinegar.
3. Stir well to remove bubbles.
4. Let sit for 2-4 weeks, shaking daily.
5. Use within 1 year.

---

## 2. Liniments (Topical Alcohol Extraction)
A liniment is an alcohol-based preparation designed for EXTERNAL use only. Alcohol penetrates the skin faster than oil (salves).

### Best for:
- Muscle pain, sprains, and bruises.
- Antiseptic washes for intact skin.

### Common Base:
- High-proof alcohol (Vodka) or Witch Hazel.

### Recipe: The "Warming" Liniment
- **Ingredients:** Dandelion root (anti-inflammatory), Cayenne pepper (circulatory stimulant), and high-proof alcohol.
- **Method:** Steep 4 weeks, strain, and rub on sore joints. **Do not use on open wounds.**

---

## 3. Fomentations (Concentrated Infusions)
A fomentation is essentially a "super tea" applied externally via a cloth.

1. **Prepare:** Make a very strong decoction (boil roots/bark for 20 mins) or infusion.
2. **Apply:** Soak a clean flannel cloth in the hot (not scalding) liquid.
3. **The "Wrap":** Place the cloth on the affected area. Wrap with a dry towel to trap heat.
4. **Benefit:** Delivers medicine directly through the skin while the heat promotes circulation and absorption.

---

## 4. Double Extraction Tinctures
Highly necessary for fungi like Reishi or Chaga.

- **Step 1 (Alcohol):** Extract in 100-proof alcohol for 4 weeks (pulls out triterpenes).
- **Step 2 (Water):** Take the remaining plant material (after straining) and boil it in water for 2 hours (pulls out beta-glucans).
- **Step 3 (Combine):** Mix the alcohol and water portions (aiming for ~25-30% final alcohol content for shelf stability).

---

## 5. Precise Dosing & Measurements
In survival, consistency matters.

- **1 Dropperful** = ~30 drops = ~1/4 teaspoon.
- **Adult Dose:** Usually 1-2 dropperfuls, 3-4 times daily.
- **Child's Dose:** Use Clark’s Rule: *(Child's weight in lbs / 150) x Adult dose*.

> [!NOTE]
> Always started with a "test dose" (5 drops) to check for idiosyncratic reactions or allergies before proceeding to full doses.
