# 14.3 Special Considerations: Children, Elderly & Chronic Conditions

**Context:** Medical protocols must adapt to fragile bodies. Standard dosages or techniques can harm specialized groups.

---

## 1. Children & Infants
**Philosophy:** "Calm is a Medicine."
Panic spreads faster than infection.

### 1.1 Pediatric Adjustments
*   **Dosage Risk:** NEVER guess adult fractions.
    *   *Action:* Print a "Weight-Based Dosing Chart" (mg/kg) for Acetaminophen and Ibuprofen NOW. Tape it to the medicine bottle.
*   **Dehydration:** Kills kids faster.
    *   *Symptoms:* Sunken fontanelle (babies), no wet diapers (8 hrs), dry cry (no tears).
    *   *Safe Fluids:* Breastmilk is #1. Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) is #2. Water is #3 (can dilute electrolytes dangerous in infants).

### 1.2 The "Boo-Boo" Kit (Psychological First Aid)
Small injuries cause big fear.
*   **Stock:** Fun bandages (Cartoons). Lollipops (distraction during stinging cleaning).
*   **Script:** "I am going to wash this. It will sting for 3 seconds. Squeeze my hand as hard as you can."

### 1.3 Child-Specific Supplies
*   [ ] Liquid meds (Pills are choking hazards).
*   [ ] Oral Syringes (x5) - Spoons spill.
*   [ ] Diaper Rash Cream (Zinc Oxide) - Multi-use for chafing/sunburn.
*   [ ] Nasal Aspirator (Snot sucker) - Babies cannot blow noses.

---

## 2. Elderly & Chronic Conditions
**Philosophy:** "Stability is Survival."
Disruption of routine kills.

### 2.1 Medication Management
*   **The "90-Day Cliff":** If meds run out, what happens?
    *   *Insulin:* Coolers/Evap cooling required. Failure = Death.
    *   *Blood Pressure:* Stroke risk rises. Low-Salt diet becomes critical survival tool.
*   **Organization:** Pill organizers are not optional. In crisis, memory fails.

### 2.2 Mobility & Falls
*   **The Fall Risk:** A broken hip in a grid-down scenario is likely fatal (bedsores/pneumonia).
*   **Modifications:**
    *   Clear ALL rugs (trip hazards).
    *   Move sleeping layout to Ground Floor immediately.
    *   Bedside commode (Bucket) to avoid dark bathroom trips.

### 2.3 Temperature sensitivity
*   **Thermo-regulation:** Elderly lose heat faster and overheat faster.
*   **Cold:** Rule "One layer more than you wear."
*   **Heat:** Wet towel on neck. Checks every hour.

---

## 3. Pets (The Silent Family)
**Philosophy:** "No animal left behind."

### 3.1 Supplies
*   [ ] 2 Weeks dry food (sealed).
*   [ ] Muzzle (Nice dogs bite when injured/scared).
*   [ ] Vet Wrap (Self-adherent tape) - doesn't stick to fur.

### 3.2 Waste
*   **Indoor:** Cats need kitty litter. Substitute: Sand, Dirt, Shredded Newspaper.
*   **Cleanliness:** Dog waste spreads disease. Bury far from water source.

### 3.3 Anxiety
*   **Thundershirt:** Tight wrap for anxiety.
*   **Hiding:** Cats will hide. Know their spot. Carrier should be accessible, not buried in garage.
