# 14.6 Sanitation & Disease Control

> [!DANGER]
> **THE GRID-DOWN KILLER:** In historical collapses and modern disaster zones, more people die from **fecal-oral diseases** (Cholera, Dysentery, Typhoid) than from starvation or violence. When the flush toilets stop working, sanitation becomes your #1 medical priority.

---

## 1. The Fecal-Oral Route
Diseases spread when microscopic amounts of waste from an infected person enter the mouth of another. This happens via:
- **Flies:** Landing on waste, then on your food.
- **Fluids:** Waste leaching into groundwater or surface water.
- **Fingers:** Poor handwashing after using the latrine or before cooking.
- **Fields:** Using "night soil" (human waste) as fertilizer without proper composting.

---

## 2. Improvised Latrines
If you have no septic or sewer, you must establish a system immediately.

### The Cat Hole (Short Term/Mobile)
- Dig 6-8 inches deep.
- 200 feet from water sources and camp.
- Fill and pack with soil after one use.

### The Deep Trench Latrine (Group/Medium Term)
- **Dimensions:** 2 feet wide, 3-6 feet deep, as long as needed.
- **Location:** Downhill and at least 100 feet from water.
- **Maintenance:** After every use, cover waste with a layer of **lime, wood ash, or dry soil**. This reduces smell and prevents flies.
- **Closure:** Once waste is within 1 foot of the surface, fill with soil and matted brush. Mark the site.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Fly Control:** Flies are the primary vector for dysentery. Keep latrines covered. A simple wooden lid or even a heavy piece of canvas can save lives.

---

## 3. Disinfection Protocols

### Handwashing Station (The "Tippy Tap")
You cannot rely on hand sanitizer alone for heavy soil.
- Hang a 1-gallon jug with a small hole near the bottom.
- Use a foot pedal (string tied to a stick) to tilt the jug.
- Soap must be present. If no soap, use clean wood ash (mildly caustic/antibacterial).

### Surfaces & Tools
- **Bleach Solution:** 1 part unscented bleach to 10 parts water (1:10). MUST be made fresh daily as it loses potency.
- **Boiling:** The gold standard for fabrics and metal tools. 10 minutes rolling boil.

---

## 4. Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS)
Dysentery doesn't kill you; **dehydration** from the resulting diarrhea does. Plain water is often not absorbed fast enough.

### The Survival Recipe (WHO Standard)
- **1 Liter** Clean Water
- **6 Level Teaspoons** Sugar (Energy + helps absorption)
- **1/2 Level Teaspoon** Salt (Electrolytes)

> [!CAUTION]
> Do not make the solution too salty. It should taste like tears. If the patient is vomiting, give small sips every 5 minutes.

---

## 5. Identifying the Killers

| Disease | Primary Symptom | Key Prevention |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cholera** | "Rice-water" diarrhea (profuse, watery) | Severe water sanitation/Boiling |
| **Dysentery** | Bloody diarrhea, severe cramps | Fly control, Handwashing |
| **Typhoid** | High fever, "Rose spots" on chest | Human waste isolation |
| **Giardia** | Pathogen from "clear" mountain water | 1-micron filtration or Boiling |

**Treatment Principle:** In the absence of antibiotics, focus entirely on **Hydration (ORS)** and **Nutritional Support**. Avoid "anti-diarrheal" meds like Imodium if blood is present; the body needs to flush the pathogen out.
