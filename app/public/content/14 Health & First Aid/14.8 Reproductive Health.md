# 14.8 Reproductive Health & Hygiene

> [!NOTE]
> In an austere environment, reproductive health management is essential for long-term survival. Lack of sanitation supplies and specialized care can lead to avoidable infections and complications.

---

## 1. Menstrual Management
Without disposables (pads/tampons), sustainable systems are required.

### Improvised Pads
- **Material:** Soft, absorbent cotton fabrics (old t-shirts, flannel).
- **Design:** Fold fabrics into layers.
- **Sanitation:** Used cloths MUST be soaked in cold water immediately, then washed with soap and **boiled** to prevent bacterial growth before reuse.

### Natural Management
- **Red Raspberry Leaf Tea:** Excellent for reducing heavy flow and easing cramps (uterine tonic).
- **Cramp Relief:** Peppermint tea or warm compresses on the lower abdomen.

---

## 2. Preventing Infections (UTIs & Yeast)
Poor hygiene and high stress make these common and dangerous if they progress to the kidneys.

### Prevention:
- **Hygiene:** Use a "Peri-bottle" (improvised with a water bottle with a hole in the cap) to rinse after using the latrine. 
- **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water to flush the urinary tract.
- **Clothing:** Wear loose-fitting, breathable cotton. Boil underwear periodically to ensure sterility.

### Natural Remedies:
- **Cranberry/Dandelion:** For UTIs, drink strong dandelion root tea (diuretic) or wild cranberry if available.
- **Garlic:** For yeast infections, increasing dietary garlic or using a diluted apple cider vinegar wash (1 tbsp per cup water) can restore pH balance.

---

## 3. Basic Maternal Care
If pregnancy occurs in a grid-down scenario, monitoring becomes a community effort.

### Nutrition:
- **Caloric Surge:** A pregnant person needs ~300 extra calories daily, and significantly more during breastfeeding.
- **Folate/Iron:** Increase consumption of dark leafy greens (Dandelion, Spinach) and red meats/liver.

### Danger Signs (Escalate Treatment):
- **Severe Preeclampsia:** Persistent high-intensity headaches, blurred vision, or massive swelling in hands/face. High risk of seizures.
- **Hemorrhage:** Profuse bleeding that cannot be slowed by rest.
- **Infection:** Foul-smelling discharge or high fever during or after pregnancy.

---

## 4. Family Planning
In extreme survival, limiting new births may be a strategic necessity for calorie management.

- **Breastfeeding (Lactational Amenorrhea):** Can suppress fertility if the infant is exclusively breastfed, but is not 100% reliable.
- **Natural Cycle Tracking:** Monitoring basal body temperature and cervical mucus (Fertility Awareness Method) is the most effective drug-free method, though it requires strict discipline.
- **Barrier Methods:** Improvised barriers are highly unreliable and carry high risk of infection.
