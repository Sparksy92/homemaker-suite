# Timber Framing: No Nails Required

Metal rusts. Wood rots. But a Joined frame lasts 500 years.

## The Joint: Mortise and Tenon
1.  **Mortise**: The Hole (Female). Chiseled out of the post.
2.  **Tenon**: The Tongue (Male). Sawed onto the beam.
3.  **Peg**: A dry oak dowel that is driven through both. It locks them together.
    *   *Draw Boring*: Drill the hole in the Tenon slightly offset (closer to shoulder). When you drive the tapered peg, it pulls the joint insanely tight.

## The Bent
You don't build a barn one stick at a time.
1.  Assemble a whole wall section ("The Bent") flat on the ground.
2.  Get 50 people with ropes and pike poles.
3.  **Raise**: Lift the whole wall up at once.
4.  Connect Bents with "Girts" (horizontal beams).
