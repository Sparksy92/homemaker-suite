# Hard Cheese: Storable Milk

Fresh milk spoils in 4 hours. Cheddar lasts for 4 years.

## The Chemistry
1.  **Acidify**: Milk + Bacteria (Culture) = Lactic Acid.
2.  **Coagulate**: Milk + Rennet (Enzyme) = Curds (Solids) + Whey (Liquid).
3.  **Dehydrate**: Cut/Cook/Press the curds to remove water.
4.  **Age**: Bacterial gangs fight for territory, creating flavor.

## Rennet Sources
*   **Tablets**: Store bought vegetable rennet.
*   **Animal**: The fourth stomach (Abomasum) of a unweaned calf/kid/lamb. Dry it, slice it, soak in water.
*   **Plant**: Stinging Nettle or Cardoon Thistle (weaker, bitter).

## Making Farmhouse Cheddar
1.  **Ripen**: Heat milk to 90°F. Add culture. Wait 45 mins.
2.  **Set**: Add Rennet. Wait 45 mins until "Clean Break" (gel splits cleanly).
3.  **Cut**: Cut curds into cubes. Cook slowly to 100°F.
4.  **Drain & Salt**: Drain whey. Mix curds with salt (prevents bad bacteria).
5.  **Press**: Put in mold. Press with 50lbs weight for 24 hours.
6.  **Wax**: Dip in wax to prevent mold. Age 3-12 months.
