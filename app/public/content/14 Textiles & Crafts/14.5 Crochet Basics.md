# Crochet: One Hook Wonder

Crochet is faster than knitting and easier to fix mistakes. It creates a thicker, more structural fabric.

## The Basic Stitches
1.  **Chain (ch)**: The foundation. Braid of loops.
2.  **Single Crochet (sc)**: Tight, dense stitch. Good for stuffed animals (Amigurumi).
3.  **Double Crochet (dc)**: Tall, airy stitch. Good for granny squares and blankets.

## Granny Squares
The classic block. worked in rounds from the center out. You can make 100 squares over a year and sew them together into a blanket.

## Tools
*   **Hook**: H (5mm) is the standard starter size.
*   **Yarn**: Acrylic is cheap/washable. Wool is warm/fire-resistant. Cotton is absorbent.
