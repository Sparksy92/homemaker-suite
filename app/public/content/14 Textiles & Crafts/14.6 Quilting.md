# Quilting: Painting with Fabric

Quilting uses three layers: Top (Pieced), Batting (Fluff), Backing.

## The Nine Patch (Beginner Block)
1.  Cut 5 squares of Dark fabric (5 inches).
2.  Cut 4 squares of Light fabric (5 inches).
3.  Sew them in 3 rows:
    *   Row 1: Dark-Light-Dark
    *   Row 2: Light-Dark-Light
    *   Row 3: Dark-Light-Dark
4.  Sew the rows together.

## Nesting Seams
Iron your seams to the side (toward the dark fabric). This makes the corners lock together perfectly ("nest") so you get sharp intersections.

## Binding
The strip of fabric that wraps around the raw edges to finish the quilt.
