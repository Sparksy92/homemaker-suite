# Knitting: Two Sticks and a String

Knitting creates a flexible, stretchy fabric suitable for garments.

## The Basic Stitches
1.  **Cast On**: Putting loops on the needle to start. (Long Tail Cast On is best).
2.  **Knit Stitch (K)**: The "V" shape. Smooth.
3.  **Purl Stitch (P)**: The bump.
4.  **Bind Off**: Taking loops off at the end.

## Fabric Types
*   **Garter Stitch**: Knit every row. Bumpy, stretchy, doesn't curl. Scarf classic.
*   **Stockinette**: Knit one row, Purl next row. Smooth "V" side, bumpy side. Curls at edges.
*   **Ribbing**: Knit 1, Purl 1. Stretchy cuffs.

## First Project: Dishcloth
Cotton yarn. Size 7 needles. Cast on 40 stitches. Knit every row until square. Bind off.
