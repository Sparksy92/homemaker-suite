# Visible Mending: Sashiko

Don't hide the tear. Celebrate it. Sashiko is Japanese embroidery used to reinforce workwear.

## The Method
1.  **The Patch**: Put a patch of denim *behind* the hole.
2.  **The Thread**: Use thick white cotton thread (Sashiko thread or Embroidery floss).
3.  **The Stitch**: Simple running stitch (dash-dash-dash).
4.  **The Pattern**: Sew rows of running stitches across the hole and onto the solid fabric. This anchors the patch and strengthens the weakened area.

## Darning (Socks)
Weaving new fabric over a hole.
1.  **Warp**: Sew vertical threads across the hole.
2.  **Weft**: Weave horizontal threads *over-under-over-under* the vertical threads.
