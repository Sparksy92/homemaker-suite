# Advanced Apothecary: Making Medicine

Tea is weak. You need potent concentrates.

## Tinctures (Alcohol Extraction)
Best for extracting Alkaloids (Painkillers).
1.  **Menstruum**: High proof alcohol (Vodka is okay, Everclear is better).
2.  **Ratio**: 1 part dried herb : 5 parts alcohol.
3.  **Process**: Jar it. Shake daily for 6 weeks. Strain.
4.  **Dosage**: Droppers, not cups.

## Salves (Oil Extraction)
Best for skin issues (Plantain, Calendula).
1.  **Infused Oil**: Cook dried herbs in olive oil on low double boiler for 4 hours. Strain.
2.  **Thicken**: Add Beeswax (1 oz wax per 4 oz oil).
3.  **Pour**: Cools into a balm.

## Poultice (The "Spit" Method)
Fastest field medic.
1.  Chew up the fresh leaf (activates enzymes).
2.  Slap the green wad directly on the bee sting/cut.
3.  Plantain (broadleaf weed) is the king of this.
