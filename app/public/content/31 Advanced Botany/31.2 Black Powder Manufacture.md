# 31.2 Black Powder Manufacture: The Fire Medicine

**Concept:** Modern smokeless powder (nitrocellulose) is impossible to make in a bucket. Black powder is simple chemistry. It fouls barrels and creates smoke, but it goes "bang" for 1000 years.

## 1. Cultural History: The Taoist Mistake
*   **China (9th Century):** Taoist monks trying to find the "Elixir of Life" mixed sulfur, charcoal, and saltpeter. It burned their faces off. They called it *Huoyao* ("Fire Medicine").
*   **Europe (13th Century):** Roger Bacon codified the formula. Armies moved from bows to "hand gonnes."
*   **The American Frontier:** Every homestead knew how to make it, though they preferred to buy the "good stuff" (Dupont).

## 2. The Holy Trinity (The Formula)
The classic ratio by weight is **75:15:10**.
*   **75% Potassium Nitrate (Saltpeter)** - The Oxidizer.
*   **15% Charcoal** - The Fuel.
*   **10% Sulfur** - The Ignition/Catalyst.

## 3. Gathering Ingredients

### Charcoal (15%)
*   **Source:** Willow, Alder, or Grapevine.
*   **Process:** Make charcoal in a retort (sealed tin with a small vent hole) to cook off volatiles without burning the wood to ash.
*   **Quality:** Must be air-float fine.

### Sulfur (10%)
*   **Source:** Difficult to harvest naturally (volcanic vents).
*   **Scavenge:** Garden centers (soil acidifier), drywall supply (some older plasters), or chemistry sets.
*   **Function:** Lowers the ignition temperature.

### Saltpeter (75%) - The Hard Part
*   **Nitre Beds (French Method):**
    1.  Dig a trench. Fill with manure, urine, straw, and wood ash (Potash).
    2.  Cover and keep moist with urine for **10-12 months**.
    3.  A white crust forms on top. This is Calcium Nitrate.
    4.  **Leaching:** Run water through the dirt to dissolve the nitrates.
    5.  **Conversion:** Add Wood Ash (Potassium Carbonate) to the liquid. This converts Calcium Nitrate -> Potassium Nitrate.
    6.  **Boil & Crystallize:** Boil the liquid. Salt crystals fall out first (discard). Cool the liquid. Needle-shaped Saltpeter crystals form.

## 4. The Process: Corning (The Secret Sauce)
If you just mix the powders, they separate. You get a "fizzle," not a "bang." You must **Corn** it.
1.  **Mill:** Ball mill the ingredients separately, then together (wet) for 24 hours. **DANGER: Spark risk.**
2.  **Wet Press:** Add a tiny amount of stale urine or alcohol (dextrin binder helps). Press the mush into a solid "puck" (density = power).
3.  **Grind:** dry the puck, then gently grind it into grains.
4.  **Sift:**
    *   **FFg:** Medium grains (Rifles/Shotguns).
    *   **FFFFg:** Fine dust (Pistols/Priming pans).

## 5. Usage
*   **Reloading:** Can reload shotgun shells and some pistol cartridges (.38 special, .45 Colt).
*   **Muzzleloaders:** The primary use case.
*   **Signaling:** Pipe bombs (stump removal) or flares.

**Safety:** This is an explosive. Static electricity can ignite fine dust. Manufacture small batches only.
