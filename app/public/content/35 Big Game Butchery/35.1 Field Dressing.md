# Field Dressing: Big Game

Turning a 1,000lb cow or 200lb deer into food before it rots.

## The Clock
Bacteria starts immediately. You must remove the guts (heat source) within 1 hour.

## Steps
1.  **Position**: Rolling onto back. Spread legs.
2.  **The Zipper**: Cut skin from anus to throat. **DO NOT CUT THE STOMACH**. Use your fingers to push guts away from the knife tip.
3.  **The Esophagus (Windpipe)**: Cut it high in the neck. Tie it directly into a knot (prevents stomach content leaking).
4.  **The Rectum**: Cut circle around anus. Pull it out and tie it off.
5.  **The Spill**: Roll animal on side. Pull guts out. Save Liver and Heart. Check liver for spots (disease).
6.  **Cooling**: Prop chest cavity open with a stick to let heat escape.

## Quartering
If you can't carry the whole animal:
1.  Remove Front Shoulders (no bone connection, just muscle).
2.  Remove Hind Quarters (ball and socket joint).
3.  Remove Backstraps (long muscle along spine).
4.  Remove Tenderloins (inside the cavity, under the spine).
