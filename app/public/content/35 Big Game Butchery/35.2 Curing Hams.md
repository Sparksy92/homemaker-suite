# Curing: The Country Ham

Preserving meat without electricity.

## Salt Curing (Prosciutto Style)
You need a whole hind leg of a pig.
1.  **The Cure**: Mix Salt and Sugar (mostly Salt). A lot of it.
2.  **The Blood**: Massage the leg to push all blood out of the femoral artery.
3.  **The Pack**: Bury the sterile leg in a box of salt. Keep cool (35-40°F) for 40 days (1 day per pound + 10 days).
4.  **The Wash**: Rinse off excess salt.
5.  **The Hang**: Hang in a cool, airy place (wrapped in cheesecloth to stop flies) for 6-12 months.
6.  **The result**: Hard, salty meat that lasts forever at room temperature. Slice paper thin.

## Smoke
Cold smoke (under 90°F) aids preservation by adding antimicrobial compounds to the surface.
