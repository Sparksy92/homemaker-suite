# Oats: The Easiest Grain

Oats are easier to grow than wheat and grow in poor/wet soil.

## Hullless Oats
**Critical**: Standard oats have a hard hull that is almost impossible to remove without industrial rollers.
**You MUST buy "Hullless Oats"** (Avena nuda). The hull falls off during threshing.

## Growing
1.  **Plant**: Early Spring (as soon as ground thaws).
2.  **Density**: Broadcast thickly. Oats outcompete weeds better than wheat.
3.  **Harvest**: When the tops start to turn white.

## Uses
*   **Rolled Oats**: Run through a "Flocker" (hand crank roller).
*   **Steel Cut**: Chopped in a coarse grinder.
*   **Straw**: Oat straw is the softest/best bedding for animals.
