# Field Corn: Growing Calories

Garden sweet corn is for taste. Field corn ("Dent Corn") is for survival. It dries on the stalk and becomes flour/meal.

## Varieties
*   **Dent Corn**: High starch. Used for cornmeal, grits, livestock feed.
*   **Flint Corn**: Very hard outer shell. Stores forever. Popcorn is a type of flint corn.
*   **Best Heirlooms**: "Bloody Butcher" (Red Dent), "Hopi Blue" (Flour).

## Planting (The Three Sisters)
Corn is a heavy feeder (Nitrogen).
1.  **Nitrogen**: Plant in blocks (for pollination), not rows.
2.  **Spacing**: 12 inches apart.
3.  **Timing**: Soil must be 60°F.

## Harvesting & Curing
1.  **Leave it**: Do not harvest when "ripe" (milky). Leave it until the stalks are brown and dead.
2.  **The Dent**: The kernels will dent inward as they dry.
3.  **Storage**: Shuck and hang ears in a mesh bag (crib) where mice can't get them.
4.  **Processing**: You need a cast iron grain mill to turn this into cornmeal.
