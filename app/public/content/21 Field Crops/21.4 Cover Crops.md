# Cover Crops: Green Manure

Never leave soil bare. Bare soil dies (UV kills bacteria, rain erodes topsoil).

## The Strategy
If you aren't growing food, grow soil.
1.  **Legumes (Nitrogen Fixers)**: Clover, Vetch, Peas. They pull Nitrogen from the air and store it in root nodules.
2.  **Biomass (Carbon)**: Rye, Sorghum. They grow huge root systems that add organic matter.
3.  **Taproots (Tillers)**: Daikon Radish. Drills deep holes to break up clay.

## Termination
How do you kill it before planting your real crop?
1.  **Winter Kill**: Plant Oat/Peas in fall. Winter kills them. They form a mulch mat for Spring.
2.  **Chop & Drop**: Cut it down with a scythe just as it flowers. Leave it on the surface as mulch.
