# Wheat: The Staff of Life

You don't need a combine harvester. You can grow enough wheat for bread in a backyard (1,000 sq ft = 1 bushel = 60 lbs = 50 loaves of bread).

## Winter vs. Spring
*   **Winter Wheat**: Planted in Fall (Sept/Oct). It sprouts, goes dormant in winter, and shoots up in Spring. Harvest in July. Higher yield.
*   **Spring Wheat**: Planted in Spring. Harvest in August. Lower yield but higher protein (better for bread).
*   **Variety**: "Hard Red Winter" is the standard bread wheat.

## The Process
1.  **Broadcast**: Throw seed by hand ("Broadcasting"). Rake it into the soil.
2.  **Harvest**: Use a Scythe or Sickle. Cut when heads bend down ("The Neck bends") and grain is hard.
3.  **Shocking**: Tie bundles ("Sheaves") and stand them up to dry in the field.

## Threshing & Winnowing (The hard part)
1.  **Thresh**: Beat the heads with a stick (Flail) to knock grain loose.
2.  **Winnow**: Pour grain from bucket to bucket in front of a fan/wind. The chaff (husk) blows away; the heavy berry falls.
