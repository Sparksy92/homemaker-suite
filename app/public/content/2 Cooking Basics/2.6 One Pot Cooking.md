# 2.6 One Pot Cooking: Maximum Flavor, Minimum Cleanup

## The Method
One pot cooking isn't just dumping things in a pot. It is *layering* flavors in a single vessel.

## The 5 Steps of a One-Pot Meal

### Step 1: The Sear (High Heat)
*   **Action:** Brown your meat (chicken thighs, beef cubes, sausage) in fat.
*   **Goal:** Create "fond" (the brown sticky bits on the bottom of the pot). This is pure flavor.
*   **Status:** Remove meat to a plate. (It doesn't need to be cooked through, just browned).

### Step 2: The Aromatics (Medium Heat)
*   **Action:** Add onions, carrots, celery (Mirepoix) to the existing fat/fond.
*   **Goal:** Soften veg and lift some of the fond.
*   **Add:** Garlic and spices usually go in the last minute of this step so they don't burn.

### Step 3: The Deglaze (Liquid)
*   **Action:** Pour in liquid (wine, broth, water, tomatoes).
*   **Goal:** Scrape the bottom of the pot with a wooden spoon. Dissolve the fond into the liquid. This creates the sauce base.

### Step 4: The Simmer (Low Heat)
*   **Action:** Return meat to the pot. Add tougher veg (potatoes) or grains (rice/lentils) if the recipe calls for it.
*   **Goal:** Cook everything through slowly. Flavors meld.

### Step 5: The Finish (Off Heat)
*   **Action:** Stir in delicate things that heat would destroy.
*   **Add:** Fresh herbs, a squeeze of lemon (acid), a splash of cream, or tender greens (spinach wilts in seconds).

## Master Recipes (Templates)

### The Stew Template
*   Meat (Beef/Pork) + Root Veg + Broth + Herbs. Long simmer (2 hours) or oven.

### The Rice Skillet Template
*   Sausage/Chicken + Onion/Pepper + Rice + Broth (2:1 ratio to rice). Cover and steam 20 mins.

### The Pasta Template
*   Ground Meat + Onion + Tomato Sauce + Water + Pasta shapes.
*   *Note:* You need more liquid than usual because the pasta absorbs it. Stir often to prevent sticking.

### The Soup Template
*   Aromatics + Main Veg/Bean + Broth. Simmer until soft. Blend (optional).
