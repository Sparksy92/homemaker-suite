# Mise en Place: Everything in its Place

This is the professional chef's secret weapon. It is not a technique; it is a philosophy.

## The Problem
You start cooking stir-fry. You throw garlic in hot oil. You turn around to chop the broccoli. By the time you turn back, the garlic is burnt black.

## The Solution
**Do not turn on the heat until 100% of cutting is done.**
1.  **Read**: Read recipe.
2.  **Gather**: Get all ingredients on counter.
3.  **Prep**: Chop onions, mince garlic, measure soy sauce into little bowls.
4.  **Clean**: Wipe down board. Move trash to bin.
5.  **Cook**: Only now do you turn on the stove.

## Why it works
Cooking becomes a stress-free assembly process instead of a frantic race against time. The "Homemaker" is never frantic.
