# 2.1 Knife Skills: The Foundation of Cooking

## Safety First
1.  **Sharp is Safe:** A dull knife slips; a sharp knife bites. Keep it honed.
2.  **The Claw:** Curl your fingertips under your knuckles on your non-knife hand. The knife blade should glide against your knuckles, making it impossible to cut your fingertips.
3.  **Secure the Board:** Place a damp paper towel or non-slip mat under your cutting board so it doesn't slide.
4.  **The Grip:** Pinch the blade itself (at the heel) with your thumb and index finger. Wrap remaining 3 fingers around the handle. Do not put your index finger along the spine.

## Basic Cuts

### 1. Slice vs. Chop
*   **Slice:** A sawing or pulling motion. For meat, bread, and tomatoes. (Let the knife do the work).
*   **Chop:** A downward push (often tip-fulcrum). For onions, carrots, celery.

### 2. The Mechanics of the Dice
How to turn a round loose onion into perfect square bits.
1.  **Cut in half** through the root. Peel skin.
2.  **Horizontal cuts:** Cut parallel to the board, towards the root, but *not through* the root.
3.  **Vertical cuts:** Cut straight down, *not through* the root.
4.  **The finish:** Cut across your previous cuts. The onion falls apart in cubes. The root holds it together until the final moment.

### 3. Sizes
*   **Rough Chop:** Irregular pieces (approx 1 inch). For stocks and roasting.
*   **Cube/Medium Dice:** 1/2 inch cubes. For stews and roasted veggies.
*   **Small Dice:** 1/4 inch cubes. For soups, salsas, and sauté bases.
*   **Mince:** As small as possible (< 1/8 inch). For garlic, ginger, and pungent aromatics.
*   **Julienne:** Matchsticks (1/8 x 1/8 x 2 inches). For stir-fries and salads.

## Care & Maintenance
*   **Honing (Daily):** Realigns the microscopic teeth of the edge. Hold steel vertical. Swipe knife down at 15-20° angle. 4-5 swipes per side.
*   **Sharpening (Yearly):** Removes metal to create a new edge. requires whetstone or professional service.
*   **Cleaning:** NEVER put a chef's knife in the dishwasher. The heat destroys the handle and dulls the edge. Wash by hand, dry immediately.
