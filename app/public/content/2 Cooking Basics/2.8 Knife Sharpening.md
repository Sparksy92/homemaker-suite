# Knife Sharpening: The Whetstone

A dull knife is a dangerous knife. You have to push harder, which leads to slips.

## The Equipment
*   **Whetstone**: A double-sided stone. 1000 grit (coarse) to sharpen, 6000 grit (fine) to polish.
*   **Honing Rod**: The metal stick. This does *not* sharpen; it just realigns the edge between sharpenings.

## The Technique
1.  **Soak**: Soak stone in water for 10 mins.
2.  **Angle**: Hold knife at 20 degrees. (Imagine a matchbook under the spine).
3.  **Stroke**: Slide blade across stone, leading with the edge, like you are trying to slice a thin layer off the top of the stone.
4.  **Pressure**: Push down on the away stroke, release pressure on the return stroke.
5.  **The Burr**: Feel the edge. You should feel a tiny wire of metal hanging off the other side. This means you have ground the metal down enough.
6.  **Flip**: Repeat on other side.
7.  **Polish**: Switch to fine side and repeat.

## The Paper Test
Hold a sheet of paper. Slice down. If it catches or tears, keep sharpening. If it sings, you're done.
