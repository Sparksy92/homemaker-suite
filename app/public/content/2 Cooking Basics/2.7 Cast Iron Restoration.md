# Cast Iron Restoration: Fixing the Rust

Found a rusty skillet at a yard sale? Don't throw it away.

## 1. Stripping ( The Reset Button)
If the seasoning is flaky or the pan is rusty, you need to strip it to bare metal.
*   **The Oven Method**: Put it in the oven on the "Self-Cleaning" cycle. It will turn everything to gray ash.
*   **The Lye Tank**: For serious collectors. Soak in a lye bath (Purple Power or pure Lye) for days.

## 2. Rust Removal
*   **Vinegar Bath**: Mix 50/50 Water and Vinegar. Soak pan for 30 minutes (NO LONGER, or it eats the metal).
*   **Scrub**: Scrub with steel wool until silver.
*   **Wash**: Wash with cold water and dry *immediately* with a towel.

## 3. Seasoning (The Protection)
Seasoning is polymerized fat. It turns into a non-stick plastic-like coating.
1.  **Warm**: Warm the pan in oven at 200°F (opens pores).
2.  **Oil**: Rub with Crisco or Grapeseed Oil. Wipe it *all* off. Like seriously, try to wipe it all off until it looks dry.
3.  **Bake**: Bake upside down at 450°F for 1 hour.
4.  **Repeat**: Do this 3 times minimum.
