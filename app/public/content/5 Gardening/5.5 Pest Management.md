# 5.5 Pest Management: Integrated Defense

## Philosophy
In an organic garden, seeing *some* bugs is good. It means you have an ecosystem. If you kill everything, you kill the pollinators and the predators.

## Identification
*   **The Bad Guys:** Aphids (green lice), Hornworms (tomato eaters), Squash Bugs (armor plated), Slugs.
*   **The Good Guys:** Ladybugs (eat aphids), Praying Mantis, Lacewings, Braconid Wasps (parasitize hornworms).

## Defense Tiers

### Tier 1: Prevention (Physical)
*   **Row Covers:** Lightweight fabric that lets light in but keeps moths out (great for cabbage/broccoli).
*   **Collars:** Cardboard tubes around seedling stems block cutworms.
*   **Mulch:** Keeps soil-borne diseases from splashing onto lower leaves.

### Tier 2: Manual Removal
*   **Hand Picking:** Pick hornworms/beetles off and drop in soapy water. Best done in early morning.
*   **Water Blast:** Spray aphids off with a hose jet.

### Tier 3: Organic Chemical
*   **Neem Oil:** Disrupts life cycle. (Don't spray in sun - burns leaves).
*   **Bt (Bacillus thuringiensis):** Natural bacteria that kills caterpillars but hurts nothing else.
*   **Diatomaceous Earth (DE):** Microscopic sharp powder. Kills slugs/ants. Must be dry to work.

## The 10% Rule
If insect damage is less than 10% of the plant, leave it alone. The plant can handle it.

## Beneficial Habitats
Attract the "good guys" so they do the work for you.
*   **Ladybug Lodges:** Leave some hollow stems (like sunflower stalks) in the garden for ladybugs to overwinter.
*   **Water Stations:** A shallow dish of water with pebbles (for landing) attracts predatory wasps and bees.

## Kitchen Pest Remedies
Before buying organic chemicals, try these home recipes:
*   **Garlic/Pepper Spray:** Blend 2 bulbs of garlic and 2 hot peppers with water. Strain and spray on leaves. Deters mammals and soft-bodied insects.
*   **Milk Spray:** Mix 1 part milk to 9 parts water. Spray on squash/cucumbers to prevent powdery mildew (fungus).
*   **Beer Trap:** A shallow tin of beer buried level with the soil. Slugs crawl in and drown.
