# Growing Carrots: The Sweets of Winter

Carrots are sweeter after a frost.

## The Secret: Soil Texture
If your soil has rocks or clay clumps, carrots will fork and twist.
*   **Prep**: Loosen soil 12 inches deep. Add sand if heavy clay.
*   **No Nitrogen**: Too much manure makes hairy roots.

## Germination Trick (The Board Method)
Carrot seeds dry out instantly and die. They need constant moisture for 14 days to sprout.
1.  Sow seeds on surface. Press in gently.
2.  Water well.
3.  **Cover**: Lay a wooden board or cardboard piece directly over the row.
4.  **Check**: Check daily. Remove board *immediately* when you see green specks.

## Thinning
The hardest part for new gardeners. You MUST kill baby carrots.
*   When 2" tall, thin to 3 inches apart. If you don't, you will get zero carrots.

## Overwintering
In many climates, you can leave carrots in the ground all winter. Just cover with a thick bale of straw. Dig them up as needed.
