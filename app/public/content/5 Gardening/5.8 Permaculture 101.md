# Permaculture 101: Working with Nature

Permaculture (Permanent Agriculture) is a design system. It's about setting things up so nature does the work for you.

## Core Ethics
1.  **Earth Care**
2.  **People Care**
3.  **Return of Surplus**

## Key Tactics

### 1. Zones of Use
Design your property by how often you visit things.
*   **Zone 0**: The House.
*   **Zone 1**: Kitchen garden, herbs, compost (Visited Daily).
*   **Zone 2**: Chickens, berry bushes, main crops (Visited Weekly).
*   **Zone 3**: Orchards, pasture (Visited Monthly).
*   **Zone 4**: Woodlot, foraging.
*   **Zone 5**: Wilderness (No human intervention).

### 2. Guilds (The Apple Tree Example)
In nature, plants don't grow in rows. They grow in communities.
Instead of just an apple tree in a grass lawn, plant a "Guild":
*   **Central Element**: Apple Tree.
*   **Fertilizer**: Comfrey or Clover (Nitrogen fixers) planted at the base.
*   **Mulch**: Rhubarb (Giant leaves shade out grass/weeds).
*   **Pollinator**: Dill or Fennel to attract bees.
*   **Suppressor**: Chives/Garlic/Daffodils (Bulbs) repel deer and voles.

### 3. Swales
Ditches dug on contour (level lines) to stop water from running off the hill. They catch water and force it to sink into the ground, watering trees planted on the mound downhill.

### 4. Stacked Functions
Every element should perform 3 functions.
*   *Example*: Chickens.
    1.  Provide Eggs.
    2.  Provide Meat.
    3.  Scratch/till the garden.
    4.  Manure fertilizes soil.
    5.  Eat pests.
