# Fruit Trees: The Long Game

"The best time to plant a tree was 10 years ago. The second best time is today."

## Planting
*   **The Hole**: Dig a wide hole (twice the root ball width), but NOT deep. The root flare (where trunk meets roots) must be visible above ground. If you bury the trunk, it rots.
*   **Water**: 5 gallons a week for the first year.

## Pruning (Winter Task)
Prune when dormant (Jan/Feb).
*   **The Goal**: Airflow and light.
*   **The 3 Ds**: Remove Dead, Damaged, and Diseased wood first.
*   **Crossing Branches**: If two branches rub, cut one.
*   **Inward Growth**: Cut branches growing *into* the center of the tree. You want a "goblet" or "vase" shape open to the sun.

## Thinning Fruit
It hurts, but you must do it.
In June, if a branch has 100 baby apples, pick off 80 of them.
*   Leave 1 fruit every 6 inches.
*   Why? If you don't, the branch will snap, the fruit will be tiny, and the tree will go "biennial" (producing zero fruit next year to recover).

## Pests
*   **Voles**: Chew bark at base in winter. Put a hardware cloth cylinder around the trunk.
*   **Deer**: Eat tips. requires 8ft fence or individual cages.
