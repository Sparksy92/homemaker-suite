# 5.8 Companion Planting Master List

Companion planting is more than just placing plants together; it is about creating a symbiotic ecosystem where plants protect, nourish, and support one another.

## The Companion Matrix

| Crop | Best Friends (Companions) | Rivals (Avoid) | Key Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tomato** | Basil, Onions, Carrots, Marigolds | Corn, Potatoes | Basil improves flavor; Marigolds deter hornworms. |
| **Potato** | Beans, Corn, Garlic, Alyssum | Tomatoes, Cucumbers, Raspberries | Beans fix nitrogen; Alyssum attracts beneficial insects. |
| **Carrot** | Tomatoes, Onions, Leeks, Rosemary | Dill, Fennel | Onions mask carrot scent from carrot flies. |
| **Cucumber** | Beans, Corn, Nasturtiums, Radish | Sage, Potatoes | Nasturtiums act as a trap crop for aphids. |
| **Beans** | Corn, Squash, Potatoes, Savory | Onions, Garlic | Classic "Three Sisters" member; fixes soil nitrogen. |
| **Brassicas** | Dill, Mint, Sage, Rosemary, Celery | Strawberries, Tomatoes | Herbs mask scent from cabbage moths. |

## Trap Cropping: The Sacrificial Strategy
Don't just fight pests—redirect them. Use "Trap Crops" to lure pests away from your main harvest.
*   **Nasturtiums:** Plant on the perimeter to attract aphids away from your beans/tomatoes.
*   **Radishes:** Plant around squash to lure cucumber beetles.
*   **Blue Hubbard Squash:** Known as the ultimate trap crop for vine borers; plant it 10 feet away from your "main" zucchini/summer squash.

## Nitrogen Fixers
Legumes (Beans, Peas, Clover) have a unique relationship with soil bacteria that allows them to "fix" nitrogen from the air into the soil.
*   **Tip:** When your peas or beans are done producing, **do not pull the roots**. Cut the plant at the surface and leave the roots to decay; this releases the stored nitrogen for the next crop (like heavy-feeding corn or leafy greens).

## Aromatic Confusion
Strongly scented herbs like Rosemary, Sage, and Mint confuse the sensory organs of many flying pests. Use them as "living mulch" or borders around your vegetable beds to create an invisible shield of scent.
