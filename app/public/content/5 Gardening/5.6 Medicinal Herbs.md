# Medicinal Herbs: The Pharmacy Garden

Growing your own medicine makes you independent of the pharmacy for minor ailments. Use these plants for teas, salves, and tinctures.

## The Starter Kit (Easiest to Grow)

### 1. Calendula (*Calendula officinalis*)
*   **Use**: Skin healer. Rashes, cuts, burns.
*   **Grow**: Annual. Easy from seed. Reseeds itself.
*   **Preparation**: Drying flowers. Infuse in oil (olive oil) for 4 weeks. Use oil to make Salve (oil + beeswax).

### 2. Peppermint (*Mentha piperita*)
*   **Use**: Digestion, headaches, cooling.
*   **Grow**: Perennial. **INVASIVE** - always plant in a contained pot or deep bed.
*   **Preparation**: Dried leaves for tea. Tincture (fresh leaves in vodka) for stomach aches.

### 3. Echinacea (*Echinacea purpurea*)
*   **Use**: Immune support at first sign of illness.
*   **Grow**: Perennial coneflower. Beautiful and hardy.
*   **Preparation**: The **Root** is the most potent part. Harvest 2-3 year old roots in fall. Tincture in high-proof alcohol.

### 4. Yarrow (*Achillea millefolium*)
*   **Use**: "The Styptic Plant" - stops bleeding. Fevers.
*   **Grow**: Wild perennial. Drought tolerant.
*   **Preparation**: Powdered dried leaves packed into cuts to stop bleeding. Tea for breaking fevers.

### 5. Lemon Balm (*Melissa officinalis*)
*   **Use**: Anti-anxiety, calming, sleep, cold sores (antiviral).
*   **Grow**: Perennial mint family. Spreads but less aggressive than peppermint.
*   **Preparation**: Fresh tea (drying loses some volatile oils). Glycerite or tincture for calming nerves.

## Making a Tincture (Simpler's Method)
1.  Fill clean jar 3/4 full with fresh chopped herb (or 1/2 full dry herb).
2.  Cover with 80-100 proof Vodka.
3.  Shake daily for 4-6 weeks.
4.  Strain and bottle. Shelf life: 2+ years.
