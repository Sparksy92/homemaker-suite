# 5.4 Composting: Turning Trash to Gold

## The Recipe: Greens + Browns + Air + Water

### Greens (Nitrogen)
*   Kitchen scraps (Fruit/Veg peels).
*   Coffee grounds.
*   Cost grass clippings.
*   Manure (Chicken/Cow/Horse - *Herbivores only*).

### Browns (Carbon)
*   Dried leaves.
*   Straw / Hay.
*   Cardboard / Newspaper (shredded).
*   Sawdust.

### The Ratio
*   Aim for **2 parts Brown : 1 part Green**.
*   *Too much Green:* Smells like sewage (slime). Add Browns.
*   *Too much Brown:* Nothing happens. Add Greens/Water.

## What NOT to Compost
*   Meat / Bones / Dairy (Attracts rats).
*   Dog/Cat waste (Pathogens).
*   Diseased plants (Tomatoes with blight).
*   Weeds with seeds heads.

## Methods
1.  **Hot Pile:** Turn/Aerated weekly. Gets to 140°F. Kills seeds. Ready in 3 months.
2.  **Cold Pile:** Dump and forget. Ready in 1 year.
3.  **Vermiculture (Worm Bin):** Red Wigglers in a tote bin indoors. Eat kitchen scraps. Make "Black Gold" castings.

## Troubleshooting
*   **Smells Bad:** Turn it (needs air) + Add Cardboard.
*   **Dry/Ants:** Water it (should feel like a wrung-out sponge).

## Homesteader Tip: Trench Composting
No room for a pile? Dig a 1ft deep trench in your garden path or between rows. Bury your kitchen scraps directly and cover with soil. The worms will do the work in-place, and you don't have to turn anything.
