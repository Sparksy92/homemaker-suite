# Growing Potatoes: Buried Treasure

Potatoes are calorie-dense and easy to store.

## Seed Potatoes
Don't use grocery store potatoes (sprayed with inhibitors). Buy certified "Seed Potatoes".
*   **Chitting**: Letting eyes sprout before planting (green/purple nubs).
*   **Cutting**: If large, cut into chunks with at least 2 eyes each. Let the cut scab over for a day before planting.

## Method 1: The Trench (Traditional)
1.  Dig a trench 6 inches deep.
2.  Place potatoes 12 inches apart.
3.  Cover with 4 inches of soil.
4.  **Hilling**: As the green plant grows up, keep burying the bottom half of the stem with more soil. Potatoes form along the buried stem.

## Method 2: The Tower/Bag (Space Saver)
1.  Roll down the rim of a grow bag or use a wire cage.
2.  Put 4 inches of soil in bottom. Place 3 potatoes. Cover.
3.  As plants grow, unroll bag and add more dirt/straw until full.
4.  Harvest: Dump the bag! No digging required.

## Harvesting & Curing
*   **New Potatoes**: Harvest anytime after flowering. Thin skin. Eat immediately.
*   **Storage Potatoes**: Wait until tops die back completely. Dig up. Do NOT wash. Let sit in dark dry place for 2 weeks to toughen skin.
