# 5.1 Garden Planning: Growing with Intent

## Site Selection
*   **Sun:** You need 6-8+ hours of direct sun for "fruit" crops (tomatoes, peppers, squash). Leafy greens can handle partial shade.
*   **Water:** Must be close to a hose/spigot. If you have to carry buckets, you will quit.
*   **Drainage:** Avoid swampy low spots.

## Bed Styles
1.  **In-Ground:** Cheapest. Till the grass, amend soil. Harder to weed.
2.  **Raised Beds:** Easier on back, better drainage, warms up faster in spring. Expensive to fill.
3.  **Containers:** Best for decks. Pots dry out fast—need daily watering.

## Know Your Zone
*   **Hardiness Zone:** Tells you what survives winter (Perennials).
*   **Frost Dates:** Determines your growing season (Annuals).
    *   *Last Frost:* When to plant.
    *   *First Frost:* When production ends.

## Crop Mapping
*   **Tall North, Short South:** Plant corn/trellises on the North side of the garden so they don't shade out smaller plants.
*   **Companions:**
    *   *Three Sisters:* Corn (support), Beans (Nitrogen), Squash (Ground cover).
    *   *Basil & Tomato:* Improves flavor, repels pests.
    *   *Marigolds:* Plant everywhere to deter bunnies/insects.

## Succession Planting
Don't plant 50 lettuces at once. Plant 5 every 2 weeks. This gives you a continuous harvest instead of a glut.
