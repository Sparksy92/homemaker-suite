# Onions & Garlic: The Kitchen Staples

## Garlic (Plant in Fall!)
Garlic is planted in Autumn (Halloween) for harvest next Summer.
1.  **Separate**: Break bulb into distinct cloves. Keep wrapper on.
2.  **Plant**: Pointy side UP. 2 inches deep. 6 inches apart.
3.  **Mulch**: Cover with 4-6 inches of straw/leaves for winter protection.
4.  **Scapes**: In June, hardneck garlic shoots up a curly flower stalk ("shape"). Cut this off to make the bulb bigger (and eat the scape like a scallion!).
5.  **Harvest**: When bottom 3 leaves die.

## Onions (The Day Length Game)
Onion bulbing is triggered by day length.
*   **Long Day**: North (US/Canada). Plant in spring.
*   **Short Day**: South. Plant in fall.
*   **Intermediate Day**: Middle ground.

### Sets vs. Starts
*   **Sets**: Those little dry bulbs in mesh bags. Fast, but prone to bolting (flowering). Good for green onions.
*   **Starts (Transplants)**: Little green plants. Better success rate for big storage onions.

## Curing
Lay out in a dry, shady, airy spot (on a screen) for 2-3 weeks until necks are tight and papery. braided strings look cool, but mesh bags breathe better.
