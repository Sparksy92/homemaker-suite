# Growing Tomatoes: The Homestead King

Tomatoes are the most popular garden crop for a reason: the store-bought ones taste like cardboard.

## Varieties
*   **Determinate (Bush)**: Grow to specific height, fruit all at once. Best for canning/sauce. (Examples: Roma, San Marzano).
*   **Indeterminate (Vining)**: Grow forever, fruit until frost. Best for fresh eating. (Examples: Brandywine, Cherokee Purple, Cherry).

## Planting
*   **Deep Planting**: Bury 2/3 of the stem! Tomatoes grow roots from their stems. This creates a massive root system.
*   **Spacing**: 24 inches apart. Airflow prevents blight.

## Pruning (Indeterminate Only)
*   **Suckers**: The little shoots growing in the "armpit" between the main stem and a leaf branch. Pinch them off. This forces energy into fruit, not leaves.
*   **Bottom Pruning**: Remove all leaves touching the ground to stop soil-borne disease splashing up.

## Common Issues
*   **Blossom End Rot**: Black scabs on the bottom of the fruit. Caused by Calcium deficiency (usually due to inconsistent watering). Fix: Water consistently. Mulch heavily.
*   **Hornworms**: Giant green caterpillars. Pick off by hand. If they have white eggs on their back, leave them! Parasitic wasps have killed them for you.

> [!TIP]
> **Companion Planting**: Plant Basil and Marigolds with tomatoes. Basil improves flavor; Marigolds repel nematodes.
