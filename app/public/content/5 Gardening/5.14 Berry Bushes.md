# Berry Bushes: Perennial Sweets

Plant once, eat for 20 years.

## Blueberries (The Acid Lovers)
Blueberries **require** acidic soil (pH 4.5-5.5). They will die in normal garden soil.
*   **Fix**: Plant in pure peat moss or add elemental sulfur a year ahead. Mulch with pine needles.
*   **Pollination**: You need at least 2 different varieties for good fruit set.

## Raspberries / Blackberries (The Canes)
Grow on canes.
*   **Primocane**: First year growth (Green).
*   **Floricane**: Second year growth (Woody).

### Pruning (The essential yearly task)
1.  **Summer Bearing**: Fruit on Floricanes (2nd year wood). Cut the woody canes to the ground *after* harvest. Leave the green ones for next year.
2.  **Ever-Bearing**: Fruit on tips of Primocanes (fall) AND Floricanes (spring). Mow them all down in winter for one big fall crop (easiest method).

## Strawberries
*   **June-Bearing**: One massive crop in early summer. Best for jam making.
*   **Ever-Bearing**: Trickle of berries all season. Best for snacking.
*   **Runners**: Plants send out "babies" on strings. Pin them down to make new plants, or cut them off to focus energy on fruit.
