# Mushroom Cultivation: Fungi Farming

Mushrooms are decomposers. They turn waste (wood, straw) into food.

## 1. Log Cultivation (Shiitake)
*   **Best For**: Long-term harvest (logs produce for 3-5 years).
*   **Wood**: Freshly cut Oak or Maple (cut in late winter).
*   **Process**:
    1.  **Drill**: Drill holes in the log in a diamond pattern.
    2.  **Inoculate**: Hammer in "Spawn Plugs" (wooden dowels covered in mycelium).
    3.  **Seal**: Cover holes with hot wax to protect from drying/insects.
    4.  **Wait**: Stack logs in the shade (the "Laying Yard"). Wait 6-12 months for spawn to colonize ("spawn run").
    5.  **Shock**: Soak logs in cold water 24 hours to trigger fruiting. Harvest!

## 2. Bucket Tek (Oyster Mushrooms)
*   **Best For**: Fast, indoor growing.
*   **Substrate**: Chopped straw or wood chips.
*   **Process**:
    1.  **Pasteurize**: Soak straw in hot water (160°F) for an hour, or lime water bath, to kill competitor molds.
    2.  **Layer**: Drill holes in a 5-gallon bucket. Layer straw and Grain Spawn (mycelium on grain).
    3.  **Incubate**: Keep bucket in dark for 2 weeks.
    4.  **Fruit**: Move to humid spot. Mushrooms pop out of the holes.

## 3. Wine Cap (Garden Giants)
*   **Process**: The easiest! Just mix sawdust spawn into a bed of woodchip mulch in a shady part of your garden. They will pop up every spring and fall.
