# Beekeeping: Sweet Rewards

Beekeeping (Apiculture) provides honey, beeswax, propolis, and pollination for your garden.

## 1. The Hive (Langstroth)
The standard stacking box system.
*   **Bottom Board**: The base.
*   **Deep Supers**: The bottom 1-2 boxes where the brood (babies) lives.
*   **Honey Supers**: The upper boxes where they store excess honey for you.
*   **Frames**: Removable rectangles where bees build comb.

## 2. Gear
*   **Veil/Suit**: Protection is key for confidence.
*   **Smoker**: Cool smoke calms bees by masking alarm pheromones.
*   **Hive Tool**: A pry bar to separate sticky frames.

## 3. The Bees
*   **Queen**: One per hive. Lays 2000 eggs/day.
*   **Workers**: Infertile females. They do everything (guard, clean, forage, make honey).
*   **Drones**: Males. Their only job is to mate with a queen. They are kicked out in winter.

## 4. The Year
*   **Spring**: Population explosion. Prevent "swarming" (when half the hive leaves) by adding space.
*   **Summer**: The "Flow". Bees are gathering nectar.
*   **Fall**: Harvest honey. Treat for Varroa mites. Feed sugar syrup if they are light on stores.
*   **Winter**: Survival mode. Do not open the hive.

## 5. First Year Expectations
*   Do not expect honey the first year. They need to build wax comb (which takes energy) and store food for their own winter survival.
