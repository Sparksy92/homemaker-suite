# Backyard Chickens: The Gateway Livestock

Chickens are the engine of the homestead. They turn kitchen scraps and bugs into high-quality protein (eggs/meat) and nitrogen-rich fertilizer (manure).

## 1. The Setup
Before you get chicks, you need a home for them.
*   **The Coop**: Needs 4 sq ft per bird. Must be dry, draft-free, and predator-proof.
    *   *Roosts*: rounded 2x4s for sleeping.
    *   *Nesting Boxes*: 1 box for every 4 hens.
*   **The Run**: 10 sq ft per bird outdoor space. Enclosed with hardware cloth (not chicken wire—raccoons can tear chicken wire).

## 2. Choosing Breeds
*   **Layers**:
    *   *Rhode Island Red*: Production powerhouse (brown eggs).
    *   *Leghorn*: High volume white eggs (flighty bird).
    *   *Easter Egger*: Friendly, colored eggs (green/blue).
*   **Meat Birds**:
    *   *Cornish Cross*: Ready to harvest in 8-10 weeks. Fast growing.
*   **Dual Purpose**:
    *   *Oprington, Plymouth Rock*: Good layers, decent meat size.

## 3. Feeding & Water
*   **Water**: Chickens die without water very quickly. Nipple waterers keep water clean.
*   **Feed**:
    *   *Starter*: High protein (18-20%) for first 8 weeks.
    *   *Layer*: Calcium-fortified (16%) for laying hens.
    *   *Grit*: Necessary for digestion if they aren't free-ranging.
    *   *Calcium*: Offer crushed oyster shell on the side for strong eggshells.

## 4. Daily Chore Routine
*   **Morning**: Open coop, check water, toss scratch grains (corn/seeds) to get them moving.
*   **Evening**: Collect eggs, lock coop door (CRITICAL for predator safety).

## 5. Health Basics
*   **Dust Baths**: Chickens bathe in dirt to kill mites. Provide a dry spot with sand/ash.
*   **Mites/Lice**: Check vent area. Treat with diatomaceous earth or permethrin if needed.
*   **Egg Bound**: If a hen is straining and unhappy, she might have a stuck egg. Warm bath and calcium can help.

> [!NOTE]
> **Egg Washing**: Fresh eggs have a "bloom" that seals them. You can leave unwashed eggs on the counter for weeks. Once washed, they MUST be refrigerated.
