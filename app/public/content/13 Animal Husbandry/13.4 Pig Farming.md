# 13.4 Pig Farming: The Calorie King

Chickens give you eggs. Cows give you milk. But for pure **calories per month** and waste recycling, the pig is the survival king.

## 1. The Converter
A pig is a machine that turns "trash" into bacon.
*   **Diet**: They eat everything. Spoiled garden veg, kitchen scraps, acorns (mast), whey from cheese making, skim milk.
*   **Efficiency**: Pigs convert feed to meat at a 3:1 ratio (Cows are 7:1).

## 2. Breeds
*   **Berkshire**: Famous for quality meat (marbling). Hardy.
*   **Tamworth**: The "Bacon Pig". Long and lean. Good foragers.
*   **Kune Kune**: Smaller, grazing pigs. Good if you have grass but less grain.

## 3. Housing
*   **Fencing**: "Pig tight" means a solid physical barrier + ELECTRICITY. Pigs are smart and strong. A single strand of electric wire at nose-height is essential.
*   **Shelter**: They need shade (they don't sweat) and a wind-break.
*   **Padding**: Deep bedding (straw/leaves) in winter.

## 4. The Harvest
*   **Timing**: Raise spring piglets (weaners) to harvest in late fall (250 lbs).
*   **Butchery (Scalding vs Skinning)**:
    *   *Scalding*: Dip into 145°F water to loosen hair, then scrape. Keeps the skin (cracklins!) on the fat.
    *   *Skinning*: Easier without specialized equipment. Like skinning a deer.
*   **Yield**: A 250lb pig yields ~150-180lbs of meat/fat.

> [!TIP]
> **Lard**: Do not throw away the fat. Rendered lard is high-energy survival fuel, cooking oil, and can be used for soap or machinery grease.
