# Raising Rabbits: The Silent Laundry

Rabbits are the most efficient meat source on a homestead. They are quiet, reproduce quickly, and their manure is "cold" (can go straight into the garden without composting).

## 1. Housing (The Hutch)
*   **Space**: Each breeder needs a cage at least 30" x 36".
*   **Wire Floor**: 1/2" x 1" wire mesh allows droppings to fall through.
*   **Protection**: Must be wind/rain proof. Rabbits tolerate cold well, but heat kills them. Shade is mandatory.

## 2. Breeds
*   **New Zealand White**: The commercial standard. Fast growth, good meat-to-bone ratio.
*   **Californian**: Another excellent meat breed.
*   **Rex**: Good meat, but prized for their incredible velvet fur.

## 3. Feeding
*   **Pellets**: Commercial rations ensure balanced nutrition (16-18% protein).
*   **Hay**: Unlimited Timothy hay is crucial for digestion.
*   **Greens**: Treats only. Too many greens cause diarrhea.

## 4. Breeding Cycle
*   **Gestation**: 31 days.
*   **Kindling**: The act of giving birth. Provide a "nest box" with straw 3 days before due date. The doe will pull fur to line it.
*   **Weaning**: 6-8 weeks.
*   **Harvest**: 10-12 weeks (Fryers).

## 5. Processing Basics
*   **Cervical Dislocation**: The most humane instant kill method (Hopper Popper).
*   **Skinning**: Rabbit skin comes off like a sweater.
*   **Yield**: 50-60% dress-out weight.

> [!TIP]
> **Rabbit Starvation**: Rabbit meat is very lean. You cannot live on rabbit alone; you will get protein poisoning. You must eat it with fats (butter, oil, or carbs).
