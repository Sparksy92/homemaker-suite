# Scrap Metal Scavenging: The Junk Hunter

The world is full of high-quality steel lying in ditches.

## The Spark Test
How to know what steel you have? Touch it to a grinder.
*   **High Carbon (Hard)**: Bright, exploding sparks (Bushy). *Use for Knives/Tools.*
*   **Mild Steel (Soft)**: Dull, long sparks (Few branches). *Use for Tongs/Hooks.*

## Best Sources
1.  **Leaf Springs (Old Trucks)**: Excellent High Carbon steel (5160). Perfect for knives.
2.  **Coil Springs**: Good High Carbon.
3.  **Files/Rasps**: Very hard steel (1095). Brittle but holds an amazing edge.
4.  **Rebar**: Junk mild steel. Good for practice hooks, but will never hold an edge.
5.  **Railroad Spikes**: Usually medium-carbon. Okay for hatchets, bad for fine knives.

## Danger
**NEVER** forge Galvanized Steel (Silver/coated). It releases Zinc fumes when heated. "Metal Fume Fever" feels like the flu but can kill you.
