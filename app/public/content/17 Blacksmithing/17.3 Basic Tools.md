# Basic Tools: The First Projects

A blacksmith makes their own tools.

## 1. S Hooks
The classic beginner project. Teaches:
*   **Draw out**: Hammering the tip to a point.
*   **Bend**: Curling around the horn of the anvil.
*   **Twist**: Twisting the middle for decoration.

## 2. Tongs
You need tongs to hold hot metal.
1.  Take two pieces of rebar.
2.  Flatten the middle of each (the hinge).
3.  Punch a hole.
4.  Rivet them together.
5.  Shape the jaws to hold your stock.

## 3. The Knife
1.  **Flatten**: Hammer a coil spring flat.
2.  **Point**: Hammer the tip down (don't grind it).
3.  **Bevel**: Hammer the edge thin.
4.  **Quench**: Heat to non-magnetic (cherry red). Dunk in oil. (It screams).
5.  **Temper**: Bake in oven at 400°F for 2 hours to soften it slightly so it doesn't shatter.
