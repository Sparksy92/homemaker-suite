# The Primitive Forge: Moving from Stone to Iron

To work metal, you need heat (Fuel + Air) and an anvil.

## The Fuel
*   **Coal**: The best. Burns hot (3000°F).
*   **Charcoal**: Works great. Make your own in a bonfire. Do NOT use "Briquttes" (BBQ coal); they have clay binders that clog the fire. You need "Lump Charcoal".

## The Forge (The Pot)
1.  **Hole in the Ground**: Dig a hole. Line with clay.
2.  **Air Supply (Tuyere)**: Bury a metal pipe (steel, not galvanized) leading into the bottom of the hole.
3.  **Bellows**: Connect a hair dryer (if power), or build a "Box Bellows" from wood/leather to pump air.
4.  **Operation**: Fill hole with charcoal. Light it. Pump air. It will roar like a jet engine.

## The Anvil
You don't need a cartoon anvil.
*   **Railroad Track**: A heavy piece of track is perfect.
*   **Sledgehammer Head**: Mount a heavy sledgehammer head in a log.
*   **Big Rock**: A flat, smooth river granite stone (temporary).
