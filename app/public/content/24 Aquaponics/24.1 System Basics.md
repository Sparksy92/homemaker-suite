# Aquaponics: Fish + Plants

The ultimate closed loop.

## The Cycle
1.  **Fish**: Eat food, poop Ammonia.
2.  **Bacteria (Nitrosomonas)**: Turn Ammonia into Nitrites.
3.  **Bacteria (Nitrobacter)**: Turn Nitrites into Nitrates.
4.  **Plants**: Eat Nitrates (Clean the water).
5.  **Water**: Returns to fish.

## System Types
*   **Media Bed**: Grow plants in gravel. Simple. Acts as the bio-filter.
*   **Deep Water Culture (DWC)**: Rafts floating on water. Best for lettuce/basil.
*   **NFT**: Pipes with thin film of water.

## Fish Choice
*   **Tilapia**: Hardy. Eat anything. Warm water only (need heaters).
*   **Bluegill/Catfish**: Native. Cold water tolerant. Grow slower.
