# Ash Soap: Cleanliness from Fire

Our ancestors made soap from the fireplace and the bacon grease jar.

## 1. Make Lye (Potassium Hydroxide)
**DANGER**: Lye burns skin and blinds eyes. Wear goggles.
1.  Drill holes in bottom of a plastic bucket. Line with grass.
2.  Fill with white Hardwood Ash.
3.  Pour rain water over ash.
4.  Collect the brown liquid that drips out. This is liquid Lye.
5.  **Test**: Float an egg in it. If a quarter-sized spot of egg shows above surface, it's strong enough.

## 2. Render Fat (Tallow/Lard)
boil animal fat in water. Cool. The clean white wax floats to top.

## 3. Saponification
1.  Heat fat.
2.  Slowly stir in Lye water.
3.  Stir until "Trace" (like pudding).
4.  Pour into molds.
5.  **Cure**: Cut into bars. Let sit 6 weeks. The lye eats the fat and turns into salt (Soap). If you use it too soon, it will burn your skin.
