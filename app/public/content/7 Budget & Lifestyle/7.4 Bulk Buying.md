# Bulk Buying: The Restaurant Store

Stop buying 5lb bags of flour.

## Where to go
*   **Restaurant Supply Stores** (ChefStore, Restaurant Depot): Usually open to public.
*   **Amish/Mennonite Bulk Stores**: Great for spices.

## What to buy
*   **Flour**: 50lb bag ($20 vs $50 at grocery). Store in food-grade buckets (Gamma Lids).
*   **Rice/Oats**: 25lb bags. Freeze for 48h to kill weevil eggs, then bucket.
*   **Spices**: Buy by the pound, refill small jars. Cumin is pennies per oz this way.
*   **Yeast**: 1lb vacuum brick ($4) vs tiny packets ($2 ea). Keep in freezer.

## What NOT to buy
*   **Oil**: Goes rancid before you can use 5 gallons.
*   **Nuts**: Go rancid quickly.
*   **Produce**: Unless you are canning it *today*.
