# 7.2 Frugal Living: Rich Life, Low Cost

## Entertainment
*   **The Library:** It's not just books. Movies, video games, tool rentals, museum passes, seeds. FREE.
*   **The Potluck:** Hosting dinner > Going to restaurant. Challenge friends to bring a dish.
*   **Nature:** Hiking/Foraging is free. Gym memberships are expensive.

## Clothing
*   **Material:** Buy natural fibers (Cotton, Wool, Linen). They last longer and don't hold odors like polyester.
*   **Mending:** Learn to sew a button and darn a sock.
*   **Thrift:** Buy high-end brands used. Quality persists.
*   **Capsule Wardrobe:** Own fewer clothes that all match. Reduces decision fatigue and laundry.

## Energy Savings
*   **Vampire Power:** Unplug TV/Computer when not in use (or use power strip).
*   **Heating:** Put on a sweater. Turn thermostat down 2 degrees.
*   **Cooking:** Batch cook. If the oven is on for chicken, roast veggies for tomorrow too.

## The "Wait 72 Hours" Rule
*   Impulse to buy something > $50?
*   Wait 3 days.
*   80% of the time, the urge passes.
