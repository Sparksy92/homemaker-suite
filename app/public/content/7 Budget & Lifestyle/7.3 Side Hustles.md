# Side Hustles: The Homestead Economy

Turn hobbies into income.

## The Law
*   **Cottage Food Laws**: Most states let you sell "non-hazardous" foods (bread, jam, cookies) from home without a commercial kitchen. Check your state limits.

## Ideas
1.  **Sourdough Starter**: Dry it. Sell packets on Etsy.
2.  **Seedlings**: Start 100 extra tomatoes in spring. Sell starts for $5/ea on Facebook.
3.  **Eggs**: Put a cooler at end of driveway. "Honor System $5".
4.  **Soap**: High margin gift item.
5.  **Mending**: Hemming pants is easy money.

## Pricing
Don't undercharge.
**(Materials + Time) x 2 = Wholesale Price.**
**Wholesale x 2 = Retail Price.**
If you can't sell it for that, it's a hobby, not a business.
