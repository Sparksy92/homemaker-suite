# Soil Science: N-P-K

Plants eat 3 main things.

## Nitrogen (N) - "Up"
*   **Function**: Leaf growth. Green color.
*   **Deficiency**: Pale yellow leaves. Stunted growth.
*   **Sources**: Blood Meal, Feather Meal, Manure, Urine (10:1 dilution), Legumes.

## Phosphorus (P) - "Down"
*   **Function**: Root growth. Flowers. Seeds.
*   **Deficiency**: Purple leaves. Poor fruit set.
*   **Sources**: Bone Meal, Rock Phosphate.

## Potassium (K) - "All Around"
*   **Function**: General health. Immune system. Water regulation.
*   **Deficiency**: Brown crispy edges on leaves.
*   **Sources**: Wood Ash (Potash), Greensand, Kelp Meal.
