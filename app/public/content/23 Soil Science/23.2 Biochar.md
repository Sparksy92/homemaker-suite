# Biochar: Terra Preta

A 2,000 year old Amazonian technology.

## The Concept
Charcoal is pure carbon. It has massive surface area (micropores). It acts as a "Coral Reef" for soil bacteria and holds onto water/nutrients.

## Making It
1.  **Pyrolysis**: Burn wood with restricted oxygen. (Cone pit method or retort drum).
2.  **Quench**: Stop the fire with water before it turns to ash. You want black, not gray.
3.  **Charge**: Raw biochar sucks nutrients *out* of soil at first. You must "Charge" it.
    *   Soak it in urine/manure tea for 2 weeks.
4.  **Apply**: Mix into soil. It lasts for 1,000 years.
