# IPM: War on Bugs

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) means using brains before poisons.

## 1. Physical Barriers
*   **Kaolin Clay**: Spray tree with white clay. Bugs hate landing on it.
*   **Tree Guards**: Plastic wrap around trunk prevents rabbit girdling.
*   **Sticky Traps**: Yellow cards coated in glue catch flies.

## 2. Biological Warfare
*   **Ladybugs**: Eat Aphids.
*   **Trichogramma Wasps**: Lay eggs inside moth eggs.
*   **Nematodes**: Microscopic worms that eat japanese beetle grubs in the soil.

## 3. Chemical (Organic)
*   **Neem Oil**: Hormone disruptor.
*   **Spinosad**: Bacteria that kills caterpillars.
*   **Dormant Oil**: Suffocates eggs in winter.
