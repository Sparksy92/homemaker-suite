# Pruning: The Art of Subtraction

Pruning hurts the tree in the short term to help it in the long term.

## The Goals
1.  **Airflow**: Prevents fungus.
2.  **Sunlight**: Ripens fruit.
3.  **Structure**: Strong branches to hold heavy loads.

## The shapes
*   **Central Leader**: Like a Christmas tree. One main trunk. Best for Apple, Pear, Pecan.
*   **Open Center**: Like a Vase. No central trunk. Best for Stone Fruit (Peach, Plum, Cherry) because they need more sun.

## The Cuts
*   **Heading Cut**: Cutting the tip of a branch to make it branch out (bushing).
*   **Thinning Cut**: Removing a whole branch at the collar to reduce crowding.
