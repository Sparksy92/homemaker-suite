# Grafting: Cloning Nature

You cannot plant an apple seed and get the same apple. You must graft (clone).

## The Concept
*   **Scion**: The stick with the genetics you want (Granny Smith).
*   **Rootstock**: The root system you want (Dwarf, Cold Hardy).
*   **Cambium**: The green layer under the bark. You must align the cambium of the scion with the cambium of the rootstock.

## Whip and Tongue (The Standard)
1.  **Cut**: Make a diagonal slash on both sticks.
2.  **Tongue**: Cut a vertical slit *into* the slanted face of both sticks.
3.  **Lock**: Slide them together so the tongues interlock.
4.  **Wrap**: Bind tight with grafting tape. Paint with wax to seal in moisture.

## Timing
Graft in **Late Winter** when the trees are dormant but sap is about to flow.
