# Welding: Joining Metal

The ultimate repair skill.

## Stick Welding (SMAW)
The most robust method. Works in wind, on rusty metal, and is simple.
*   **The Machine**: An old "Tombstone" AC/DC welder or even 2 car batteries wired in series (Emergency only).
*   **The Rod**: A metal stick covered in flux.
    *   **6013**: "The Farmer's Rod". Easy to start, looks pretty. Low penetration.
    *   **6011**: "The Rust Rod". Digs deep through paint/rust. Ugly welds but strong. Use this for repairs.
    *   **7018**: "Structural Code". Strongest, but needs clean metal and dry storage.

## Technique
1.  **Scratch Start**: Scratch the rod like a match to strike the arc.
2.  **Puddle**: Watch the molten pool, not the flash.
3.  **Slag**: The flux turns into a glass coating ("Slag"). You must chip it off with a hammer to see the weld. "If it's slag, you drag."
