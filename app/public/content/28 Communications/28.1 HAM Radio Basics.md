# Communications: HAM Radio

When the cell towers fall, the radio waves remain.

## The Baofeng (UV-5R)
The $25 radio that everyone has.
*   **Legal**: You need a Technician License (multiple choice test) to transmit. You can LISTEN for legal.
*   **Range**: Line of sight. 1-5 miles usually.
*   **Repeater**: A tower on a mountain that listens to your weak signal and re-blasts it at high power. Extends range to 50+ miles.

## HF (High Frequency)
The "World Wide" bands. bounces signal off the Ionosphere.
*   **NVIS Antenna**: A wire strung low (10ft) horizontal to ground. Shoots signal straight up. Bounces down. Covers 0-300 miles (Regional) perfectly.
*   **Power**: Can talk around the world on 100 Watts (Lightbulb power).
