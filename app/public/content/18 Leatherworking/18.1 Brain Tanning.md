# Brain Tanning: Spirits of the Deer

"Every animal has enough brains to tan its own hide." (Except buffalo).

## The Chemistry
Raw skin goes rotton (bacteria) or hard (rawhide). Tanning uses emulsified fats to replace the water in the skin fibers, keeping them soft forever.

## The Process
1.  **Flesh**: Scrape *every* bit of meat/fat off the inside.
2.  **Hair**: Soak in wood ash water (Lye) to loosen hair. Scrape hair off (or leave on for furs).
3.  **Brain**: Cook the animal's brain in water. Blend into a smoothie.
4.  **Soak**: Rub the brain-slurry into the hide firmly. Let sit overnight.
5.  **Wring**: Wring it out.
6.  **stretch**: This is the hard part. You must pull and stretch the hide *constantly* while it dries. If you stop, it turns to cardboard. You must stretch it until it is fluffy and white.
7.  **Smoke**: Hang over a smoky (cool) fire. The smoke formaldehyde locks the tan so it stays soft even if it gets wet later.
