# Moccasins: Feet on the Earth

The center-seam moccasin is the simplest pattern.

## The Pattern
1.  Stand on leather. Trace your foot.
2.  Add 2 inches around the perimeter.
3.  Cut out.

## The Stitch
1.  Fold leather in half lengthwise (under your foot).
2.  Sew up the heel.
3.  Sew up the toe (Center seam) until you reach the instep (top of foot).
4.  **Pucker**: The toe needs to be gathered (puckered) slightly to create volume for your toes.

## Sole
Moccasins wear out fast.
*   **Goo**: Coat the bottom in "Shoe Goo" or a mix of tire dust and glue (modern Native trick).
*   **Double Sole**: Sew a thick piece of rawhide on the bottom.
