# Sewing Leather: The Saddle Stitch

You cannot just push a needle through leather.

## Tools
*   **Awl**: A sharp spike to punch holes.
*   **Two Needles**: Blunt harness needles.
*   **Waxed Thread**: Artificial sinew or waxed linen.

## The Saddle Stitch
The strongest stitch on earth. If one thread breaks, the other holds.
1.  Punch a line of holes with fork/awl.
2.  Put a needle on *both ends* of one long thread.
3.  Pass Needle A through Hole 1. Center the thread.
4.  Pass Needle A through Hole 2.
5.  Pass Needle B through Hole 2 (from the other side).
6.  **Pull tight**.
7.  Repeat. You are weaving the needles past each other in every hole.
