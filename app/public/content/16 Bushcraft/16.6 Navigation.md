# Navigation: Finding Critical North

GPS batteries die. The earth does not.

## The Sun (Shadow Stick Method)
1.  **Place a stick** in the ground. Mark the tip of the shadow with a stone.
2.  **Wait 15 minutes**.
3.  **Mark the new tip**.
4.  **Draw a line** between the two stones.
5.  **West-East**: The first mark is West. The second is East. Stand with your left foot on West and right on East. You are facing North.

## The Stars (Northern Hemisphere)
1.  **Find the Big Dipper**: Look for the "ladle".
2.  **The Pointer Stars**: The two stars at the end of the cup part of the ladle.
3.  **Trace a line**: Imagine a line shooting out from those two stars. It points directly to **Polaris** (The North Star).
4.  **Polaris**: It is the end of the Little Dipper's handle. It never moves. It is always True North.

## Moss?
"Moss grows on the North side of trees."
**MYTH**. Moss grows where it is damp. In a dense forest, that could be anywhere. Do not trust moss.
