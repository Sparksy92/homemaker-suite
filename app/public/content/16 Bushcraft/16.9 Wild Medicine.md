# Wild Medicine: The forest pharmacy

## Willow Bark (Nature's Aspirin)
The bark of the White Willow contains *Salicin*, which converts to Salicylic Acid (Aspirin) in the body.
*   **Use**: Pain relief, fever reduction.
*   **Method**: Chew on a clean twig or brew bark tea. Caution: Blood thinner (just like aspirin).

## Plantain (The Green Band-Aid)
Not the banana. The low weed with ribs on the leaves growing in your driveway.
*   **Use**: Bee stings, mosquito bites, cuts.
*   **Method**: "Spit Poultice". Chew the leaf into a green mush. Plaster it directly on the bite. Pain relief is almost instant.

## Yarrow (The Blood Stopper)
Feathery leaves, white umbel flowers.
*   **Use**: Deep cuts.
*   **Method**: Crush leaves/flowers and pack into a bleeding wound to encourage clotting. Used by Roman soldiers ("Herba Militaris").

## Honey
If you have it. It is antibacterial. Put directly on burns or infected cuts.
