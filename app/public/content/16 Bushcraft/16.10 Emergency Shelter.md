# Emergency Shelters: The Debris Hut

If you have no tent and night is falling.

## The Debris Hut (The Squirrel Nest)
It works by trapping your body heat in a small space with massive insulation.
1.  **Ridgepole**: Find a sturdy 8ft stick. Prop one end on a tree stump/rock (waist high).
2.  **Ribs**: Lean smaller sticks against the ridgepole on both sides to make an A-frame tent shape. Leave just enough room for you to crawl inside.
3.  **Insulation**: Pile leaves, grass, pine needles on top.
    *   **Thickness**: You need **3 FEET** of debris. Not 3 inches. 3 feet.
    *   **Waterproofing**: Shingle the top layer with bark or green boughs if raining.
4.  **Bedding**: Stuff the inside with 2 feet of dry leaves. You nestle *into* pile, not on the dirt.

## Snow Trench
If in deep snow.
1.  Dig a trench.
2.  Lay boughs across top.
3.  Cover boughs with snow.
4.  **Cold Sink**: Dig a deeper hole at the door for cold air to settle into, keeping the sleeping shelf warmer.
