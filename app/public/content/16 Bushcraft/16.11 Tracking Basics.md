# Tracking: Reading the News

Animals leave stories in the mud.

## The Sign
*   **Tracks**: Footprints. Sharp edges = Fresh. Crumbling edges = Old.
*   **Scat**: Droppings. Warm? You are close.
*   **Runs**: Compressed grass trails.
*   **Rubbings**: Bark scraped off trees (Deer antlers).

## The Gaits
*   **Diagonal Walkers**: Cat, Dog, Deer. Rear foot lands where front foot was. Saves energy.
*   **Bounders**: Weasels. Front feet land, back feet land behind them.
*   **Gallopers**: Rabbits. Back feet land *in front of* front feet.

## Stalking (The Fox Walk)
To move without sound:
1.  **Land**: Land on the *outside edge* of the ball of your foot.
2.  **Roll**: Roll inward to the ball.
3.  **Feel**: Feel for twigs.
4.  **Heel**: Finally, drop your heel.
Most people walk Heel-Toe (The "Thump"). Animals walk Toe-Heel.
