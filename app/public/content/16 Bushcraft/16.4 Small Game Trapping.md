# Trapping: The Silent Hunter

Hunting requires you to be present. Trapping works while you sleep.

## The Snare (Rabbit/Squirrel)
1.  **Wire**: Beading wire or picture hanging wire is best.
2.  **The Loop**: Make a loop the size of your fist (for rabbit). 4 fingers off the ground.
3.  **Placement**: Look for a "Run" (a beaten path in the grass). Animals are creatures of habit. Place the loop directly in the path.
4.  **Funneling**: Place twigs on either side of the loop to subtly force the animal to go *through* not around.

## The Deadfall (Paiute)
Uses a heavy rock to crush the prey.
*   **Pros**: Lethal instantly. No wire needed.
*   **Cons**: Complex trigger system. Hard to set without smashing your own fingers.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ethics**: Check traps every 24 hours. Never leave a trap set if you move camp.
> **Warning**: Trapping laws vary wildly. This is for survival situations.
