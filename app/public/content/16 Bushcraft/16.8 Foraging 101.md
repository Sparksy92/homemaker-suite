# Foraging 101: The Supermarket of the Swamp

Calorie density is low in plants, but vitamins are high.

## The Big Four (Easy Identification)
1.  **Cattails**: "The Supermarket of the Swamp".
    *   *Spring*: Shoots/Pollen (eating like corn).
    *   *Fall/Winter*: Roots (Starch).
2.  **Dandelions**: Entire plant is edible.
    *   *Leaves*: Salad (bitter).
    *   *Roots*: Roast for "coffee".
3.  **Pine Needles**: Tea. High in Vitamin C (prevents scurvy). White Pine is best (5 needles per bundle). **Avoid Yew** (Red berries = death).
4.  **Acorns**: Must be leached.
    *   *Process*: Shell them. Grind them. Soak in water changes for days until water runs clear (removes tannins). Bake into flour.

## The Universal Edibility Test
**WARNING**: Only use in life-or-death starvation.
1.  **Smell**: Smells like almonds/peaches? Bad (Cyanide).
2.  **Contact**: Rub on wrist. Wait 15 mins. (Reaction?)
3.  **Lip**: Rub on lip. Wait 15 mins. (Burning/Numbing?)
4.  **Taste**: Chew small bit. Spit out. Wait 15 mins.
5.  **Swallow**: Eat tiny bit. Wait 8 hours.
6.  **Eat**: Eat a handful. Wait 8 hours.
**This takes 24 hours just to prove one plant is safe.**
