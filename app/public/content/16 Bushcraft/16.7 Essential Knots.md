# Essential Knots: If you can't tie knots, tie lots.

Wrong. A bad knot kills. You only need 3.

## 1. The Bowline (The King of Knots)
Makes a fixed loop that never slips and never jams. Used for rescue (tying around a waist) or securing a tarp.
*   **Mnemonic**: "The rabbit comes out of the hole, goes around the tree, and back down the hole."

## 2. The Taut-Line Hitch (The Tent Saver)
A slide-and-grip knot. You can slide it up to tighten a tent guy-line, and it holds friction.
*   **Use**: Tents, tarps, clotheslines. Eliminate sagging lines forever.

## 3. The Clove Hitch (The Anchor)
Used to start lashings (tying sticks together).
*   **Method**: Loop over the log. Cross over. Loop under. Tuck the end under the X.
*   **Use**: Starting a tripod for a shelter or pot hanger.
