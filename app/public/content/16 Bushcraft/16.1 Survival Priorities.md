# Survival Priorities: The Rule of 3s

When you are lost or in a survival situation, panic is the killer. Focus on the Rule of 3s to prioritize your actions.

## The Rules
You can survive:
1.  **3 Minutes** without **Air** (or in icy water).
2.  **3 Hours** without **Shelter** (in harsh weather).
3.  **3 Days** without **Water**.
4.  **3 Weeks** without **Food**.

## interpretation
Most people obsess over food ("I need to hunt!"). **Wrong.**
*   **Step 1: Shelter**. Get out of the wind/rain. Hypothermia kills faster than dehydration.
*   **Step 2: Water**. Find a source. Filter it.
*   **Step 3: Fire**. Warmth, moral support, and signaling.
*   **Step 4: Food**. This is last. You can go a long time without eating. Don't waste calories hunting until you are warm and watered.
