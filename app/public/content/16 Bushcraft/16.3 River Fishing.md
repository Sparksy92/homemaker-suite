# River Fishing: Tools from Nature

Running water is a conveyor belt of food.

## The Fish Funnel (Weir)
Fish are lazy. They follow the current but rest in eddies.
1.  **Build a V**: Pile rocks in the river to form a "V" shape pointing downstream.
2.  **The Trap**: Leave a small gap at the point of the V. Place a woven basket (willow/reed) in the gap.
3.  **Result**: Fish swim downstream, get funneled by the rock walls, and forced into the basket.

## The Gorge Hook
No metal hook? No problem.
1.  Find a small, hard bone or piece of wood (1 inch long).
2.  Sharpen both ends.
3.  Tie line to the *middle*.
4.  Bait it (worm/grub).
5.  **How it works**: The fish swallows it vertically. When you pull the line, the stick turns horizontal (sideways) and lodges in the throat.

## Spear Fishing
Harder than it looks due to refraction (light bending).
*   **Aim Low**: The fish is lower than it looks. Aim 6 inches below the fish.
