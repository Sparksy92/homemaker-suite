# Fire Starting: Primitive & Modern

Fire is life. It cooks food, boils water, and keeps predators away.

## The Ferro Rod (Modern Flint)
The most reliable tool. It works when wet.
1.  **Tinder**: You need a bird's nest of dry grass, birch bark, or scraped fatwood.
2.  **Strike**: Don't flick the wrist. Hold the striker still and **pull the rod back** aggressively. This dumps molten sparks into the tinder without smashing the pile.

## The Bow Drill (Friction)
If you have no tools.
*   **Components**: Hearth board (soft wood), Spindle (straight stick), Hand hold (rock/hard wood), Bow.
*   **The Physics**: Spin the spindle against the board until it creates black dust (coal).
*   **The Coal**: The dust will start smoking on its own. Carefully transfer this ember to your tinder bundle and blow gently.

## Tinder Fungus (Chaga)
Grows on birch trees. It takes a spark easily and smolders for hours, allowing you to transport fire.
