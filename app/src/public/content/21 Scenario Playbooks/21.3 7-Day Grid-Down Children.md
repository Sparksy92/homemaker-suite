# Survival Playbook: 7-Day Grid-Down with Children

**Scenario:** Widespread grid failure. Services (Schools, Stores) closed.
**Duration:** 7 Days (1 Week).
**Goal:** Maintain order, hygiene, and mental health for a family unit. Children absorb parental stress—calm parents = calm kids.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Parents Only)

### 1.1 The "Game" Narrative
*   **Goal:** Frame the event as an adventure ("Camping at home"), not a disaster.
*   **Action:** Hold a family meeting. Explain calmly: "The power is out for a while. We are safe. We have food. We are going to have a camp-out."
*   **Roles:** Assign jobs immediately.
    *   *Age 4-7:* "Flashlight Monitor" / "Pet Watcher".
    *   *Age 8-12:* "Water Runner" / "Fire Helper".
    *   *Teen:* "Comms Officer" (Radio) / "Security Check".

### 1.2 Secure the Environment
*   **Childproofing:** Dark houses are dangerous. Clear trip hazards (toys) from hallways.
*   **Light Strategy:** Give every child a glow stick or headlamp. It reduces fear of the dark significantly.

---

## 2. Daily Routine (Structure is Safety)

**Children thrive on routine. Chaos breeds fear.**

### Morning (08:00 - 12:00)
*   **08:00:** Breakfast together. Hot cocoa (comfort food).
*   **09:00:** Hygiene. Teeth brushing, face washing.
*   **10:00:** "School" / Skill Time.
    *   *Activity:* Learn knots, read books, draw maps of the neighborhood.
    *   *Why:* Keeps brains active and distracted.

### Afternoon (12:00 - 17:00)
*   **12:00:** Lunch. (Sandwiches/Crackers).
*   **13:00:** Quiet Time / Nap. Parents need this time to plan/discuss adult issues without kids hearing "scary talk".
*   **15:00:** Active Play. Burn off energy. (Tag, Hide and Seek - providing safe environment).

### Evening (17:00 - 20:00)
*   **17:00:** Dinner Prep (Collaborative). Kids help stir/open cans.
*   **18:00:** Dinner by Candlelight. Tell stories.
*   **19:00:** Wind down. No scary ghost stories.
*   **20:00:** Bedtime. Maintain normal sleep schedule.

---

## 3. Specific Challenges

### 3.1 Boredom & Tech Withdrawal
*   **Withdrawal:** Kids addicted to screens will be irritable for 24-48 hours. Expect tantrums.
*   **Solution:** Valid replacement. Board games, card games, drawing.
*   **Tip:** Save a "Backup Battery" for 1 hour of iPad time on Day 3 or 4 (Morale booster).

### 3.2 Food Refusal
*   **Issue:** Stress suppresses appetite or makes pickiness worse.
*   **Solution:** Stick to familiar foods if possible (Mac & Cheese, PB&J). Don't force "Survival Rations" (MREs) unless necessary.
*   **Snacks:** Keep high-value snacks (Fruit snacks, Granola bars) for low moments.

### 3.3 Fear / Nightmares
*   **Solution:** Co-sleeping. Move mattresses into one room (The "Family Nest"). Increases warmth and security.

---

## 4. Systems Checklist

### 💧 Water & Hygiene
*   **Sanitation:** If toilets fail -> Bucket Toilet.
    *   *Drill:* Teach kids how to use it. "Cover your deposit with sawdust." (Make it a rule).
*   **Handwashing:** Mandatory before eating. (Prevent Dysentery).

### 🩺 Health
*   **First Aid:** Scrapes/Cuts are more likely in dark houses. Treat immediately to prevent infection (no doctors).
*   **Meds:** Ensure kids' regular meds (Asthma/ADHD/Insulin) are accessible.

### 🛡️ Safety
*   **Rule:** "If you hear the whistle, come to Mom/Dad immediately."
*   **Fire:** Keep lighters/matches secured HIGH up. Kids might try to "help" and start a fire.

---

## 5. Lessons Learned
*   *Did you run out of snacks?* -> **Stockpile "Comfort Foods" specifically for kids.**
*   *Too dark?* -> **Buy varied light sources (lanterns vs headlamps).**
*   *Diapers?* -> **If you have a baby, stock 2x normal supply.**
