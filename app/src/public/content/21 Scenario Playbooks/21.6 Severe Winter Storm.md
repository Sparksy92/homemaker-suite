# Survival Playbook: Severe Winter Storm (No Heat + Limited Food)

**Scenario:** Blizzard traps you inside indefinitely. Power is out. Heating fuel (Propane/Oil/Wood) is exhausted. Food is scarce (Pantry empty).
**Duration:** 7-14 Days.
**Goal:** Prevent freezing to death. Stretch calories.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Day 0)

### 1.1 The "Core" (Shrink the House)
*   **Action:** ABANDON THE HOUSE. Move everyone into the smallest, most central room (without windows if possible, or South facing).
*   **Insulate:** Tape plastic/blankets over windows. Stuff towels under doors.
*   **Nest:** Build a "fort" with mattresses on the floor. Everyone sleeps together sharing body heat.
*   **Reason:** A smaller volume is easier to heat with body warmth (100W per person).

### 1.2 Calorie Audit
*   **Inventory:** Count every calorie. (e.g., 20,000 Total).
*   **Math:** 20,000 / 4 People / 14 Days = 357 Calories/Day. (Starvation Rations).
*   **Rule:** NO MOVEMENT. To survive on 350 calories, you must hibernate. Stay in the sleeping bag 20 hours/day.

### 1.3 Fluid Logistics
*   **Water:** Pipes will freeze. Fill pots NOW.
*   **Snow:** Bring snow inside in buckets to melt *passively* (do not waste fuel melting snow if possible).

---

## 2. Daily Protocol

### Morning (10:00 - Late Rise)
*   **Action:** Stay in bags until sun warms the room.
*   **Breakfast:** Hot water (if fuel allows). Warmth > Calories.
*   **Hygiene:** Dry wash (Baby wipes). Do not get wet. Wet = Hypothermia.

### Mid-Day (12:00 - 14:00)
*   **Action:** One person checks the house/pipes. Everyone else stays sedentary.
*   **Meal:** The single meal of the day. Consolidate rations to feel "full" once, rather than hungry all day.
*   **Sun:** If sun is out, open curtains *only* on South side. Close immediately when sun passes.

### Evening (16:00 - Early Bed)
*   **Action:** Candlelight (careful of O2 depletion).
*   **Sleep:** 17:00 (Sunset). Huddle. Wear hats. Put layers *on top* of the sleeping bag, not just inside.

---

## 3. Systems Checklist

### 🔥 Heat Conservation
*   **Layers:** Base layer (wicking) -> Insulation (Fleece/Sweater) -> Shell (Windproof).
*   **Head:** 40% of heat lost through head. Hat is mandatory 24/7.
*   **Floors:** Cold sinks. Sleep elevated on mattresses/cots, or pile cardboard/rugs under you.

### 🥬 Nutrition Strategy (Starvation Mode)
*   **Protein:** Save jerky/tuna for warmth (digesting protein creates heat).
*   **Carbs:** Quick energy.
*   **Fats:** Slower burn. Mix oil/butter into everything. "Grease creates heat."

### 🩺 Health Watch
*   **Hypothermia Stages:** Shivering -> Confusion -> Stops Shivering (Critical) -> Unconsciousness.
*   **Treatment:** Skin-to-skin contact inside a sleeping bag. Warm sweet drink. Do *not* rub skin (damages tissue).
*   **Frostbite:** Numb white patches on nose/fingers. Thaw with *tepid* water. Do not burn.

---

## 4. Lessons Learned
*   *Did you almost starve?* -> **Keep 30 days of rice/beans (Cost: $20).**
*   *Did you freeze?* -> **Buy a "Mr. Buddy" propane heater + 20lbs of fuel.**
*   *Did you panic?* -> **Read this manual again.**
