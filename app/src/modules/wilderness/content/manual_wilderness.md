## 1. The Psychology of Survival (STOP)

Panic kills faster than cold.
*   **S - Sit:** Stop moving. Take a breath. Adrenaline makes you stupid.
*   **T - Think:** Assess immediate threats (Bleeding? Cold? Bears?).
*   **O - Observe:** Look around for resources (Shelter, Water, Fuel).
*   **P - Plan:** Make a plan before you move. "I will build a shelter before dark."

## 2. Fire: The Life Force

Fire = Warmth, Water purification, Signaling, Morale.

### 2.1 The Fire Triangle
*   **Heat (Spark)** + **Fuel (Wood)** + **Oxygen (Air)**.
*   *Remove one, fire dies.*

### 2.2 Tinder, Kindling, Fuel
1.  **Tinder:** Catches the spark. (Dry grass, birch bark, cattail fluff, cotton ball + vaseline). Size: Hair.
2.  **Kindling:** Catches the flame. (Small twigs). Size: Pencil lead to Pencil thickness.
3.  **Fuel:** Sustains the fire. (Split wood, logs). Size: Thumb to Thigh.

### 2.3 Friction Fire (Bow Drill) - The Hard Way
*   **Components:** Bow, Drill (Spindle), Hearth Board, Bearing Block.
*   **Wood Choice:** Soft wood on soft wood (Willow, Poplar, Cedar).
*   **Technique:**
    1.  Carve notch in hearth.
    2.  Spin drill with bow. Friction creates dust (coal).
    3.  Dust collects in notch. Smoke -> Coal.
    4.  Transfer coal to tinder bundle (Bird's nest).
    5.  Blow into flame.
*   **Warning:** Extremely difficult in wet conditions. Always carry a Bic lighter.

### 2.4 The Ferro Rod (Modern Standard)
*   **Technique:** Scrape SLOWLY with force. Throw sparks INTO the tinder, not over it.
*   **Reliability:** Works when wet. Lasts 10,000 strikes.

## 3. Shelter (The Micro-Environment)

You can die of Hypothermia at 50°F if wet/windy.
*   **Rule:** Get off the ground. Conduction sucks heat out of you.
*   **A-Frame Tarp:** Simple. Sheds rain. Needs ridge line.
*   **Lean-To:** Reflects heat from fire. Open front.
*   **Debris Hut:** (See Shelter Module). Best for cold without fire.

## 4. Water (Field Procurement)

*   **Follow Gravity:** Water flows downhill. Head to valley bottoms.
*   **Indicator Plants:** Willows, Cottonwoods, Sycamores grow near water.
*   **Dew:** Drag a t-shirt through tall grass at sunrise. Wring it out.

## 5. Navigation (Getting Found)

### 5.1 The Sun (East/West)
*   **Shadow Stick Method:**
    1.  Place stick in ground. Mark tip of shadow (West).
    2.  Wait 15 mins. Mark new tip (East).
    3.  Line between marks is E-W line.

### 5.2 The Stars (North)
*   **Process:** Find Big Dipper. Follow "pointer stars" (outer edge of cup) 5x distance to Polaris (North Star).
*   **Polaris:** Always North (within 1 degree). Does not move.

### 5.3 Handrails & Backstops
*   **Handrail:** Linear feature (River, Road, Powerline) parallel to your route. "Keep the river on my right."
*   **Backstop:** Linear feature beyond your target. "If I hit the highway, I've gone too far."

## 6. Signaling (Rescue)

### 6.1 Passive Signals
*   **Ground-to-Air:** Huge "X" or "V" made of contrasting logs/rocks. (X = Unable to move, V = Require Assistance).
*   **Contrast:** Visual noise is the enemy. Straight lines do not exist in nature.

### 6.2 Active Signals
*   **Mirror:** Flash at planes/boats. Can be seen for 20+ miles.
*   **Fire:** 3 Fires in a triangle (International Distress).
*   **Smoke:** Green boughs on hot fire = Thick White Smoke (Day). Rubber/Oil = Black Smoke (Winter/Snow).

## 7. Food (The Last Priority)

Do not eat unless you have water (digestion consumes water).
### 7.1 The Universal Edibility Test (Review)
*   **Step 1:** Smell (Almonds? Rot?).
*   **Step 2:** Contact (Skin).
*   **Step 3:** Lip/Tongue.
*   **Step 4:** Swallow (Tiny piece).
*   **Wait:** 8 hours between steps.
*   **Note:** Only use in true starvation (3+ weeks). Getting poisoned causes vomiting/diarrhea (dehydration/death) faster than starvation.

### 7.2 Trapping (Passive Hunting)
*   **Snares:** Wire loop on game trail. Head height for target (Rabbit = fist width).
*   **Deadfalls:** Heavy rock/log triggers. (Paiute Deadfall).
*   **Ethics:** Illegal in peacetime. Crucial in survival. Check traps daily to prevent suffering/spoilage.

## 8. Weather Adaptation

### 8.1 Cold (Hypothermia)
*   **Symptoms:** Shivering (Good), Shivering Stops (BAD - Stage 2), Confusion (Paradoxical undressing).
*   **Treat:** Dry clothes -> Skin-to-skin warming -> Warm sugary drink.

### 8.2 Heat (Hyperthermia)
*   **Heat Exhaustion:** Sweating, pale, weak. (Hydrate + Shade).
*   **Heat Stroke:** NO sweating, red skin, hot, unconscious. (COOL IMMEDIATELY - Life Threatening).
