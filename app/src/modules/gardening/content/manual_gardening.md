**Version:** 1.0 (Survival Edition)
**Domain:** Horticulture / Permaculture
**Complexity:** Medium
**Dependency:** Tools, Water

## 1. Garden Construction: Building the System

You cannot eat grass. You need to convert lawn/wasteland into calorie-producing soil.

### 1.1 Sheet Mulching ("Lasagna Gardening")
**The Survival Standard.** No digging. No tilling. Builds soil *instantly*.
1.  **Mow:** Cut existing vegetation as short as possible. Leave clippings in place (Nitrogen).
2.  **Barrier:** Lay down cardboard or 6 layers of newspaper. Overlap edges by 6 inches. Wet it thoroughly. (This kills weeds/grass).
3.  **Nitrogen Layer (Green):** Add 2-3 inches of manure, veggie scraps, or fresh grass clippings.
4.  **Carbon Layer (Brown):** Add 4-6 inches of straw, dead leaves, or shredded paper.
5.  **Planting:** Dig a small pocket through the top layers, fill with potting soil, and plant starts immediately.

### 1.2 Raised Beds
**Best for wet/clay areas.**
*   **Dimensions:** 4ft wide (reachable from both sides) x 8ft long. 12+ inches deep.
*   **Materials:** Logs, stones, scrap lumber (avoid chemically treated wood if possible).
*   **Fill:** Hugelkultur (bury rotting wood at bottom) -> Compost -> Topsoil.

### 1.3 Double Digging (Biointensive)
**High Calorie / Small Space.** Labor intensive.
1.  Dig a trench 12" deep. Move soil to the end of the bed.
2.  Loosen the subsoil (bottom of trench) another 12" deep with a fork.
3.  Dig second trench, moving topsoil into the first trench.
4.  Repeat.
5.  **Result:** 24 inches of loose soil. Roots grow down, not out. You can plant 4x closer together.

## 2. Soil Enrichment: Feeding the Earth

"Feed the soil, not the plant."

### 2.1 Cover Crops ("Green Manure")
Grow these *not to eat*, but to kill and bury. They fix nutrients and secure topsoil.
*   **Nitrogen Fixers:**
    *   *Red Clover:* Perennial. Best for pathways.
    *   *Hairy Vetch:* Winter hardy. Fixes massive Nitrogen.
    *   *Field Peas:* Fast growing spring/fall cover.
*   **Biomass Builders:**
    *   *Winter Rye:* Unkillable. Grows in freezing temps. Breaks up clay with massive roots.
    *   *Buckwheat:* Fast (6 weeks). Smothers weeds. Bees love it.
*   **Protocol:** Cut them down *before* they go to seed. Leave on surface as mulch (Chop and Drop).

### 2.2 Dynamic Accumulators
Deep taproots mine minerals from subsoil and bring them to the surface leaves.
*   **Comfrey (Bocking 14):** The "Medical Miracle" for soil. Comparison: Comfrey leaf tea has higher N-P-K than manure.
    *   *Use:* Cut leaves 4x/year. Mulch around tomatoes.

### 2.3 Tea Composts
*   **Compost Tea:** Aerate compost in water for 24hrs. Spray on leaves (Foliar feed). Microbiome boost.
*   **Weed Tea:** Drown weeds (without seeds) in a bucket of water. Cover (it will stink). Wait 2 weeks. Dilute 10:1. High Nitrogen liquid fertilizer.

## 3. Pest Control: The War on Bugs

**Rule #1:** 90% of bugs are neutral or good. Don't nuke the garden.

### 3.1 The "Bad Guys" (Rogues Gallery)

| Pest | Identification | Damage | Remedy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aphids** | Tiny green/black specs under leaves. | Sucking sap. Curling leaves. | Blast with water hose. Ladybugs. Neem Oil. |
| **Cabbage Moth/Worm** | White moth. Green velvet worm. | Holes in Broccoli/Kale/Cabbage. | **Bt Spray** (Best). Row covers. Decoy moths (white plastic). |
| **Cutworm** | Grey grub. Curls into 'C'. | Cuts seedlings off at base overnight. | **Collars:** Cardboard tube around stem (1" into soil). |
| **Potato Beetle** | Striped orange/black beetle. | Skeletonizes potato leaves. | Hand pick daily. Crush orange egg clusters under leaves. |
| **Slugs/Snails** | Slimy. Nocturnal. | Irregular holes. Slime trails. | **Beer Trap:** Shallow dish of stale beer. They drown. Diatomaceous Earth. |
| **Squash Bug** | Grey shield shape. | Wilts vines. Injects toxin. | Hand pick. Look for copper egg clusters. Tape method (lift eggs). |
| **Tomato Hornworm** | Huge green worm. White stripes. | Strips tomato plants in hours. | UV Light at night (they glow). Hand pick. Braconid Wasps. |

### 3.2 Improvised Remedies
*   **Soap Spray:** 1 tsp vegetable oil + 1 tsp dish soap + 1 quart water. Smothers soft-bodied insects.
*   **Garlic/Pepper Spray:** Blend 2 bulbs garlic + 2 habaneros + 1 quart water. Strain. Repels mammals and bugs. (Wear goggles).
*   **Wood Ash:** Sprinkle around base of plants. Deterrents slugs and provides Potassium.

### 3.3 The "Good Guys" (Allies)
**NEVER KILL THESE.**
1.  **Ladybugs:** Larvae look like tiny alligators. Eat 5,000 aphids/year.
2.  **Praying Mantis:** Generalist predator.
3.  **Braconid Wasp:** Tiny parasitic wasp. Lays white eggs on Hornworms. If you see a worm with "rice" on its back, LEAVE IT. It is a zombie hatchery.
4.  **Lacewings:** Larvae ("Aphid Lions") are voracious predators.

## 4. Planting & Propagation

### 4.1 Seed Depth Rule
Plant seeds **2-3x their width** deep.
*   *Tiny (Lettuce/Carrot):* Surface sow, dust lightly with soil.
*   *Medium (Beet/Spinach):* 1/2 inch.
*   *Large (Bean/Corn/Pea):* 1 inch.

### 4.2 Hardening Off
**Crucial Step.** You cannot move a plant from a warm windowsill to the wild instantly. It will die of shock (Sun/Wind scald).
*   **Day 1-3:** Outside in shade for 2 hours.
*   **Day 4-5:** Morning sun for 4 hours.
*   **Day 6-7:** Full sun all day. Overnight if temps allow.

### 4.3 Succession Planting (Don't Starve)
Space out plantings to ensure continuous harvest.
*   **Radish:** Every 2 weeks.
*   **Lettuce:** Every 2 weeks.
*   **Beans:** Every 3 weeks.
*   **Corn:** Every 3 weeks.
