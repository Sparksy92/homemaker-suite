# Survival Manual: Food Preservation
**Version:** 1.0 (Survival Edition)
**Domain:** Chemistry / Microbiology
**Complexity:** High (Botulism Risk)
**Dependency:** Food Production, Water, Energy (Heat)

---

## 1. The Enemy: Spoilage & Botulism

You are fighting bacteria, mold, and enzymes.
*   **Clostridium botulinum:** The silent killer. Odorless, tasteless, deadly neurotoxin. Thrives in *anaerobic* (no oxygen), *low acid* environments.
    *   *Kill Switch:* Temperature > 240°F (116°C) for specific times. Boiling (212°F) is NOT enough for spores.
    *   *Acid Defense:* pH < 4.6 prevents growth.

---

## 2. Water Bath vs. Pressure Canning

**CRITICAL RULE:**
*   **High Acid Foods (pH < 4.6):** Fruits, Pickles, Tomatoes (mostly), Jams. -> **Water Bath Canning** (Boiling) is safe.
*   **Low Acid Foods (pH > 4.6):** Vegetables, MEAT, Poultry, Fish, Soup. -> **Pressure Canning** is MANDATORY.

### 2.1 Water Bath Canning (The Basics)
*   **Gear:** Large pot, rack (keep jars off bottom).
*   **Process:**
    1.  Sterilize jars.
    2.  Fill hot jars with hot food (leave 1/2" headspace).
    3.  Wipe rims CLEAN (vinegar cloth).
    4.  Apply lid and ring (finger tight).
    5.  Submerge in boiling water (1" over top).
    6.  Boil for recipe time (e.g., 10 mins for jam).
    7.  Cool 24 hrs. Check seal (lid shouldn't pop).

### 2.2 Pressure Canning (The Survivalist Standard)
*   **Gear:** Pressure Canner (NOT a pressure cooker/Instant Pot - they don't hold stable pressure).
*   **Process:**
    1.  2-3 inches of water in canner.
    2.  Add jars. Lock lid.
    3.  Vent steam for 10 mins (exhaust air).
    4.  Put on weight (10lbs or 15lbs depending on altitude).
    5.  Process Time: Pints/Quarts vary. (e.g., Green Beans = 20 mins @ 11lbs).
    6.  Let pressure drop to ZERO naturally.

## 3. Fermentation (Controlled Rot)

The low-tech survival king. Creates probiotics.

### 3.1 Lacto-Fermentation (Sauerkraut/Kimchi)
*   **Science:** *Lactobacillus* bacteria (on the veggie skins) eat sugars and poop Lactic Acid (Preservative).
*   **The Ratio:** 2-3% Salt by weight of veg+water.
    *   *Simple:* 1 Tbsp salt per quart of water.
*   **Process (Sauerkraut):**
    1.  Shred Cabbage. Weigh it.
    2.  Add 2% salt. Massage until water releases.
    3.  Pack into crock/jar TIGHTly.
    4.  **Weight it down:** Cabbage MUST be under the brine. "Under the brine, everything's fine."
    5.  Wait 3-4 weeks.

### 3.2 Vinegar Making (Scrap Vinegar)
*   **Ingredients:** Apple cores/peels, sugar water.
*   **Process:**
    1.  Jar + Scraps + Sugar Water.
    2.  Cover w/ cloth (needs air/yeast).
    3.  Stir daily.
    4.  Alcohol forms first (Hard Cider). Acetobacter turns alcohol to Acetic Acid (Vinegar).
    5.  Done when it tastes sharp (~2 months).

## 4. Dehydration (Solar & Air)

Removing moisture stops bacteria.

### 4.1 Solar Dehydrator Build
*   **Components:** Black painted box (collector), Screened trays (food), Glass top (greenhouse effect), Vents (airflow).
*   **Physics:** Hot air rises through trays, carrying moisture out top vent.
*   **Temp:** Target 120°F - 140°F.
*   **Warning:** Too cool = mold. Too hot = cooked (hard shell, wet inside).

### 4.2 Meat (Jerky)
*   **Lean Meat Only:** Fat goes rancid. Trim all fat.
*   **Cut:** With the grain (chewy) or against (brittle). 1/4" strips.
*   **Cure:** Salt + Sugar + Smoke (Optional).
*   **Dry:** Until it cracks but doesn't break.
*   **Storage:** Vacuum seal or glass jar. 2-3 months.

### 4.3 Pemmican (The Ultimate Ration)
Lasts 50+ Years.
*   **Recipe:**
    *   50% Dried lean meat (pounded to powder).
    *   50% Rendered Fat (Tallow/Suet).
    *   Optional: Dried berries (acid helps preservation).
*   **Process:** Mix meat powder into melted fat. Pour into molds. Let harden.
*   **Calorie Density:** Extreme. 3000+ kcal/lb.

## 5. Smoking (Chemical Preservation)

Smoke contains antimicrobial compounds (phenols) and dries the surface (Fly barrier).

### 5.1 Cold Smoking (< 100°F)
*   **Goal:** Cure, not cook. Preservation.
*   **Duration:** Days to Weeks.
*   **Wood:** Hardwood ONLY (Hickory, Oak, Apple). No Pine (Resin = bitter/toxic).
*   **Setup:** Smoke generator separated from food box by pipe to cool the smoke.

### 5.2 Hot Smoking (160°F - 225°F)
*   **Goal:** Cook + Flavor + Short term preservation.
*   **Duration:** Hours.
*   **Result:** Eat within days unless dried/canned further.

## 6. Root Cellars (Earth Cooling)

Using the stable temperature of the earth (50°F-55°F).

### 6.1 Requirements
*   **Temp:** 32°F - 40°F is ideal.
*   **Humidity:** 90-95% (prevent shriveling).
*   **Ventilation:** Critical to remove Ethylene gas (ripening hormone).
*   **Darkness:** Prevents sprouting (potatoes turning green/toxic).

### 6.2 Improvised "Clamp" (Field Cellar)
1.  Dig hole. Line with straw.
2.  Pile Veggies (Potatoes/Carrots) in a cone.
3.  Cover with 6" Straw.
4.  Cover with 6" Dirt.
5.  Leave a straw "chimney" at top for vent.
6.  Dig drainage trench around it.

## 7. Long-Term Storage Architecture

### 7.1 The Bucket Method (Dry Goods)
*   **Items:** Wheat berries, White Rice, Beans, Oats, Pasta. (NOT Brown rice - oils rancidify).
*   **Container:** 5-Gallon Mylar Bag inside HDPE Bucket.
*   **Oxygen Absorbers:** Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3 (Rust). Removes oxygen.
    *   *Size:* 2000cc per 5-gallon bucket.
*   **Process:** Fill bag. Add absorber. Heat seal immediately (hair straightener). Lid.
*   **Shelf Life:** 25-30 Years.

### 7.2 The Enemy: Oxygen, Moisture, Light, Heat, Pests (OMLHP)
*   **O:** Oxygen Absorbers.
*   **M:** Desiccant Packs (Silica). *Warning: Do not use O2 absorbers and Desiccant together (Desiccant dries out the O2 absorber needed to work).*
*   **L:** Mylar (opaque).
*   **H:** Buried or basement.
*   **P:** Buckets/Metal tins (Rats chew plastic bags).

## 8. Emergency Calorie Rations (Hardtack)

"Pilot Bread". Lasts forever. rock hard.
*   **Recipe:** 2 cups Flour, 1/2 Tbsp Salt, Water (~3/4 cup).
*   **Dough:** Stiff. Roll flat. Poke holes (docking).
*   **Bake:** 375°F for 30 mins. Flip. 30 mins more.
*   **Use:** Must be soaked in coffee/soup to eat without breaking teeth.
