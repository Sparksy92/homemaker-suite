# Survival Manual: Shelter & Home Systems
**Version:** 1.0 (Survival Edition)
**Domain:** Construction / Security / Physics
**Complexity:** Medium
**Dependency:** Energy, Tools

---

## 1. The Hierarchy of Shelter

1.  **Clothing:** Your first shelter. Keep dry. Layering (Base, Mid, Shell).
2.  **Immediate:** Tarp / Tent / Debris Hut.
3.  **Temporary:** Cabin / retrofitted room.
4.  **Permanent:** Hardened homestead.

---

## 2. Home Winterization (The "Micro-Climate")

If the grid fails in winter, your house is a freezer.
### 2.1 The "Warm Room" Concept
*   **Strategy:** Do not try to heat 2000 sqft. Pick ONE room (South facing, small).
*   **Modification:**
    *   Hang carpets/blankets over doorways.
    *   Cover windows with bubble wrap (poor man's double glazing) or moving blankets at night.
    *   Tent inside the room: Sleeping in a small tent *inside* the house traps body heat effectively.

### 2.2 Insulation
*   **Heat Loss:** Roof (25%), Windows (20%), Walls (20%), Drafts (15%).
*   **Fixes:**
    *   *Drafts:* Rolled up towels (snakes) at door bottoms. Caulk window frames.
    *   *Windows:* Clear plastic sheeting taped to frames creates dead air space.

## 3. Structural Repair & Improvisation

### 3.1 Broken Windows
*   **Repair:** Plywood is best (security + insulation).
*   **Improvised:** Cardboard + Plastic bags (stuffed with leaves/paper for R-value).

### 3.2 Roof Leaks
*   **Tarping:** The blue tarp is the universal roof.
    *   *Technique:* Wrap ends around 2x4s and screw through the 2x4 (don't rely on grommets, they tear).
    *   *Pitch:* Ensure steep angle to shed snow/water.

## 4. Fire Safety (The Greatest Threat)

Cooking and heating with open flame indoors is dangerous.
### 4.1 Carbon Monoxide (CO) - The Silent Killer
*   **Detectors:** Battery operated. Stockpile batteries.
*   **Symptoms:** Headache, dizziness, nausea, sleepiness.
*   **Ventilation:** Crack a window slightly even if cold.

### 4.2 Chimney Fires
*   **Cause:** Creosote buildup from burning green/wet wood.
*   **Prevention:** Burn hot fires daily to dry it out. Clean flue annually.
*   **Extinguishing:** Close air intakes immediately. Chimfex stick (flare that sucks oxygen). Water (steam explosion risk - last resort).

## 5. Security Fortifications

### 5.1 Layered Defense (The Onion)
1.  **Perimeter:** Fence, thorny bushes (Blackberry/Hawthorn), tripwires (noise makers).
2.  **Yard:** Remove cover for attackers (bushes near house). Motion lights (solar).
3.  **Shell:** Hardened doors/windows.
4.  **Safe Room:** The final fallback.

### 5.2 Hardening Doors
*   **Weak Point:** The Jamb (it splits).
*   **Fix:** Replace 1/2" screws in strike plate with 3-4" screws that go deep into the stud frame.
*   **Barricade:** Door Club / 2x4 brackets.

### 5.3 Window Security
*   **Visual:** Blackout curtains (don't show you have light/power).
*   **Physical:** Security Film (prevents shattering). Bars (ensure fire escape release).
*   **Psychological:** "Beware of Dog" signs (even if you don't have one).

## 6. Improvised Shelter (Wilderness/Urban)

### 6.1 Debris Hut (Nature's Sleeping Bag)
*   **Ridgepole:** Long sturdy pole, one end on ground, one in tree fork.
*   **Ribs:** Lean sticks against ridgepole closer together.
*   **Insulation:** Pile leaves/grass/pine needles 3 FEET THICK on top.
*   **Bedding:** 1 foot thick pile inside (keep off cold ground).

### 6.2 Urban Scavenge
*   **Pallet Shelter:** Pallets filled with newspaper/trash for insulation. Covered with tarp.
*   **Vehicle:** Poor insulation (metal box). Crack window for ventilation (CO/Moisture). Sleep in back seat (warmer than trunk).

## 7. Sanitation & Waste

If the toilet doesn't flush...
### 7.1 The Twin Bucket System
*   **Bucket 1 (Liquid):** Pee here. Dilute 10:1 for garden fertilizer. Sterile-ish.
*   **Bucket 2 (Solid):** Poop here. Cover with sawdust/peat moss. Composting (Humanure).
*   **Why Separate?** Urine creates the smell when mixed with feces. Separating reduces odor by 90%.

### 7.2 Trash Management
*   **Burnable:** Paper/Cardboard (Fuel).
*   **Compostable:** Food (Garden).
*   **Recyclable:** Metal/Glass (Materials).
*   **Toxic:** Plastic. Do not burn (Dioxins). Bury or store.
