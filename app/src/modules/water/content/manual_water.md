# Survival Manual: Water Systems
**Version:** 1.0 (Survival Edition)
**Domain:** Hydrology / Engineering
**Complexity:** Critical (Rule of 3: You die in 3 days without water)
**Dependency:** None (Foundational)

## 1. The Rule of 3 (Water)
*   **3 Minutes** without Air.
*   **3 Days** without Water.
*   **3 Weeks** without Food.
*   **Priority:** Water is higher priority than food, shelter (usually), or security.

## 2. Water Sourcing

Where to find it when the taps run dry.

### 2.1 Surface Water (High Risk)
*   **Streams/Rivers:** Moving water is better than stagnant, but ALL surface water is assumed contaminated (Giardia/Crypto).
*   **Lakes/Ponds:** Scum on top suggests stagnation. Draw from subsurface if possible.

### 2.2 Rainwater Harvesting (Medium Risk)
*   **The Roof:** 1 inch of rain on 1,000 sqft roof = **600 Gallons**.
*   **The First Flush:** The first 5-10 gallons wash the bird poop/dust off the roof.
    *   *Diverter Build:* Vertical pipe before the barrel. The dirty water fills the pipe, a floating ball seals it, then clean water flows to barrel.
*   **Storage:** Opaque barrels (prevent algae). Screened top (mosquitoes).

### 2.3 Groundwater / Wells (Low Risk)
*   **Dug Well:** Shallow water table.
*   **Driven Point (Sand Point) Well:**
    *   *Gear:* 1.25" galvanized pipe with a screened point. Sledgehammer (or slide hammer).
    *   *Terrain:* Sandy/Loamy soil. Water table < 25ft.
    *   *Process:* Drive pipe down. Add sections. Test for water. Install pitcher pump.

### 2.4 Transpiration (Vegetation)
*   **Bag Method:** Clear plastic bag over leafy branch. Sun heats leaves -> water evaporates -> condenses in bag.
*   **Yield:** Low. Emergency only.

## 3. Purification & Filtration

**Filtration** removes stuff (dirt, microplastics). **Purification** kills bugs (bacteria, viruses). YOU NEED BOTH.

### 3.1 The Two-Stage System
1.  **Filter (Turbidity):** Remove the mud. Bacteria ride on dirt particles. UV/Chemicals can't penetrate dirt particles.
    *   *Improvised Filter:* Inverted bottle. Layers (Bottom to Top): cloth -> charcoal -> sand -> gravel -> rock.
2.  **Purify (Pathogens):** Kill the invisible stuff.

### 3.2 Boiling (The Gold Standard)
*   **Rule:** Rolling boil for **1 Minute**. (3 Minutes if > 6,500ft elevation).
*   **Pros:** Kills everything. No chemicals.
*   **Cons:** Fuel intensive. Water tastes flat (pour back and forth to aerate).

### 3.3 Chlorine Bleach (Liquid)
*   **Type:** Unscented, Regular (6% or 8.25% Sodium Hypochlorite). NO "Splashless", NO Scented.
*   **Dosing:**
    *   **Clear Water:** 8 drops per gallon.
    *   **Cloudy Water:** 16 drops per gallon.
*   **Wait Time:** 30 Minutes. Should smell slightly of chlorine. If not, treat again.
*   **Shelf Life:** Bleach degrades in 6-12 months. Rotate stock.

### 3.4 Calcium Hypochlorite (Pool Shock) - Long Term
*   **Form:** Dry powder/granular (65-70% strength).
*   **Shelf Life:** 10+ Years.
*   **Process:**
    1.  **Make Stock Solution:** 1 heaping teaspoon (granular) into 2 gallons water. Do NOT drink this. It is corrosive.
    2.  **Treat Water:** Mix 1 part Stock Solution to 100 parts water (approx 1 pint stock per 12 gallons water).
*   **Warning:** EXPLOSIVE contact with oil/organic matter. Store in glass, keeping dry.

### 3.5 SODIS (Solar Disinfection) - The Zero Tech Option
*   **Gear:** Clear PET plastic bottles (Recycle code #1).
*   **Process:**
    1.  Fill clear water (must see through it).
    2.  Lay in direct sun on reflective surface (roof/foil).
    3.  **Time:** 6 hours full sun. (2 days if cloudy).
*   **Mechanism:** UV-A radiation damages DNA of pathogens. Heat helps too (>122°F).

## 4. Off-Grid Plumbing

### 4.1 Gravity Fed System
*   **Header Tank:** Large barrel/tank in attic or on tower.
*   **Physics:** 1 ft elevation = 0.43 PSI.
    *   *Shower:* Needs ~15-20ft height for decent pressure, or specialized low-pressure head.
*   **Fill:** Hand pump or 12V DC solar pump moves water from ground cistern to header tank.

### 4.2 Greywater (The Second Use)
*   **Sources:** Laundry, Shower, Sink.
*   **Uses:** Flush toilet, Water trees/bushes. (NOT root vegetables).
*   **Filter:** Woodchip/Sand biofilter to remove soap scum before garden application.

### 4.3 Blackwater (Sewage)
*   **Rule:** Keep it separate. see "Composting" module.
*   **Septic:** If grid fails, septic pump fails. Do not use flush toilets unless you have gravity septic.

## 5. Disease Recognition (Waterborne)

### 5.1 Bacterial (Cholera, E. Coli, Salmonella)
*   **Symptoms:** Rapid onset (hours/days). Violent diarrhea, vomiting.
*   **Treatment:** Rehydration (ORS). Antibiotics (Cipro/Azithromycin) if available.

### 5.2 Protozoan (Giardia, Cryptosporidium)
*   **Symptoms:** Delayed onset (1-2 weeks). "Beaver Fever". Sulphurous burps, greasy floating stool, gas.
*   **Resilience:** Crypto is resistant to Chlorine. Boiling is best.
*   **Treatment:** Metronidazole (Fish Zole).

### 5.3 Viral (Hepatitis A, Rotavirus)
*   **Symptoms:** Jaundice (Hep A), fever, vomiting.
*   **Filter:** Too small for most ceramic filters. Boiling or Chlorine required.

## 6. Rationing Protocols

### 6.1 The Hierarchy of Use
1.  **Drinking:** 1 Gallon/day (Activity dependent).
2.  **Medical/Hygiene:** Wound cleaning, hand washing.

## 7. Projects & Builds

### 7.1 Project: The Bio-Sand Filter (Bucket Version)
**Goal:** Filter turbidity and pathogens from pond/river water using biology, not just physics.
**Principle:** A "Schmutzdecke" (biolayer) forms on top of the sand, eating pathogens.
**Materials:**
1.  **Container:** 5 Gallon food-grade bucket (or 55 gal drum for home).
2.  **Piping:** 1/2" PVC pipe, end cap, bulkhead fitting (or simple hole + silicone).
3.  **Filtration Media:**
    *   **Gravel:** 2 inches (Bottom layer).
    *   **Coarse Sand:** 2 inches (Middle).
    *   **Fine Sand:** 10+ inches (Top layer - The worker).
    *   **Diffuser Plate:** A plastic lid with holes drilled in it.

**Step-by-Step Build:**
1.  **Prep the Bucket:** Drill a hole near the bottom for the outlet tube. The outlet tube must rise up the side of the bucket and exit *above* the sand level (this ensures the sand never dries out).
2.  **Wash the Sand:** **CRITICAL.** Wash your sand in a bucket with water until the run-off is CLEAR. If you don't, your filter will clog or taste like mud.
3.  **Layering:**
    *   Install bottom drainage pipe (drilled with many small holes, wrapped in cloth).
    *   Add **Gravel** (covers pipe).
    *   Add **Coarse Sand**.
    *   Add **Fine Sand** (Fill to 2 inches below the outlet level).
4.  **The Diffuser:** Place the drilled plastic lid on top of the sand. This prevents pouring water from disturbing the sand bed.
5.  **Acclimation (The Wait):** Pour dirty water through. Do NOT drink it yet. It takes **2-3 weeks** for the biolayer (good bacteria) to form on top.
6.  **Usage:** Pour water in. Collect clear water out. **Purify** (Boil/Bleach) this water until you are 100% sure the biolayer is mature.

### 7.2 Project: First-Flush Rainwater Diverter
**Goal:** Automatically reject the first 5 gallons of rain (containing bird poop/roof dust) so only clean water enters your tank.
**Materials:** 3" or 4" PVC Pipe, T-Junction, Screw cap, Tennis Ball (or floating ball), Reducer.

**Build Logic:**
1.  **The Drop:** Install a T-Junction on your downspout. The "Main" path goes down into a capped vertical pipe (The Chamber). The "Branch" path goes to your barrel.
2.  **The Mechanism:**
    *   Insert a floating ball (Tennis ball) inside the vertical chamber.
    *   Ensure there is a restriction (reducer) at the top of the chamber so the ball seals the hole but doesn't pass through.
3.  **Operation:**
    *   Rain starts. Dirty water fills the vertical chamber.
    *   Ball floats up.
    *   Chamber gets full -> Ball seals the top.
    *   Clean water is forced sideways into the branch (to your barrel).
4.  **Reset:** Drill a tiny "weep hole" in the bottom screw cap. This allows the dirty water to slowly stickle out between rains, resetting the system.

### 7.3 Skill: Making Activated Charcoal
**Goal:** Create a chemical filter to remove toxins, pesticides, and bad tastes.
**Process:**
1.  **Carbonize:** Burn loose hardwood (Willow/Oak) in a metal tin with a small hole (to vent gas but limit oxygen). You want *Charcoal*, not *Ash*.
    *   *Alternative:* Take coals from a very hot fire and quench them instantly in water.
2.  **Crush:** Pulverize the charcoal into small granules (rice size). Dust is okay but clogs filters.
3.  **Activate (Chemical):** Soak in a solution of Calcium Chloride (Road salt alternative) or Lemon Juice for 24 hours. This eats away internal structure, increasing surface area.
4.  **Wash & Dry:** Rinse thoroughly with clean water. Bake in an oven/fire (250°F) to dry.
5.  **Usage:** Place in a cloth sock. Water passes through *after* the sand filter.
