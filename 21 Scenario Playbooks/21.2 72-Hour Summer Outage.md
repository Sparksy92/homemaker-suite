# 21.2 Scenario: 72-Hour Summer Outage (Summer)

**Scenario:** Grid failure during a heatwave. Temperatures > 95°F (35°C). High humidity.
**Duration:** 72 Hours (3 Days).
**Goal:** Prevent heatstroke, maintain hydration, and manage food spoilage without refrigeration.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Hour 0-1)

### 1.1 Heat Management (Passive Cooling)
*   **Action:** Close all windows, blinds, and curtains immediately to trap cool air inside.
*   **Strategy:** "Button up" during the day (10 AM - 7 PM). Open windows at night (if temp drops) to flush heat.
*   **Basement:** If you have one, move sleeping arrangements there. It is the coolest part of the house.

### 1.2 Water Security
*   **Action:** Fill bathtub and all available containers.
*   **Why:** Municipal pumps may fail. In heat, you need 2 Gallons/Person/Day (Drinking + Cooling).

### 1.3 Food Triage
*   **Fridge:** Will warm up fast in summer heat.
*   **Action:**
    1.  Eat ice cream/cold perishables immediately (Team morale boost).
    2.  Cook meat that evening (BBQ outside).
    3.  Do NOT open fridge unnecessarily.

---

## 2. Daily Protocol

### Day 1: The Pot Roast
*   **08:00:** Breakfast. Perishables (Milk/Cereal).
*   **11:00:** Heat rises. Wet t-shirts/towels around necks. Evaporative cooling is powerful.
*   **13:00:** Siesta. No heavy labor during peak heat. Read, rest.
*   **17:00:** Dinner. The "Feast". Cook the thawing meat from the freezer on the grill. Invite neighbors if you have too much (builds alliance).
*   **20:00:** Sunset. Open windows if outside temp < inside temp. Set up fans (battery powered) in windows to pull cool air in.

### Day 2: Hydration Discipline
*   **08:00:** Check urine color. Dark = Dehydrated. Drink water immediately.
*   **10:00:** Food Shift. Switch to non-perishables (Canned fruit is great for hydration/sugar).
*   **14:00:** Critical Heat Stress Check.
    *   *Symptoms:* Headache, nausea, stop sweating (Danger!).
    *   *Treat:* Sponge bath, feet in bucket of water.
*   **19:00:** Dinner. Salty snacks/soup to replace electrolytes lost to sweat.

### Day 3: Hygiene & Waste
*   **Problem:** Heat makes garbage smell and rot fast. Flies appear.
*   **Action:** Double bag all food waste. Store OUTSIDE away from house.
*   **Hygiene:** Sweat = Rashes. Wash key areas (pits/groin) daily with cloth and small amount of soapy water.
*   **Restore:** When power returns:
    1.  Check Fridge Temp. If > 40°F for > 2 hours -> Throw away milk/meat/leftovers. "When in doubt, throw it out."
    2.  Start A/C slowly (monitor for surge).

---

## 3. Systems Checklist

### 💧 Water
*   **Goal:** 1 Gallon for drinking, 1 Gallon for cooling (wet towels).
*   **Electrolytes:** Add pinch of salt + sugar to water if sweating heavily.

### 🔥 Energy (Cooling)
*   **Fans:** Battery fans are life-savers.
*   **Tech:** Solar chargers work GREAT in summer. Charge phones/fans during day.

### 🩺 Health & Safety
*   **Heat Stroke:** Life threatening. Cool the patient aggressively (wet sheets, fanning).
*   **Mosquitoes:** If windows are open without screens, use nets or repellent. (See `Module: Pests`).

### 🧠 Morale
*   **Irritability:** Heat makes people angry. Recognize this. "Halt" triggers (Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired).
*   **Cool Treat:** If you have a cooler with ice, save a cold drink for Day 2 afternoon.

---

## 4. Lessons Learned
*   *Too hot to sleep?* -> **Buy battery powered fans.**
*   *Lost food?* -> **Learn to pressure can meat (shelf stable).**
*   *Dehydrated?* -> **Stock oral rehydration salts.**
