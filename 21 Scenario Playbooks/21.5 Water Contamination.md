# 21.5 Scenario: Water Contamination

**Scenario:** "Boil Order" or "Do Not Drink" order issued due to chemical spill, algae bloom, or pipe burst.
**Duration:** Indefinite (Until "All Clear" signal).
**Goal:** Prevent sickness (Giardia/Cryptosporidium/Chemicals). Ration safe water.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Hour 0)

### 1.1 Stop the Flow
*   **Action:** Turn OFF the main water valve to the house immediately.
*   **Why:** Contaminated water entering your pipes ("Head") will take weeks to flush out later. Keep your internal pipes clean.
*   **Appliances:** Turn off Ice Makers, Dishwashers, Washing Machines immediately.

### 1.2 Inventory Check
*   **Audit:** Check the Hot Water Heater tank. (It has 40-50 Gallons of *clean* water stored inside).
*   **Audit:** Check toilet tanks (Not bowls). If clean, bag it.
*   **Audit:** Count bottled water.

### 1.3 Classification
*   **Is it Biological?** (E. Coli / Sewage) -> **Boiling WORKS.**
*   **Is it Chemical?** (Oil Spill / Industrial Runoff) -> **Boiling FAILS.** (It concentrates chemicals). **Carbon Filter MUST be used.**

---

## 2. Daily Protocol

### Hydration Rule
*   **Requirement:** 1 Gallon/Person/Day (Drinking + Hygiene).
*   **Source:** Use bottled water for drinking ONLY.
*   **Hygiene:** Use "Gray Water" (Rain barrel / Creek) for flushing toilets. Do NOT use drinking water for toilets.

### Treatment Processing (The "Clean Line")
1.  **Stage 1: Pre-Filter.** Pour dirty water through T-shirt or coffee filter to remove big chunks/mud.
2.  **Stage 2: Disinfect.**
    *   *Boil:* Rolling boil for 1 minute. (Kills biologicals).
    *   *Bleach:* 8 drops regular bleach per gallon. Shake. Wait 30 mins. (Smell chlorine? Good. No smell? Add 8 more drops).
3.  **Stage 3: Chemical (If Suspected).**
    *   Pass through Activated Charcoal filter (Brita/Berkey). *Note: Standard filters may not remove heavy metals.*

### Hygiene Protocol
*   **Bathing:** Sponge bath only. Use 1 cup of water + soap.
*   **Teeth:** Use bottled water. Do NOT use tap water.
*   **Cooking:** Boil pasta in treated water. Use minimal water recipes (Stir fry > Soup).

---

## 3. Systems Checklist

### 💧 Water Storage
*   **The Heater Hack:**
    1.  Turn off heater power/gas.
    2.  Connect hose to drain valve at bottom.
    3.  Open pressure relief valve at top (to let air in).
    4.  Drain clean water into buckets.
*   **Rain:** Capture rain from gutters. (Filter -> Boil -> Drink).

### 🧼 Sanitation
*   **Dishes:** Use paper plates. Do not waste water washing dishes.
*   **Hands:** Hand sanitizer is superior to water washing in this scenario.

### 🩺 Health Watch
*   **Symptoms:** Diarrhea, vomiting, cramps.
*   **Response:** Isolate patient. "Oral Rehydration Salts" (Salt+Sugar+Water) immediately. Dehydration kills faster than the bug.

---

## 4. Lessons Learned
*   *Did you have only 1 case of water?* -> **Store 2 weeks worth (14 Gallons/Person).**
*   *Filter clogging?* -> **Pre-filters (T-shirts) extend filter life by 10x.**
*   *Chemical fear?* -> **Buy a filter explicitly rated for VOCs/Heavy Metals.**
