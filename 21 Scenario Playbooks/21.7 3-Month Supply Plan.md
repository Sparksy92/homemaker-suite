# 21.7 Scenario: 3-Month Supply Plan

**Scenario:** "Normal Life" -> "Prepared Life".
**Duration:** Strategic Roadmap (Not an emergency).
**Goal:** Build a 90-day buffer of Food, Water, and Hygiene supplies without going broke or looking crazy.

---

## 1. The Strategy: "Copy Canning"

### 1.1 Do NOT Buy "Survival Food"
*   **Error:** Buying $5,000 of freeze-dried food you will never eat.
*   **Strategy:** "Eat what you store, store what you eat."
*   **Method:**
    *   If you eat 2 jars of spaghetti sauce per week...
    *   Buy 4 jars this week. Eat 2. Shelf 2.
    *   Repeat for 10 weeks.
    *   **Result:** You now have 20 jars (10 weeks worth) without noticing the cost.

### 1.2 The "Deep Pantry" Zoning
*   **Zone 1: The Working Pantry (Kitchen).** Open boxes, daily use.
*   **Zone 2: The Bulk Storage (Closet/Basement).** Unopened cases.
*   **Zone 3: The Long Term (Under Bed/Attic).** Rice buckets / Mylar bags (10-30 year shelf life).

---

## 2. The 90-Day Roadmap

### Month 1: Water & Hygiene (The Foundation)
*   **Week 1:** Buy 2 cases of water ($10).
*   **Week 2:** Buy 1 extra pack of Toilet Paper ($15).
*   **Week 3:** Buy 1 extra Toothpaste/Soap ($10).
*   **Week 4:** *Action:* Fill 5 empty soda bottles with tap water (free). Label date. Store in dark closet.

### Month 2: Calories (The Bulk)
*   **Week 5:** Buy 20lbs Rice ($10). Seal in Mylar or food bucket.
*   **Week 6:** Buy 10lbs Dry Beans ($10).
*   **Week 7:** Buy 5lbs Oats ($8).
*   **Week 8:** Buy "Comforts". 5lbs Sugar, 5lbs Salt, 1 Gallon Oil. ($15).
*   **Goal:** You now have ~50,000 calories stored for < $50. This is life insurance.

### Month 3: Proteins & Variety (The Flavor)
*   **Week 9:** Canned Meat (Tuna/Chicken/Spam). Buy 5 cans/week.
*   **Week 10:** Canned Fruits/Veggies. Buy 5 cans/week.
*   **Week 11:** Spices. Salt, Pepper, Garlic Powder, Soy Sauce.
*   **Week 12:** Vitamins. A simple Multivitamin covers nutritional gaps in a rice/bean diet.

---

## 3. Storage Logistics

### ❄️ Temperature
*   **Enemy:** Heat degrades food. Store in cool, dark place (Basement > Closet > Garage).
*   **Pests:** Mice love rice.
    *   *Solution:* Store grains in hard plastic buckets or glass jars. Cardboard boxes are not mouse-proof.

### 🔄 Rotation
*   **Rule:** FIFO (First In, First Out).
*   **Action:** When you buy new pasta, put it behind the old pasta. Grab from the front.

---

## 4. The "Gap" Check
(Perform this audit at Day 90)

*   [ ] **Can I cook it?** (Do you have a camp stove if gas/electric fails?)
*   [ ] **Can I open it?** (Do you have a manual can opener? Two of them?)
*   [ ] **Can I eat it?** (Do you actually like Spam? If not, donate it and buy Tuna).

---

## 5. Lessons Learned
*   *Did flour get weevils?* -> **Freeze flour for 48 hrs before storing to kill eggs.**
*   *Did oil go rancid?* -> **Rotate oil every 12 months. Buy smaller bottles.**
*   *Did you forget pets?* -> **Apply same logic to Dog Food.**
