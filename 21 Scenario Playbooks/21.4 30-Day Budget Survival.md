# Survival Playbook: 30-Day Low-Budget Survival After Job Loss

**Scenario:** Sudden loss of income. Zero discretionary spending allowed. Stocks must last 30 days.
**Duration:** 30 Days.
**Goal:** Survive without spending cash. Eat nutritious meals. Maintain morale to find new work.

---

## 1. Immediate Actions (Day 0)

### 1.1 The Friendly Audit
*   **Action:** Pull EVERYTHING out of the pantry, freezer, and back of shelves.
*   **List:** Write down every single item.
*   **Calculate:**
    *   *Inventory:* 10 cans of beans, 5 lbs rice, 2 lbs frozen chicken, 1 jar peanut butter.
    *   *Calories:* Map total calories. (e.g., 50,000 total).
    *   *Math:* 50,000 / 30 Days = 1,660 Calories/Day. (Survival baseline met).

### 1.2 The "No-Spend" Decree
*   **Rule:** Lock the credit cards. Delete saved cards from Amazon/Uber Eats.
*   **Substitution:**
    *   *Coffee:* Drink what you have, then switch to water/tea.
    *   *Entertainment:* Cancel Netflix/Spotify (save $30). Use Library.

### 1.3 Utilities Triage
*   **Heat/Cooling:** Adjust thermostat by 5 degrees to lower bill immediately.
*   **Water:** Shorter showers.

---

## 2. Weekly Protocol

### Week 1: The Fresh Food Sprint
*   **Strategy:** Eat perishable foods first.
*   **Menu:** Big salads, fresh fruit, milk-based recipes.
*   **Action:** Process anything about to spoil (freeze bananas for smoothies).

### Week 2: The Freezer Dive
*   **Strategy:** Tap into the frozen assets.
*   **Menu:** Mystery Casseroles. Combine that random bag of frozen peas with the half-bag of tater tots.
*   **Foraging (Urban):** Dandelion greens are free, nutritious, and everywhere. (Wash well).

### Week 3: The Pantry Slog (Rice & Beans)
*   **Strategy:** Make boring food taste good.
*   **Spice Module:** Use those soy sauce packets from the drawer. Use the random spices in the rack.
*   **Menu:**
    *   *Breakfast:* Oatmeal (water based).
    *   *Lunch:* Leftover Rice fried with onion.
    *   *Dinner:* Lentil soup.

### Week 4: The Stretch
*   **Strategy:** 1/2 Rations if needed. Intermittent Fasting (Skip breakfast) to make dinner larger.
*   **Community:** Visit a food pantry if you are truly out. There is no shame in survival.

---

## 3. Systems Checklist

### 🥬 Food
*   **Wildcrafting:** Check `Module 12`. Are there crabapples nearby? Walnuts?
*   **Gardening:** Plant fast crops (Radishes: 25 days) on Day 1. By Day 30, you have fresh food.

### 💵 Finance
*   **Asset Liquidation:** Sell unused electronics/clothes on Facebook Marketplace. Cash = Security.
*   **Gig Work:** Immediate cash gigs (Day labor, cleaning) > Waiting for perfect job.

### 🧠 Mental Health
*   **Routine:** Job hunting is a 9-5 job. Keep the routine.
*   **Exercise:** Run/Walk outside. It's free anti-depressant.

---

## 4. Lessons Learned
*   *Did you panic?* -> **Build a 3-month emergency fund next time.**
*   *Too much rice?* -> **Diversify pantry with canned meats/fruits.**
*   *Bored eating?* -> **Learn to cook with spices.**
