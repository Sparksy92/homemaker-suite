# 0.3 Tools & Equipment: The Essential Kit

## The Philosophy of Gear
Buy quality, buy once. Avoid "unitaskers" (tools that do only one specific job, like a strawberry huller) in favor of versatile, durable equipment. A well-equipped kitchen needs surprisingly few items if they are the *right* items.

## Tier 1: The Absolute Essentials
*If you have nothing else, start here. These tools allow you to cook 95% of meals.*

### Cutlery
*   **Chef’s Knife (8-inch):** The workhorse. For chopping, slicing, and dicing.
*   **Paring Knife (3-4 inch):** For peeling and delicate work.
*   **Serrated Bread Knife:** For bread, tomatoes, and leveling cakes.
*   **Honing Steel:** To keep edges aligned between sharpenings.

### Cookware
*   **Cast Iron Skillet (10-12 inch):** Natural non-stick, goes from stovetop to oven to campfire. Indestructible.
*   **Large Stock Pot (8-12 quart):** For soups, stocks, boiling pasta, and batch cooking.
*   **Saucepan (2-4 quart):** For grains, sauces, and reheating.
*   **Rimmed Baking Sheets (Half-Sheet Pan):** For roasting, baking, and freezing flat items.

### Prep & Utensils
*   **Cutting Board (Large):** Wood or heavy plastic. Avoid glass (it ruins knives).
*   **Mixing Bowls (Set of 3):** Stainless steel or glass.
*   **Measuring Cups & Spoons:** Dry and liquid measures.
*   **Wooden Spoon:** Flat-edged is best for scraping the bottom of pans.
*   **Tongs:** Metal with locking mechanism.
*   **Vegetable Peeler:** Y-shaped peelers are often faster and more ergonomic.

## Tier 2: The Efficiency Boosters
*Items that save significant time and labor.*

*   **Food Processor:** For bulk chopping, shredding cheese, making dough, and purees.
*   **Immersion Blender:** Puree soups directly in the pot.
*   **Dutch Oven (Enameled Cast Iron):** The king of slow cooking, braising, and bread baking.
*   **Instant Read Thermometer:** Essential for food safety and perfect meat cookery.
*   **Kitchen Scale:** For baking accuracy and portioning.

## Tier 3: The Preservation & Production Kit
*Required for "Series 4: Preservation" and "Series 5: Gardening" tasks.*

*   **Water Bath Canner:** For high-acid foods (fruits, pickles, jams).
*   **Pressure Canner:** Essential for low-acid foods (vegetables, meats, soups). *Note: A pressure cooker is NOT a pressure canner.*
*   **Mason Jars & Lids:** Quarts and pints. Wide mouth is easier to fill.
*   **Dehydrator:** For drying herbs, fruits, and jerky.
*   **Vacuum Sealer:** Extends freezer life dramatically; strictly necessary for long-term frozen storage.

## Care & Maintenance
*   **Cast Iron:** Never soak. Scrub with salt/chainmail. Dry immediately and oil.
*   **Knives:** Hand wash only. Sharpen professionally once a year; hone weekly.
*   **Wood:** Oil boards and spoons with food-grade mineral oil monthly to prevent cracking.
