## 2. Gap Analysis

| Domain | What Exists | What is Missing | Next Wave Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gardening & Soil** | Soil pH basics, compost outlines. | Spacing rules, companion planting matrices, crop profile reference cards, indoors vs direct sow guides, seasonal planting timelines. | **Critical High** |
| **Water Systems** | Bio-sand filter details, general purification. | Rainwater catchment sizing guides, cistern/barrel comparisons, gravity flow layouts, irrigation overview. | **Critical High** |
| **Energy & Electricity** | Solar component outlines. | Watts/Amps/Volts load calculators, solar system schematics, wiring safety, battery chemistry comparisons. | **High** |
| **Shelter & Construction** | General orientation basics. | Raised bed framing blueprints, passive solar orientation calculations, foundation and insulation comparison tables. | **Medium** |
| **Food Preservation** | Canning and curing outlines. | Preservation method comparison tables, safety timelines, storage guidelines. | **Medium** |
| **Wildlife & Foraging** | Species records, mushroom rules. | Expanded local forage list, trapping/processing guides. | **Low** |
| **Medical & Sanitation** | Herbal basics. | Emergency triage workflows, hygiene/waste setups. | **Low** |

## 3. Priority Ladder (Implementation Wave)
1. **Gardening & Soil**: Build crop cards, sowing/harvest timelines, raised bed layouts, and soil amendment comparisons.
2. **Water Systems**: Build system flow maps, catchment sizing calculators, filtration method comparisons.
3. **Energy & Electricity**: Build interactive load planning calculators, battery chemistry tables, solar safety callouts.
4. **Shelter & Construction**: Build framing blueprints and construction step-by-step guides.
