## Power Outage Checklist
1.  **Water:** Do you have water stored? (Fill tub if you suspect long outage).
2.  **Heat:** Close doors to unused rooms. Block drafts.
3.  **Food:**
    *   Do NOT open the fridge/freezer unless absolutely necessary.
    *   Fridge stays cold: 4 hours.
    *   Freezer stays frozen: 48 hours (if full).
    *   *First:* Eat perishable dairy/produce.
    *   *Second:* Eat freezer items efficiently.
    *   *Third:* Switch to pantry/canned goods.

## Water Purification
If water is suspect, boil it.
1.  **Rolling Boil:** 1 minute (3 minutes at high altitude).
2.  **Bleach (Unscented Only):**
    *   Clear water: 8 drops (1/8 tsp) per gallon.
    *   Cloudy water: 16 drops (1/4 tsp) per gallon.
    *   *Wait:* Let stand 30 minutes. It should smell slightly of chlorine.

## Measurements
*   3 tsp = 1 tbsp
*   4 tbsp = 1/4 cup
*   16 tbsp = 1 cup (8 oz)
*   2 cups = 1 pint
*   2 pints = 1 quart
*   4 quarts = 1 gallon (128 oz)
