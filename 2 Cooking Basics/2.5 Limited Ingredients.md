# 2.5 Limited Ingredients: Cooking from the Cupboard

## The "Iron Chef" Mindset
When the fridge is empty and the budget is zero, you must cook from the "Deep Larder." This is where skills > ingredients.

## The "Grain + Green + Bean" Formula
You can live indefinitely on this trio.
1.  **Grain:** Rice, Pasta, Quinoa, Toast, Polenta.
2.  **Green:** Frozen spinach, canned green beans, foraged wild greens, kale.
3.  **Bean (Protein):** Lentils, chickpeas, black beans, fried egg, tuna.
*   *Sauce:* Oil & Vinegar, Soy Sauce, Hot Sauce, or Salsa.

## Stone Soup Strategies
How to make something from nothing.

### 1. The Frittata / Quiche
*   **Base:** Eggs + Milk/Water.
*   **Fill:** Literally any leftover veggie (roasted or raw), cheese ends, stale bread chunks, deli meat scraps.
*   **Cook:** Stovetop until set, broiler to finish.

### 2. Fried Rice
*   **Base:** Cold leftover rice (essential).
*   **Fill:** Frozen peas/carrots, onion, egg, spam/bacon bits.
*   **Flavor:** Soy sauce, oil, garlic power.

### 3. Potato Hash
*   **Base:** Potatoes (diced small).
*   **Fill:** Onions, peppers, leftover meat.
*   **Key:** Fry until crispy. Top with an egg.

### 4. Pasta "Aglio e Olio" (Garlic & Oil)
*   **Base:** Spaghetti.
*   **Sauce:** Olive oil gently heated with sliced garlic and red pepper flakes.
*   **Finish:** Parsley (dried or fresh). That's it. Gourmet flavor for $0.50.

### 5. Savory Oatmeal
*   Oats aren't just for breakfast. Cook them in broth instead of water.
*   Top with sautéed onions, an egg, and hot sauce. Texture is like risotto.

## Substitution logic
Don't have the specific vegetable? Swap by texture/cook time.
*   **Hard Veg:** Carrots $\leftrightarrow$ Potatoes $\leftrightarrow$ Turnips $\leftrightarrow$ Sweet Potato.
*   **Soft Veg:** Zucchini $\leftrightarrow$ Yellow Squash $\leftrightarrow$ Eggplant.
*   **Leafy:** Spinach $\leftrightarrow$ Kale $\leftrightarrow$ Chard $\leftrightarrow$ Beet Greens.
*   **Aromatic:** Onion $\leftrightarrow$ Leek $\leftrightarrow$ Shallot $\leftrightarrow$ Scallion (white part).
