# 15.3 Security: The Quiet Professional

**Concept:** Security is not about having a bunker. It is about **Deterrence** and **Detection**.

## 1. Defensive Landscaping
Use nature to direct traffic. You don't need barbed wire if you have biology.

### The "Living Wall"
Plant these thorny barriers under windows and along your perimeter.
*   **Osage Orange (Hedge Apple):** historically used before barbed wire. "Horse high, bull strong, pig tight."
*   **Blackberry / Raspberry:** Fast-growing, thorny tangles. Bonus: Food source.
*   **Hawthorn:** Long, needle-like thorns.

### The Funnel Effect
Don't build a wall around everything. Leave an "obvious" opening.
*   **Goal:** Force intruders into a specific path (a "choke point") where *you* can see them, rather than letting them wander hidden edges.

---

## 2. Situational Awareness (Discipline)
Your behavior gives you away.

### Light Discipline
*   **Blackout Curtains:** Heavy wool blankets or multiple layers of fabric over windows. Test it: Go outside at night and look at your house. If you see a glow, you failed.
*   **Red Light:** Use red filters on flashlights. It preserves natural night vision and doesn't travel as far as white light.

### Noise Discipline
*   **Quiet Hours:** Establish times when work stops. Sound travels further at night (colder, denser air).
*   **Gear Check:** Tape down metal buckles on backpacks. Silence is survival.

---

## 3. The Layered Defense
1.  **Outer Layer:** Thorny hedges (deterrent).
2.  **Middle Layer:** Crunchy gravel paths (detection by sound).
3.  **Inner Layer:** Reinforced doors (3-inch screws in strike plates) and blackout protection.
