# 15.4 Advanced Security: Systems & Protocols

**Concept:** Advanced security isn't about bigger guns. It’s about **early warning** and **information superiority**.

## 1. Electronic Surveillance (Force Multipliers)
You can't stay awake 24/7. Let technology watch for you.

### Trail Cameras
*   **Placement:** Choke points (gates, trail heads) and "dead space" (areas you can't see from windows).
*   **Type:** No-glow IR (infrared) so they aren't spotted at night. Cell-linked versions send photos to your phone immediately.
*   **Protocol:** Check/swap SD cards weekly. Map where they are so you don't lose them.

### Motion Alerts
*   **Driveway Alarms:** Simple magnetic or PIR sensors. Put the receiver in the main living area.
*   **Perimeter Trips:** Fishing line attached to a simple battery-operated door alarm is a cheap, disposable perimeter warning.

---

## 2. Hardening the Home
Buying time for you to react.

### Door Reinforcement
*   **Strike Plates:** Replace standard 1-inch screws with **3-inch screws** that bite into the structural stud, not just the door frame.
*   **Door Jamb Armor:** Metal sleeves that wrap around the door edge and jamb preventing kick-ins.

### Window Security
*   **Security Film:** 8-12 mil film prevents glass from shattering instantly. A rock bounces off; a sledgehammer takes multiple hits.
*   **Dowel Rods:** Simple wood dowels in the track of sliding windows/doors prevent them from being forced open.

---

## 3. OPSEC (Operational Security)
Your biggest vulnerability is your mouth and your digital footprint.

*   **Need to Know:** Does the delivery driver need to know you have a year's supply of food? No.
*   **Digital Habits:** Don't post photos of your prep pantry on social media with geotags.
*   **Pattern Interruption:** Varing your daily routine (departure times, routes) makes you harder to predict/target.
