# 8.1 Nutrition 101: Fueling the Machine

## The Macroutrients

### Protein (Building Blocks)
*   **Job:** Repair tissue, immune function, satiety.
*   **Sources:** Meat, Eggs, Beans, Lentils, Greek Yogurt.
*   **Goal:** Aim for a palm-sized portion at every meal.

### Carbohydrates (Energy)
*   **Job:** Quick fuel for brain and muscles.
*   **Simple (Fast):** Sugar, White bread, Fruit. (Spikes insulin).
*   **Complex (Slow):** Oats, Beans, Vegetables, Whole grains. (Sustained energy).
*   **Goal:** Focus on complex carbs. Pair simple carbs with fiber/protein to slow absorption.

### Fats (Hormones & Brain)
*   **Job:** Nutrient absorption (Vit A,D,E,K), hormone production.
*   **Sources:** Butter, Olive Oil, Avocado, Nuts, Animal fat.
*   **Goal:** Don't fear fat. Fear processed trans-fats (hydrogenated oils).

## Micronutrients (The Spark Plugs)
*   **Iron:** Red meat, Cast iron cooking, Spinach.
*   **Vitamin D:** Sunshine, Eggs, Fish.
*   **Vitamin C:** Citrus, Peppers, Cabbage. (Helps absorb Iron).

## Hydration
*   **Sign:** Urine should be pale yellow. Clear = too much. Dark = Dehydrated.
*   **Electrolytes:** Water isn't enough if you are sweating. Add a pinch of salt and squeeze of lemon.

## The "Plate Method"
No counting calories.
*   **1/2 Plate:** Non-starchy vegetables (Greens, Broccoli, Peppers).
*   **1/4 Plate:** Protein.
*   **1/4 Plate:** Starch (Potatoes, Rice, Bread).
*   **Fat:** Thumb-sized portion added.
