## Risk Considerations

### Medical/Legal Risks:
- **Herbal medicine and natural antibiotics** sections need disclaimers
- Consider consulting with an attorney about liability
- Always emphasize when to seek professional medical care
- Make poisonous plant warnings VERY prominent

### Content Quality Risks:
- Foraging information must be 100% accurate - errors could be dangerous
- Consider expert review for plant identification content
- Multiple source verification for all safety-critical information

### Technical Risks:
- Large content expansion may impact performance
- Need robust CMS or data structure for managing 100+ plant species
- Image storage costs may increase significantly
