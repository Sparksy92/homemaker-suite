# 6.2 Basic Repairs: Fixing Before Replacing

## The Toolkit (Series 0.3 Update)
Add these to your drawer:
*   Multi-bit Screwdriver.
*   Hammer.
*   Tape Measure.
*   Adjustable Wrench.
*   Pliers (Needle nose).
*   WD-40 (Makes stuck things move).
*   Duct Tape (Makes moving things stay).

## Unclogging Drains
*Before calling a plumber ($300).*
1.  **The Zip-It:** Plastic barbed stick ($2). Shove down drain, pull up hair.
2.  **The Volcano:** Cup of Baking Soda down drain. Cup of Vinegar. Cover. Wait 15 mins. Kettle of Boiling Water.
3.  **The Plunge:** Fill sink partially. Cover overflow hole with rag. Plunge vigorously.
4.  **The Trap:** Put bucket under sink pipe. Unscrew the U-bend (P-trap). Clean out the gunk manually.

## Drywall Anchors
*Never hang heavy things on drywall without a stud or anchor.*
*   **Stud Finder:** Use magnetic one to find nails in studs.
*   **Toggle Bolts:** Best for heavy loads on hollow walls.
*   **Plastic Plugs:** Okay for light pictures.
*   **Patching:** Spackle, putty knife, sand, paint.

## Running Toilet
*It's usually the flapper.*
*   Open tank. If water is running into the overflow tube, the float is set too high. Adjust screw.
*   If water is leaking into bowl (ghost flush), the rubber flapper is old. Buy new one ($5). Turn off water, swap, turn on.

## Furniture Scratches
*   **Walnut Trick:** Rub a raw walnut meat over scratches in dark wood. The oils hide the damage.
*   **Marker:** specialized furniture markers.

## Safety
*   **Electricity:** Turn off the BREAKER, not just the switch. Test with non-contact voltage tester.
*   **Water:** Know where your Main Shutoff Valve is. Tag it.
